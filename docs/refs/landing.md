## The real place
In the game's frame: x east, z south, the bedroom centred on the origin (south wall at z = 2.5, the hall's south end
at z = 4.9). Sizes are from the photos (a door is 0.8-0.9 m, the windows about 0.9 m a sash) and the sketch's
proportions; ±0.3 m.

**The landing.** About 4.8 m east-west by 4.7 m north-south: x -2.4 to 2.4, z 4.9 to 9.6. Flat ceiling 2.7 m, one
white drum light in the middle. Walls warm tan (about `#d8c4ae` lit; the photo reads `#bead99` in shade), ceiling
and trim white, carpet pale beige (about `#d4ccc2`), white baseboards 0.12 m.
- **North wall** (z 4.9): the hall's south-end opening, x -0.8 to 0.8, the door to it standing open. A bifold closet at
  the east end, about x 1.0 to 2.3, 0.6 m deep (into z 4.3-4.9, clear of the game's big closet, which ends at z 4.3).
- **East wall** (x 2.4): a pair of double-hung windows, together about 1.75 m wide, sill 0.85 m, 1.35 m tall, white
  casing, centred about z 6.6 (in line with the hallway, which looks straight down at them). Trees outside, as through
  the bedroom window.
- **South-east corner: the stairs.** An open stairwell 1.0 m wide, x 1.4 to 2.4, going south from the south wall line
  along the east wall: about four steps down (0.19 m each) to a square platform step 1.0 x 1.0 m about 0.75 m below
  the landing, then the flight turns west and goes on down out of sight (to the kitchen). A black handrail on the east
  wall. No door.
- **South wall** (z 9.6): west of the stairwell, a recess about 2.7 m wide (x -1.4 to 1.3) and 0.75 m deep (to z 10.35);
  the wall between the recess and the stairwell is the recess's east side. A short jut of wall (about 0.5 m) closes
  the recess on the west.
- **The desk build** fills the recess, all one white built-in piece: a drawer pedestal at the east end, the first
  computer spot, a second pedestal, the second computer spot, then at the west end the corner unit cut at 45°, open
  shelves below. Desk top 0.76 m, 0.6 m deep. **The TV platform is the top of that corner unit**, raised "a foot or two"
  above the desk (top about 1.1-1.3 m), angled at 45° to face north-east into the room, with Michael's TV on it. Two
  black office chairs. **Desk tops empty** (Michael). The wall shelf in the photo can be left out.
- **West wall** (x -2.4): the opening into the hallway, 1.1 m wide, about z 6.8 to 7.9. No door.
- **The futon and coffee table** (from his note; the photos show a later family's furniture): a black futon, about
  1.9 m wide, and a black coffee table in front of it, facing the TV platform in the south-west. Where exactly is open:
  the futon under the east window, turned 25-30° toward the platform, keeps the walk from the bedroom door to the
  stairs clear (south down the middle, then east along the south wall). Check it in play.

**The hallway to Sean's room.** 1.1 m wide, from the landing's west wall (x -2.4) about 4.5 m west to x -6.9, at
z 6.8-7.9. Ceiling 2.6 m with an attic hatch (about 0.6 x 0.9 m, white trim) and a return-air grille, two recessed
lights. Same tan walls, beige carpet, white baseboards. At the west end, two shut white six-panel doors: Sean's door in
the north wall just short of the end (about x -6.3), and the far-end door across the end wall. Neither opens in the
game (Sean's room is not built); they could take a prompt line later.

**The bedroom (shape only).** Michael's room has a vaulted ceiling: from short walls (about 2.4 m) on the east and west
it rises to a ridge running north-south, about 3.8 m up, with the gable on the north wall. The dormer on the north wall

with a window) and the closet; its ceiling is flat at 2.6 m and its window square-topped. **Furniture and wall colours
stay exactly as the game has them** (the photos' are a later family's).

## The game today (v1.372)
- `buildBedroomScene`: the room 6.0 x 5.0 x 2.6 m; a hall 1.6 m wide running 2.4 m south from the room's south wall,
  with the bathroom on its west side, the hall closet (the workbench, a bifold) on its east, and the big closet off the
  room's south-east corner. The hall's south end (z 4.9) is the "entry door", drawn closed: E there is **Go outside**
  (`front_door`, `openMap()`, v1.88). The **back door** (v1.257, D.40) is on the hall's west wall between the bathroom
  and the entry door: E there is **Go out back (the range)** (`back_door`, `enterRange()`). Beyond the entry door there
  is nothing.
- The player spawns at the hall's south end, back to the entry door (`Scenes.bedroom.spawn`), and comes back there
  from a match and from the range (`enterBedroom`).
- The tutorial ends on the front door: "The front door you're by is the way outside: every match starts out there. The
  map table opens the same map." (`js/72-player.js`, play lane.)
- The crew (D.28) is names on the phone and in briefings; nobody stands in the house.

## The gaps, biggest first
1. There is no landing: the hall's south door leads nowhere and is the way to the map.
2. The back door to the range is in a place the real house had none.
3. The crew has nowhere to be between matches.
4. The bedroom's ceiling is flat and its window square (shape only).

## Suggested order
1. **The landing (D.74 A)** — one maps-lane version: build the landing, the hallway to Sean's room (shut doors) and the
   top of the stairs as above. The hall's south door is drawn open and walked through. Take out the back door and its
   glass (plain wall, casing gone); take out the `front_door` prompt. At the top of the stairs an invisible wall across
   the stairwell, and E there is **Go out back (the range)** → `enterRange()` (the `back_door` interactable, moved);
   coming back from the range puts you at the top of the stairs facing into the landing. Matches start from the map
   table only. The spawn stays where it is (the hall's south end, facing the room). The kids taken into your crew
   sit on the futon or at the desk chairs, or stand by the window and the hall, one more each time someone joins, each
   with a line when you walk up and press E (D.74 A). The tutorial's last card (play lane's `72-player`) needs one
   line changed to point at the map table and the stairs: a small cross-lane edit, said in the PR.
2. **The bedroom's shape** — its own version after the landing: the vaulted ceiling and the arched dormer window,
   nothing else; furniture and colours exactly as they are (Michael, 28 Oct).
