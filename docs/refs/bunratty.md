## The real place
**Layout.** The game's +x runs roughly along the real court (WNW → ESE), the game's +z is roughly real SSW, so the
river is "south" in both. Measured from the road map and the satellite:
- The court leaves Sentinae Chase Dr at a Y with a mown grass island (lantern lamp, street sign) and runs ~120 m ESE,
  then bends to SE for ~90 m to a rounded end. Total ~210 m (the game's lane is ~85 m: the map is about a 0.4 model).
  It is one long bend, concave to the south-west, not an S.
- Road ~7 m wide, two lanes, no centre line, no sidewalk, rolled concrete kerbs; old pale-grey asphalt. The end is a
  bulb or teardrop ~16-18 m across (estimate: the satellite is half hidden by trees).
- Houses on the court: two on the north side (footprints ~23 m and ~29 x 22 m), one large one south of the bend
  (~30 x 23 m), one SW of the end (~24 m), Michael's south-southwest of the end (~27 x 19 m), one east of the end
  (~23 m, grey roof, with its own pool), plus a small outbuilding between those two. A corner house at the Y. Houses
  stand 15-35 m back from the kerb on long drives; lots are ~50-60 m wide.
- Michael's house: ~35-40 m SSW of the court's end, down a drive that runs from the bulb to a concrete motor court at
  the house's side. The neighbour's drive runs beside it. Front faces the court; back faces the river.
- The creek is ~95 m east of the court's end in a ravine running south; the river is ~200-210 m SSE of the end.

**The ground.** From the heightmaps and Michael: the court is on a spur between the river and the creek ravine. The Y
at Sentinae Chase is the high point; the street descends to the bulb (the drive-by frames show a crest and a road in a
cut, lawns banking 1-2 m above the kerb on the uphill side). Past the lots the woods fall hard: south to the river,
east into the creek ravine. Estimates (no elevation reading at this zoom): ~10-15 m of fall along the street, a further
~20-30 m from the backyards to the river, the woods slope at 25-40° past the lawns. Michael's house is a walk-out: the
front door and garage are at the main floor; the back is a full storey (~3.2 m) lower; the lawn below the pool terrace
another ~1.1 m lower and tilting toward the woods; then the drop.

**The buildings.** "Very large and spacious": 650-750 m² houses, two storeys plus a walk-out basement on the downhill
side, ridges ~12-14 m above the front grade. Styles mixed: French-eclectic stucco (cream #d6c9ae, grey-beige #9a958c-ish)
with cream trim, arched windows, steep hip-and-gable roofs; tan-grey brick with twin gables and arched porticos; a cream
house with a two-storey columned portico. Roofs brown or grey asphalt shingle. Side-entry garages, two or three bays.
Black lantern lamps at the fork and along the street; stacked-stone pillars (tan, ~0.6 m square, ~1.5 m, capped, some
with a lantern) at the drive entrances.

**Michael's house, front.** Cream stucco main block ~20 x 14 m, hipped dark-brown roof (~#5e4a40) with cross gables, a
two-storey honey-tan stone entry tower (~4 m wide) with arched window and double wood doors up ~6 steps, a bay window
left of the door, a front gable at the left end with an arched window. Garage wing at the right end (as seen from the
court), ~10 x 8 m, three doors facing sideways onto the motor court (~12 x 12 m of concrete). Big oaks on the front lawn.

**Yards and trees.** Lawns only round the houses (summer #5e752d-#637427), everything else closed forest: tall straight
hardwoods (oaks, tulip poplar) and loblolly pines, trunks clear to 8-12 m, canopy #5c7027; Leyland cypress and
arborvitae screens along drives; crape myrtles (pink), Japanese maples, clipped boxwood and azalea in dark mulch beds;
pampas grass by the walls. Fences: black aluminium pool fences only, no lot-line fences seen.

**Where the fights were (Michael):** in and round his front and back yards, and on the driveway in front of the garage.
