## The real place
- **Layout.** A flat, marshy floodplain field beside the river with a dirt trail into it ("Wizard Hollow" on the sign).
  The field itself is fairly open (scattered big trees); everything off the trail and around the field edge is thick:
  deadfall, logs, shrubs, saplings. Flat to the river, then a drop at the bank. Across the water the ground climbs
  steeply, wooded, with big houses up top seen through the trunks (Michael ties it to the Bunratty / Sentinel on the
  River slope). Which bank the field is on is not clear from the pictures (question below).
- **Ground.** Flat overall but lumpy: hummocks and wet hollows of 0.2-0.5 m over a few metres, roots, logs. Marshy, so
  low spots hold dark mud or standing water. Leaf litter over red-brown Georgia clay; bare dirt landings at the water.
- **The river bank.** A 1.5-2.5 m cut, near vertical, red clay with exposed roots (180824, 180836), brushy at the top
  (0034). Logs and whole fallen trees lie from the bank into the water; driftwood piles 4-6 m across, 1.5-2 m high,
  catch against them.
- **The river.** 70-100 m wide, slow, opaque khaki-green to dark olive, with a sand bar and sunken branches. Not blue.
  Reflections of the far tree wall dominate in calm light.
- **The far side.** A low clay cut at the water (1-3 m), then a slope of roughly 25-35° rising 25-35 m, solid hardwoods
  with straight grey trunks, a few pines; large 2-3 storey houses (light siding, stone, big decks and windows facing the
  river) at the top, half hidden; a small wooden dock on posts at the water.
- **Trees.** Piedmont floodplain hardwoods: oaks, tulip poplar, sweetgum, beech, river birch near water; a minority of
  loblolly pine (one in 0030; none in most frames). Big trunks 0.5-0.9 m, straight, grey-brown (#7e746b), clear of
  branches to 8-12 m; canopy 20-30 m up, closed. Understory: thin saplings 3-6 m (5-10 cm thick), ferns, shrubs.
- **Brush and wood on the ground** (Michael's "a lot more"): fallen logs 0.3-0.5 m thick and 4-10 m long lying flat
  (#706855); grey deadfall branch piles along trail edges ~1 m high (#595d59); stacked cut rounds where the trail was
  cleared; shrubs 1-2.5 m (#4f5d31) thick at the field edge and along the bank.
- **Colours.** Water #928f5c-#aaaa77 (sunlit), #5c7156-#636d54 (shade/reflection); leaf litter #605f4c (summer),
  #6c4e39 (winter); trail #8b8178; grass verge/sunlit patches #69824f-#71825a; bark #7e746b; weathered boards #979b91;
  driftwood #6a5b48, bleached #c9b79b; canopy #4f5839.
- **Fights.** Michael's notes put them in the relatively clear field ("the battlefield"), with the thick brush around it.
