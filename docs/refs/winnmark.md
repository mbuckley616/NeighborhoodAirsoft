## The real place
**Layout.** Winnmark Ct runs from Winnmark Dr (east) about 265 m west to a bulb, nearly straight (east-north-east to
west-south-west) with a slight bend south just before the bulb. About eight or nine houses on the north side, five on
the south. North-side lots are about 34 m wide (house centres 30-36 m apart); each house's back yard runs 30-50 m to
the golf course, behind a black picket fence. The golf pond (about 115 x 70 m) lies north-west of the bulb, about
50-70 m from it; the creek leaves the pond's south end and runs south along the course 60-80 m west of the bulb. The
south-side houses back onto the wooded middle of the teardrop, shared with the back yards of Winnmark Dr's houses
70-100 m away: that is the "shared backyard area", one continuous wood with no fences across it. Lane width about
6.5-7 m with concrete curbs; the bulb is probably 12-15 m in radius (not measurable on the map; typical for the
area).

**The ground.** Along the street, nearly flat: a gentle fall west to the bulb, perhaps 3-5 m over 265 m (1-2 %).
Across the lots, the ground falls away from the street: houses have walk-out lower levels (listing C shows three
storeys at the back, two at the front), so the back grade is about 3 m below the front. Seth's lot is lower again:
its driveway drops steeply off the road (Michael: "very steep") to a house pad perhaps 2-3 m below the road over
15-20 m (12-15 %), and his back yard falls further into the wood and the creek, say another 3-5 m over 40-50 m.
Many yards are terraced: a flat lawn or patio held up at the house by a retaining wall (0.6-1.2 m), sometimes a
second step below, with the black fence along the edge.

**The houses.** Large two-storey houses with walk-out basements. Frontage 18-25 m
including a side-load garage wing, depth 14-18 m. Eaves about 6-6.5 m above the front grade; steep roofs (10/12 to
12/12, 40-45 degrees) with several front gables, a hip on the main block and smaller hips over bays; ridges 11-13 m
up. Two-storey arched entries with columns, fanlights over the door, round or half-round gable vents, tall arched
windows, three-sided and octagonal bays, chimneys (brick, or stucco-clad on stucco houses). Three wall finishes:
rose-tan brick (A), deep red brick with cream quoins and a cream belt course (B), beige stucco with cream quoins (C).
Trim cream to white; shutters near-black or very dark brown/navy. Roofs grey-brown weathered shingle, never red-brown.
Backs: big main-floor decks of brown-stained wood (about 12 x 3.5-4 m, 3 m up on posts, stair to the yard), lower
patios of flagstone under or beside them, pools behind some houses (listing A's drone view shows one).

**Seth's house** (Michael): in the middle of the street; lot sunk below the road with a very steep driveway; a
"really big deck off the main floor"; under the deck a hot tub "almost the size of his pool", so 3-4 m across; a pool
in the yard; the back yard falling into heavy woods with a small creek that runs to the golf course. Most fights
were in that driveway and that back yard.

**Trees and planting.** A near-closed canopy 20-25 m high: loblolly pines with bare trunks to 12-15 m, white and red
oaks, tulip poplars, sweetgums, maples. Under the pines, pine straw and thin lawn; between yards and in the shared
wood, saplings and shrubs. Front yards: foundation boxwoods, azaleas, Japanese maples, crepe myrtles, pine-straw beds
edged with fieldstone. Driveways curve to side garages; walks are brick pavers.
