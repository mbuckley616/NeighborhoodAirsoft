## The real place
**Two hubs, about 650 m apart.** The club is not one building with a pool in front of it, as the game has it:
- **The main clubhouse** stands on higher ground at the north-east end (off Steeplechase Ln, near its north end). It is big:
  about 65 x 40 m with a parking lot of about 250 x 60 m on its north side. Its south side has an upper deck and, below it,
  a round brick terrace, then a putting green, then the course falling away south and west. A pond lies to the left (east) of
  the putting green, below the terrace.
- **The swim and tennis centre** is at the other end, on the peninsula inside the river's bend, where Steeplechase Ln ends
  in a loop with a roundabout. That is where the pools are, and it is the end nearest the river.
- Winnmark Ct (Seth's, the game's Winnmark) is off the course's east edge. The course runs between it and the river: the
  kids' run "across the golf course towards the river" is about 600-900 m, roughly east to west, downhill.

**The ground.** The heightmaps put the peninsula almost at river level (dark, about 265-275 m), and the clubhouse end
about 20-30 m higher. So the course falls gently (1-4 %) from the clubhouse toward the river, with local rolls of 1-3 m
(mounds round the greens, swales along the fairways). The terrace sits about 3-5 m above the putting green. The swim
and tennis centre is graded flat. West of it the fairway is flat floodplain; the river bank drops 2-4 m to the water.

**The swim and tennis centre (from the close satellite, about 0.14 m per pixel, checked against cars and stalls).**
Real north is up; the river is west and south of it.
- *Pool clubhouse*: about 24 x 38 m, long axis north-south, in the middle of the compound. Hipped and cross-gabled roofs
  in dark charcoal shingle, the lower porch roofs grey standing-seam metal, one big tan stone chimney, a white two-storey
  porch with square columns and railings, white trim, cream-white siding. Two storeys at the porch, about 9-10 m to the ridge.
- *Pools* (all on one tan stamped-concrete deck): an **L-shaped lap pool**, 25 m x about 14 m (8 lanes) with a diving well
  about 10 x 10 m stepping off its north-east corner; a **free-form pool** to the north, about 17 x 13 m; a **splash pool**
  to the south, about 18 x 15 m, zero-depth, with a play tower about 6 m tall (blue frame, yellow rails, red tube slide,
  tipping red bucket on top) and red hoop sprayers. Water a light turquoise.
- *Deck*: the compound is about 45 m east-west by 90 m north-south. Loungers in rows along the lap pool; red umbrellas
  (about 2.7 m across) in a line down the east side by the lot; small cabanas and a pavilion about 4 m square with dark
  pyramid roofs at the corners.
- *Fence*: a brown privacy fence about 1.8 m tall, solid panels, with tan stone piers about 0.7 m square and 2.1 m tall
  every 6-8 m, all round the compound. Black lantern lamp posts about 4 m tall along it.
- *Lot*: east of the compound, about 25 x 90 m, two rows of stalls down a centre aisle, then Steeplechase Ln's loop and a
  roundabout with an island. Gate piers at the drive: tan ashlar stone, about 1.2 m square and 2.4 m tall, capped.
- *Courts* west of the pool clubhouse: three blocks of blue hard courts on a green surround (north-west), three pickleball
  courts (blue with red kitchens) beside the pools, and two blocks of two green-grey clay courts (west and south-west).
  A two-court block is about 35 x 36 m. Black chain-link about 3 m tall with a dark windscreen, light poles about 10 m tall,
  roofed bench shelters between courts, crape myrtles round them.
- *Cart and maintenance yard* north of the courts: an asphalt yard about 35 x 35 m crowded with carts and mowers, and a
  barn about 24 x 23 m with a brown roof.
- *To the west*: a cart path runs north-south along the outside of the courts, then a fairway about 40 m wide, then a
  wooded bank about 30 m deep (big hardwoods, pines), then the river. Court fence to the water is about 70-80 m.
- *The river*: the Chattahoochee, about 50-70 m wide here, wrapping the peninsula on the west and south. Its far bank is
  wooded and climbs steeply (see the README). Water from the satellite is a murky olive grey-green.
  No picture shows the beaches or the rope swing; their look is from Michael's note only (see gap D1).

**The main clubhouse terrace (from the two deck photos).** A concrete apron, then a near-semicircular terrace of
red-brown brick pavers about 22-25 m across, split by curved beds of deep purple loropetalum and clipped boxwood into
three bays; 8-10 dark metal tables with chairs and closed cream umbrellas. A low step down to a putting green about
25 x 15 m, with a bronze golfer seated on a bench at its edge. Beyond it the 18th-ish green and the course roll away
to tall pines and a tree line; the pond is to the left, below.

**The course.** Wide open rolling turf, bermuda (green in summer, straw tan when dormant in March), cut into fairway,
lighter greens and darker rough. Bunkers of near-white sand with sharp grass lips. Concrete cart paths about 2.5-3 m
wide, curving. Trees: tall loblolly pines in groves (clear trunks to 10-15 m, crowns on top), big single oaks in the
open, crape myrtles (pink, multi-stem, 5-7 m) by paths and buildings. Ponds and creeks are irregular, grass to the
waterline, dark still water. A two-arch stone cart bridge crosses a creek: deck about 3 m wide and 12 m long, parapets
of dry-stacked dark grey stone about 0.7 m tall that hump over the arches. Houses back onto the course in places
(white porches, big lots) but the course has no fence.

**Michael's memories.** They ran across the course toward the river, hung out on the river beaches, swung off a rope
swing tied to one of the trees there, and golfers in carts were annoyed or angry with them. No particular fight spots.
