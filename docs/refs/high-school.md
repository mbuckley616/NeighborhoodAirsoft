## The real place
Sizes are measured off the satellite scale bars; heights are estimates from the photos.

**Where it sits.** On the high, flat ground north of Holcomb Bridge Rd, about 3 km north of the river, near the Kroger.
The heightmaps show no ravine through the campus: it is a graded plateau. The ground falls only at the edges, where
Scott Rd runs round the west and south about 4-6 m below the grounds (a retaining wall plus a grass bank, 184605; the
back drive climbs from the road, 184640). Woods (pines, oaks, sweetgums) close the north and east, with two-storey
houses beyond them.

**The layout, west to east** (north is up in 184422; the campus is about 500 m E-W by 400 m N-S):

|---|---|---|

| Home grandstand + press box | about 64 m long × 25 m deep (with concourse) | East side of the track, facing west. |
| Visitor stands (aluminium) | about 66 m long × 10 m deep | West side of the track, backing onto Scott Rd. |

**The stadium** (184444, 184452, 184507). Eight-lane red track (sunlit `#b35c53`, shade `#a24f49`) round a royal-blue
apron (`#023e9c`). Turf `#558d5e` with white yard lines, black end zones with white lettering, a block letter mark at
midfield (do not copy; the game uses its own). Yellow goalposts at both ends. The home grandstand is concrete, about
20 rows (about 8 m to the top row, 16 m deep), in three sections with stairs and blue rails (`#6388b1` in sun) between
them, a dark blue fascia along the front wall (`#132f61`), a block of blue seats (`#3072a8`) in the centre high up, and a
two-level glass press box (about 25 m long, 4 m tall) on top. Team benches stand on the track in front. Tall light poles,
about 25-30 m, with banks of floodlights: two behind the home stand, more round the oval (six or eight in all).
Chain-link fence with a blue windscreen round the track; tall hardwoods behind the north end.

**The front entrance** (184535, 184812). A one-way circle drive (about 31 m across, the lane about 7 m wide) with red-painted
fire-lane kerbs, round a raised grass island (15 m) with one tall flagpole (about 12 m, two flags) and a small paved
apron. The entrance itself is a two-storey glass hall (about 14 m wide, 9-10 m tall) under a curved, standing-seam metal
roof (`#8d95a7` grey, it arches like a shallow barrel and overhangs the front), framed by two tall piers of tan stone
(`#eacda7` in sun), with the double doors at the centre. Seen from above, the entrance roof is a cross of four gables
(184812). Either side: one-storey red-brown brick wings (`#775042` in sun, `#6f4334` shade) with cream trim; on the right a
taller white block (`#c6cac4`) with a cream band. Lamp posts and blue sign posts along the walks; a few red maples.

**The lots** (184422, 184812). Plain asphalt (a weathered mid grey; the 184535 photo has a blue cast), white stall lines,
stalls 2.7 × 5.5 m in double rows of about 17 m (two stalls plus a 6 m aisle). Each row ends in a curbed grass island
with one small tree; tall lot lights stand along the islands. The west lot is the bigger, with a small building near its
north-west corner. The east lot is an L: one long east-west double row along the school's east wing and two north-south
double rows east of the circle. Buses park along the east side.

**The school's walls** (184535, 184605, 184640). Red-brown brick base, a tan stucco upper band (`#c8bc9c` in sun, `#8a836b` in
shade) on the taller parts, few and narrow windows on the back walls, flat grey roofs (`#a8aaa7`) dotted with rooftop units.
The dome is white with grey seams.

**The practice fields** (184800). Natural grass (`#7a7e6c`, duller than the stadium turf), lined every 10 yards (9.1 m)
across, with small portable goals at both ends rather than goalposts. A tackling sled and a few benches stand on it.
The baseball field: red-brown infield and warning track (`#c98a6a`-ish sunlit), a tall dark outfield fence, a press box
and a long grey-roofed building on the first-base side, a green-netted batting cage. Tennis courts blue (`#295694`) inside
a green surround.

**The back entrance and the mural** (184640, 184605). The back drive leaves Scott Rd on the south-west side and climbs
about 5 m over 60-80 m between lines of young oaks and pines to the west lot and the stadium. Near it, along Scott Rd, a
long concrete retaining wall holds up the grass bank under the brick classroom wings; a black chain-link fence runs
along its top. The mural is painted on that wall, facing the road.

**The mural, described so it can be painted as our own.** Wall: plain beige concrete (`#b8ab92` lit, `#958a76` in
shade), roughly 4 m tall (estimate), the painted part about 14-16 m long. The piece is graffiti-style: a row of six or
seven huge, chunky, interlocking 3D block letters, about 3 m tall, slightly staggered up and down. Each letter face is a
"chrome" gradient: pale blue-white at the top (`#c8d4e2`), sky blue (`#8aa4c4`), steel blue (`#4f6a92`), then a hard
horizon line to dark navy (`#1c2836`) in the bottom half, with white glints. The extruded sides are dark navy to black,
outlined in near-black (`#141a20`). Behind the letters a ragged white and pale-blue spray burst (`#e8ecf0`, `#b8c8dc`)
spreads out to both ends like a splash. Above the right half of the letters, a line of smaller black slab capitals; above
the left half, a cartoon figure with a lance and a red-orange pennant. A small hand signature at the far right. For the
game: keep the palette, the chrome block letters and the splash; spell a word of our own (e.g. `RIDGE`, with
`HOLLINS RIDGE` in black slab capitals above), swap the figure for a plain red pennant on a pole (or our own mascot, if
Michael wants one), and leave out the signature. A 1024 × 256 canvas on a 14 × 3.5 m plane is enough.

**Where the fights happened.** Michael's notes put the fights in the two lots, the practice fields and the stadium.
