## The real place

**Where and which way.** The north-east corner of Holcomb Bridge Rd (GA 140) and Eves Rd, on the upland about 2.5 km
north of the Chattahoochee, just south of the high school. No water anywhere near: the heightmaps put it on high ground,
and the lot is graded flat. The store stands on the north side of its lot and faces south (about 10° west of south) toward
Holcomb Bridge Rd, which is how the game has it already (store at −z, the road at +z). The ground rises a little into
woods behind the store and to the east (pines on the skyline in 184033); the lot itself reads level, falling slightly to
the south for drainage (a metre or two over its depth at most; not measurable from these pictures).

**The store front (estimates from the Street View; no scale in them).**
- Long red-brown brick wall, running bond, with buff horizontal bands (a sill band near 1 m, one above the canopy, one near
  the top) and a light parapet cap. Parapet ~9.5–10 m.
- A continuous dark charcoal steel canopy at ~3.6–4 m, ~2.5 m deep, held by diagonal tie-rods up to the wall, over the
  front walk the whole length.
- Under the canopy: aluminium storefront glazing and glass doors between brick piers.
- Above the canopy: tall clerestory windows, grey-green glass in light frames, ~4.5 m to ~8.5 m, in six or seven bays
  separated by brick piers. The store's sign sits on this band just west of the east tower, ~12 m long, letters ~3 m tall.
- **Two entrance towers**, one at each end of the block, each ~12 m wide and ~14–15 m to the top, standing ~2 m proud of the
  wall: brick corner piers with buff cast-stone panels, white cornice caps, a glazed front over a vestibule with its own
  small canopy. The **west tower** has a dark standing-seam hipped roof with a small gable over its window; the **east
  tower** is a full glass gable with a half-round fan window under a dark metal edge.
- Along the walk: stepped racks of potted flowers either side of the east entrance (a garden-centre look), patio swings
  and outdoor furniture for sale further west, benches, carts, a propane/ice cage, a crosswalk with a painted STOP and
  stop signs at each tower, yellow bollards at the crosswalk ends, blue accessible stalls in the first stalls opposite.

**The strips.** One storey (~6–7 m), brick, with a dark standing-seam metal mansard over the shop signs and a recessed walk
with black steel tube railings. **The Mexican restaurant** closes the west strip: a brick front with a white cornice, three
white round arches on white columns making a covered porch, a black iron fence round a patio of turquoise tables and
chairs in front, potted cactus by the door, and a pale cream corner tower (~10 m) with red trim at its top on the west
side. It reads from across the lot as the one non-brick-box shape on the frontage.

**The lot.** Asphalt with cracks and patches, white stall lines, blue accessible stalls, white arrows in the aisles. Each row
ends in a curbed planting island (red-painted noses at the drive) with low rounded evergreen shrubs (holly or boxwood,
~1 m, yellow-green), a few boulders, and either a crepe myrtle (multi-stem, 4–6 m, smooth tan bark, bare in April) or a
mature round-crowned shade tree (oak or maple, 10–12 m). A long island of the same runs between the front drive and the
row ends. Tall grey light poles (~10–12 m) with flat shoebox heads stand in the islands; a flagpole (~15 m, US flag) stands
in an island west of centre near the store. The woods round the centre are tall loblolly pines mixed with hardwoods.
