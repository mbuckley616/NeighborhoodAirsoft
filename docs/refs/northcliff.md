## The real place
**Where it is.** Just west of Eves Rd, south-east of Martin Lake, about 0.9 km east of the Chattahoochee where it runs
north-south and about 1.3 km north of where it turns east (overview map). The creeks drain west to the river. The
heightmaps are too coarse here to measure the slope; the creek bottom is plainly the low ground and the loop's middle
the high ground, probably 8-15 m between them (an estimate).

**Layout** (metres from the satellite):
- Northcliff Trace is a **loop**, about 130 m east-west by 150 m north-south on its centreline, with houses on both
  sides of it (a block of about eight inside, a ring outside). The loop is reached from the south-east: a stem road
  about 80 m long runs from its south-east corner down to a **junction** in the creek bottom, where Stoneglen Close
  leaves to the north-east and Northcliff Trace carries on south across the creek.
- **The loop's south leg is the game's street.** It runs roughly west-north-west to east-south-east, about 130 m
  between the corners. The houses inside the loop (north side) sit up the hill; the houses on the south side back onto
  a wooded creek corridor whose creek is 35-40 m south of the road, the same distance as the game's. The west end does
  not stop in a bulb: the loop bends north. The east end leads to the junction and the club.
- Lots are about 30 m wide along the road; houses sit 12-19 m back from the kerb. Footprints are about 15-18 m wide
  and 10-12 m deep (the listings say 3,000-5,400 sq ft, two storeys).
- **The club** (Michael's hub), on the south-east side of the junction, in the creek bottom: a 25 x 12 m pool (water
  `#b8f0fd` from above) inside a pale concrete deck (`#f0e0d3`) about 4 m wide, a clubhouse about 11 x 12 m with a grey
  roof on the deck's north end, two tennis courts side by side east of the pool, about 32 x 34 m together (blue courts
  `#7094b8` in a green surround `#85a4a6`, chain-link round them). A spur road about 60 m long runs east from the
  junction along the club's north side, with parking on it. The side creek runs in a wooded ravine just east of the
  courts. Trees close round all of it.
- **Stoneglen Close** climbs about 200 m north-north-east from the junction and ends in a cul-de-sac bulb about 24 m
  across, with a narrow (about 3 m) lane leaving the bulb to the north. Houses on both sides; the east-side back yards
  run 50-100 m back to a belt of woods. Through those woods a straight cleared line runs north-east, parallel to the
  street's south half; the road map draws the side creek along it. A sewer-easement track along a creek is common here
  and could be Michael's "raised private gravel road", but the pictures can't confirm which line it is or where the
  twins' yard was (and the guide shouldn't point at a house).

**Trees and ground.** Almost complete canopy: loblolly pines (straight bare trunks, crowns high up, 25-30 m tall) mixed
with oaks and poplars of 20-25 m; houses sit in clearings and most front yards are pine straw (`#9a8066`), mulched beds
(`#836049`) and shrubs, with lawn only where the canopy opens (summer lawn in dappled shade about `#5a603f` to
`#767a55`; winter dormant `#948764`). Driveways are concrete, long and often sloped (`#6b7376` in shade).

**Houses.** Two of the three photographed are stucco with fieldstone: off-white (`#c8c9c5`) or tan (about `#a89878`
sunlit, `#6a6350` in the shade it is mostly in), fieldstone (`#afa895`, `#9c8b7b`) at corners and entry gables, two or
three front-facing gables, arched windows. The third is a red-brick colonial (sunlit brick about `#ae8c73` on the
listing photo, redder to the eye) with slate-blue shutters (`#4c5a63`), dormers on a garage wing and a white garage door.
Roofs are asphalt shingle, brown (`#74605f`) or grey (`#5d5a61`-`#726d73`). Board privacy fences (`#c17e61`, stained)
close some back yards at the sides.

**Back-yard pools.** Four private pools show in about 25 lots (rectangles and a kidney), most with a deck.

**Where the fights were** (Michael): the twins' back yard off the Stoneglen Close bulb: open, flat, tree-lined, a raised
gravel road along its far end (parallel to the street), home-built courses, bunkers and cover, and the twins with
better guns. The pool was where the neighbourhood met.
