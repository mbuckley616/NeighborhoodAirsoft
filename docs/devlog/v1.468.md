## v1.468 — The Glow-Stick Tracer
Michael chose **A** on "What the play lane builds next, after the ally callouts" (decisions, 9 Nov): two more homemade
parts, the glow-stick tracer on the MUZZLE and the tennis-ball grip on the UNDER slot, one a version. This is the first.

**What a player gets.** The **Glow-Stick Tracer**, $6, 0.1 lb, in the shop's Mods tab under MUZZLE: two green glow sticks
cracked and duct-taped along either side of the barrel's end, reaching 4 cm past the muzzle so the BB leaves between
them. It goes on the muzzle of any long gun (the 35 with a muzzle slot), so it is a trade against the cardboard barrel and
the soda-bottle silencer.
- **At night** every BB you fire draws a thin green streak behind it, 1.4 m long once it is clear of the gun, bright and
  not dimmed by the night fog, pointing back the way it came. You see where your shots go in the dark.
- **So do the kids.** A kid of another side within 35 m of the streak, with a clear line from his eyes to it, knows where
  it came from (`tracerSeen`, 78-bb-flight; he looks at most five times a second). For the next 0.3 s he takes it as a
  sighting of you where you stood when you fired (`_lastSawPlayer`, `_lastSeenPos` in `updateEnemies`, 80-ai): a kid
  with no line to you then fires covering shots at that spot and peeks quickly, as he does at a kid who just ducked. For
  3 s he also turns to you in a team match, 12 m nearer in his choice of target, as if you had shot at him (`threatScore`).
- **By day** the streak barely shows (an eighth as strong) and the kids take nothing from it: they could see you anyway.

The part's stats sit in `ATTACHMENTS.glow_tracer` (14-gear, last in the list so the shop's order is unchanged above it);
its model is in `buildMuzzleParts` (54-fp-gear); the streak is `addTracerStreak`/`updateTracerStreak` (76-bbs), a cone
that is a child of the BB's mesh, so it goes when the BB does, and hides once the BB has hit something.
`Game.scenario.timeOfDay` is now set when a match is entered (64-scenario-flow), for the streak and the kids.
