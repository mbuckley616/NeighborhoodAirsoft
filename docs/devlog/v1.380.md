## v1.380 — Co-op, step 1: a friend comes over to your room
Card `fable/d73-coop`, backlog D.73 (Michael, 23 Oct: "have another player join your bedroom and join in all of your campaign matches ... they came over to 'hang out'"). He answered the card's four questions on 28 Oct with **B**: 1B (your save is the story; your friend's bag gets the match money too), 2A (they walk round your room as their own kid; only you start matches, shop, use the workbench or sleep), 3A (one slot of your side in every match) and 4A (their own look and gear, a full bag of BBs). This first step is the room: getting a friend in, and seeing each other there.

**What a player does.** The phone has a new button over the texts, **CO-OP · have a friend over**. It opens a co-op screen on the phone: your name (the same one the Pickup Game's online board keeps), **HAVE A FRIEND OVER**, which shows a four-letter code, and **GO OVER**, with a box for a friend's code. Once the friend is in, each of you walks round the room and sees the other as a kid with the other's own look from their save, walking, turning and crouching as they do (no gun in the house, like the crew on the landing). A line says "BLUEY CAME OVER" in your game and "YOU'RE AT HOSTY'S" in theirs. At your place the friend's E on the map table, the computer, the closet, the workbench, the bed or the pickup board does nothing but say "HOSTY'S ROOM · ONLY HOSTY CAN DO THAT" (2A). Their phone works, and so does a word with the kids on the landing. The friend's phone reads **GO HOME** and yours **SEND BLUEY HOME**; either ends it. When the friend goes, your game says "BLUEY WENT HOME" and your door stays open on the same code. When you end it, their game says "Hosty ended co-op" and leaves them in their own room. Their save never moved.

**How it works.** Co-op is the online play link (`Net`, v1.230/v1.366) with `Net.coop` set. The new file `js/95-coop.js` (play lane, between the Pickup Game's file and the phone's) holds co-op's own screen, messages and the friend's kid. The friend's `hello` says it is for co-op, and the host's game answers with `coop` (who the host is). A Pickup Game guest at a co-op door, or the other way round, is turned away with a new reason ("That code is for something else"). A friend knocking while you are out in a match is told to try again when you're home. There is one friend at a time. In the room each game sends where its own person is about 15 times a second (`b`), and moves the other's kid from it as v1.366 moves a remote kid. Each game is in its own copy of the room, built from its own save, so the friend sees their own crew on the landing. While the link is a co-op one, the Pickup Game board's online panel says to send the friend home first. A match the host plays now leaves co-op as it was; step 2 takes the friend along. Nothing loads or changes unless a co-op link is open; single player is as it was.
