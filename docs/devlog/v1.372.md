## v1.372 — The AUG, a bullpup automatic rifle (gun batch 3)
Backlog D.63 (Michael, 22 Oct: "I think my ideal would be 30-50 ... And generally just a few more Automatic rifles"; his A on 23 Oct, batches of four to six). Batch 3 is his "few more Automatic rifles", one a version: the G36C came in v1.371, and this is the second.

The **AUG** is a battery bullpup rifle, $290, under AUTOMATICS between the G36C ($260) and the M249 ($320). In a bullpup the mag sits behind the grip, so a long barrel fits in a short gun, and that is what it trades on: its BB is the fastest and straightest of the rifles short of the two snipers, 58 m/s and straight for 22 m (the AK 55 and 20, the G36C 52 and 18). It is also tighter aimed (0.28 of the hip spread, the AK 0.35, the G36C 0.30), and you walk with it at 0.88 (the AK 0.85, the G36C 0.9). It fires a little slower than the G36C, 10.8 BBs a second (the AK 10, the G36C 11.7). Its hip spread is the AK's. The mag is a smoky see-through 330 (the AK's 350, the G36C's 300), with a 500 high-cap for $40 and a $20 spare. It weighs 7.5 lb. It takes a sight and two rail parts but no under-barrel part: its own folding foregrip is there, as on the P90 and the Vector.

In first person (`buildFPAUG`, `js/52-fp-guns.js`) it is olive green, which no other gun is. A long body runs from the butt plate forward, and the clear mag sits behind the pistol grip with BBs showing inside it. A top rail carries flip-up irons. Ahead are a round shroud with the folding foregrip under it, the long barrel and its orange tip. The big trigger guard runs from the grip to the foregrip, and the charging handle is on the left side. Your right hand is on the grip and your left on the foregrip. Aimed, the front post's tip sits on the reticle (`js/74-gun-hud.js`), and an optic sits on the rail between the irons. The kids' copy (`buildEnemyGunMesh`, `js/50-kids.js`) is drawn in their blocky style, though no kid carries one yet. It also has its own reticle, a shop picture, a Loadout-kid model and a spare mag in the loadout list.
