## v1.437 — The Clear Bullpup, a cheap see-through battery rifle (gun batch 7, D.85); 43 guns
The third gun of batch 7 (Michael's **A** of 4 Nov: six more to 46, the kinds the builder's pick from what the shop is
thin on). The clear plastic guns were all automatics, and the cheapest rifle was the $85 Spring Assault Rifle, so this
is a toy-aisle battery bullpup that leads the rifles.

**The Clear Bullpup**, $40, first under RIFLES: a see-through battery bullpup, the gearbox and the battery showing
through the plastic.
- **A BB a pull, four a second.** An electric semi like the Electric Rifle (six a second), no charging handle; click as
  fast as you like and it fires at its own cycle.
- **It jams.** About one pull in twelve it chews a BB after the shot and jams (`semiJam`, a new spec field read in
  `onLmbDown`'s electric branch, `js/70-input.js`): hold R to clear it, as on the clear automatics. The shop line says
  "Electric semi (a BB a pull, up to 4/s, jams 1 pull in 12)".
- **The trade.** A soft BB (36 m/s; the Electric Rifle 45) in a wider cone (0.38; 0.28) that starts to curve at 7 m
  (10), but it is light (3 lb, as the clear automatics) with their walk (1.1). It holds 100 BBs, and as with every clear
  gun there is no bigger mag and no spare: a speed loader fills it.
- It takes a sight and one rail, and as a long gun a muzzle and a stock part; no under-barrel slot.

The first-person model is new, on the clear guns' grip, guard and hand: a long clear body from the butt plate to the
front, the gearbox and the yellow battery inside, the milky mag behind the grip with BBs in it, a long clear barrel and
its orange tip, a milky carry rail along the top with the rear notch and the front blade, and the yellow power switch
on the right flank. A kid holding one (a loan to your crew) has the clear automatics' see-through body, longer.
