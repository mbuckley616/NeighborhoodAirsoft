## v1.375 — The cart ride and the derby get their own result lines
Found in play (critic, v1.372). Cart Path Only is won by riding two laps, and the Downhill Derby by reaching the bottom of the hill. But `endScenario` had no lines for either, so a win fell through to the ordinary tag-everyone branch: "YOU GOT THEM. The last one's out. Marcus, Jamie, Trey, Brooke, and Devon come walking out from behind cover." It read that way whoever was left; in 9 of the critic's 13 wins one to five kids were still in, and one win had no tags and no shots. A loss read "Too many angles, not enough cover", which is the team-match line.

Both now have their own lines (`js/64-scenario-flow.js`), and each counts the kids you tagged on the way: none ("You never tagged one of them"), some ("You tagged 2 of the 5 on the way") or all.
- **The ride, won:** "TWO LAPS, YOU MADE IT". Eric pulls the cart back up to the barn, and the kids still in pop up out of the bunkers and the trees to wave, named; or nobody was left to wave you in. "Same time tomorrow?"
- **The ride, lost:** "YOU'RE OUT". Eric stops the cart before the first lap is done, or on the second lap, and the kids climb out of the bunkers grinning.
- **The derby, won:** "YOU MADE IT DOWN". The cart rolls to a stop in the bulb, and the first of the lawn kids jogs down the hill: "No way. My turn in that thing."
- **The derby, lost:** "YOU'RE OUT". You climb out of the cart with so many metres still to go, and the kids come down off the lawns laughing.

The pay, the ladder and what wins or loses are unchanged.
