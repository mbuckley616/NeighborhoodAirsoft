## v1.381 — Co-op, step 2: your friend in your matches, with their pay
Card `fable/d73-coop`, backlog D.73, its second and last step. Michael's answer is B: 1B (the win and the ladder are yours; your friend's save gets the match's money too), 3A (one slot of your side in every match) and 4A (their own look and gear, a full bag of BBs). v1.380 got a friend into your room; until now a match you started left them standing there.

**What a player does.** With a friend over, start any match from the map table or the result card's PLAY AGAIN or NEXT, and your friend's game goes to the same briefing. It skips their own map and locks, so a new save can join you anywhere you have opened. BEGIN waits for both of you ("WAITING FOR BLUEY…"), as online play does. In a match where you have teammates your friend takes the last kid's place on your side: in Two on Two, Bluey comes instead of Seth. In a match where you are alone they stand beside you, and the other side gets one more kid from that street: in Just You and Seth, Trey comes along with Seth. They play with their own look, their own gun, eyewear and shoes and a full bag of BBs. The match's lives are theirs too, with their own eyewear's extra hits. A tag works as in your own matches: you come back to your start only where the match says so (The Last Saturday). A player tagged out stays on the field gun up and watches the other play on. The match is lost when both of you are out. The kids on your side don't keep it alive, as in your own matches.

The result is yours. The win goes on your summer: the ladder, the calendar, Seth's score and the crew's asks. Your friend's game shows the same result, pays the match's money and hit bonus into their own save (1B), and leaves their ladder where it was. Their card says "At Hosty's: the pay is yours; the win goes on their summer", and its PLAY AGAIN reads "NEXT: HOSTY'S CALL" with NEXT and PREVIOUS shut. When you go back to your room their game follows you there. When you play again or go on, it takes them into the next briefing. If your friend presses F they sit the rest of that match out and a kid plays their spot ("BLUEY SAT THIS ONE OUT"), and they are still over. If their link drops, a kid plays their spot and co-op ends. If yours drops, their match ends ("The host left") with nothing lost.

Some matches are one player's: a race, the ride-along, capture the flag, a VIP match, the zombie tag, a free-for-all, the tutorials and the range. Each needs its own sharing (a cart, a flag, a VIP), which co-op does not build. Your friend waits in the room through those and is told "HOSTY PLAYS THIS ONE ALONE · YOU WAIT HERE". While you are out alone, a match of yours pauses as it always has.

**How it works.** It is online play's referee model (v1.366) on co-op's link. `enterScenario` asks co-op for the match (`coopScenario`): on the host it is the match with the crew in it (`crewScenario`) and the friend put in. Each entry goes over the wire (`cm`) as plain values. A crew kid's loan of your gun stays out, so nothing of yours is lent from the friend's bag. The friend's game builds the same match from it, with the host as an ally kid in the friend's own place. From the briefing on, Net's match code runs it, with five co-op turns:
- The host's lives and bag are his own (`netAfterStart` and `netLaunch` leave them alone).
- The friend's lives in the host's game are the match's plus their eyewear's extra hits, sent with their `ready`.
- A tag on the friend sends them home only where the match does (`coopWalksHome`).
- Both out loses (`coopCheckOut`, in place of `checkWinCondition` for a person out).
- When the match ends, both games go back to hanging out (`coopMatchOver`) instead of online play's "over".

The friend's `endScenario` pays as any match does but marks nothing completed and asks no crew questions. Their timer never ends the match itself (the host's game says when). BACK TO YOUR ROOM keeps the link and sends `home`. The friend is one of your side's squad mates, so the wheel's orders reach them on screen and they ignore them, as a person would.
