## v1.428 — The long mag's extra BBs stay with the long mag (Found in play, v1.426)
The critic found it on 3 Nov: with the Taped-Up Long Mag on the AR and a spare 25-round AR mag in a slot, BEGIN reads
38 / 38, but swap the spare in and the HUD reads 25 / 38. The gun's mag size (`Game.gun.maxAmmo`) was set once at BEGIN
and the spare-mag swap only moved BBs, so the long mag's +13 belonged to the gun: a speed loader filled the 25-round
spare to 38, and the long mag went into the pouch under the spare's name, "Spare 25-rd AR Mag · 3".

Now a mag's size goes with the mag. At BEGIN each pouch remembers its mag's size (`Game.gun.spareCap`, a plain spare's
own size, at most the gun's bare mag, as it loaded before) and whether it holds the long mag (`Game.gun.spareLong`);
`Game.gun.magLong` says the long mag is the one in the gun. The swap (`updateGun`, `js/74-gun-hud.js`) trades all three
with the BBs. So with the spare in, the gun holds 25 and the HUD says 25, a speed loader tops it up to 25, and the
pouch reads "Taped-Up Long Mag · 3"; swap back and the gun holds 38 again with its 3.

The long mag's slow reload follows it the same way: a speed loader feeds 27 BBs a second only while the long mag is in
the gun (40 into the plain spare), and a swap takes 0.6 s when the long mag goes in or comes out (0.4 between two plain
spares, with two in your slots).
### Verified
