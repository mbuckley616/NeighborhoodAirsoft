## v1.386 — Static over the walkie
Backlog D.80 (Michael, control room, 28 Oct): "Audio that comes over the walkie talkie should have a 'static' filter over it, to make it sound more scratchy / like it's passing through a walkie talkie."

**What was there.** A walkie line (a teammate out of earshot, v1.268, or the answer to your squad order) was the talk button's click and then the kid's voice, flat and from nowhere: nothing said radio but the click.

**Why not a filter on the voice.** The kids' voices are the browser's speech (`speechSynthesis`), and its sound never passes through the page's AudioContext, so it cannot be band-limited or distorted the way a real filter would. The radio is laid round it instead.

**What changed.** `walkieStatic` (`js/76-bbs.js`, beside `walkieVoice`) plays, with every walkie line: a squelch as the button opens; a hiss, white noise through a 450 Hz high-pass and a 2.1 kHz band-pass, that crackles under the whole line (its level jumps every 35-85 ms, with an occasional louder pop); then a louder squelch tail and a second, lower click as the button lets go. The hiss lasts the line, guessed from its words at the walkie's speech rate (0.35 s + 0.3 s a word, 0.8-6 s). A new walkie line cuts the old one's static with its voice. The noise is its own buffer from `Math.random`, so a sound never draws on the rounds' seeded `gameRandom`. It goes through the effects volume like every other sound. A teammate near you still speaks where he stands, with no static.

### Verified
`tests/walkie-callouts.test.mjs` gains three checks. A teammate 200 m off speaks over the walkie and his line brings its static (a 3-4 word line: 1.24-1.53 s of hiss, 16-21 crackles); a teammate 4 m off adds none. Rendered offline at 44.1 kHz, a 1.5 s line's static is silent before it (RMS 0), a hiss under the line (RMS 0.018, about −35 dBFS), a louder squelch tail (0.036), and silent 0.3 s after (0). walkie, walkie-callouts, squad-orders, squad-hud and smoke pass.

### Still open
How loud the hiss should sit under the voice is for the ear: it is set at about a tenth of the speech's volume, so the line stays clear. The voice itself is still clean underneath; a scratchier voice waits for recorded voices (D.9), which can go through a real filter. Your friends' voices online (D.10's voice chat) do not get the static yet: that code is in `js/94-pickup-online.js`, under the co-op FREEZE.
