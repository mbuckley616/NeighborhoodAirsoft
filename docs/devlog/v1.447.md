## v1.447 — The Break-Barrel (D.87, gun batch 8's second gun); 48 guns
Michael chose **B** on "What the play lane builds next, after the third homemade wave" (control room, 5 Nov): gun batch
8, four more guns toward 50, the kinds left to the builder (backlog D.87). The question's second suggestion was a cheap
break-barrel rifle, and this is it.

**The Break-Barrel**, $60, under RIFLES after the Lever Gun: a beech-stocked spring air rifle from the farm store. One
shot, then you break the barrel down on its hinge to cock the spring and drop the next BB in.
- **One shot a break, 1.3 s to break and load** (1.43 s from the pull to ready, measured), the slowest cycle of the
  rifles with the L96. It uses the Double-Barrel's `break` cock with one barrel: the cock bar reads BREAK OPEN · LOAD,
  and the hint says "break the barrel and load a BB".
- **A stiff spring for the money**: a 58 m/s BB (the AR 45, the Lever Gun 55, the Pump Sniper 68, the sniper 75),
  straight for 16 m (the AR 10, the sniper 25), a tight cone (0.16; the AR 0.28, the sniper 0.12), aimed 0.28.
- **Light**: 6.5 lb, the Lever Gun's walk (0.9). BBs come in a tin of 30, a big tin of 50 for $8, spare tins $4.
- Slots: an optic on the spring tube and one rail on the barrel, a muzzle and a stock. No under-barrel slot: the off
  hand holds the forend just behind the hinge, and the barrel ahead of it is thin.

So it is the cheapest way to reach out past the AR, at $60 against the Lever Gun's $120, paid for in shots: it fires
less than one a second.

**The model** (`buildFPBreakBarrel`, `52-fp-guns`): a blued spring tube over a beech forend, the beech wrist and stock,
a brass trigger, and a long thin barrel on a hinge at the tube's front, with a breech block and its rear notch, a hooded
front post near the muzzle and the orange tip. Breaking it tips the barrel down 39° (`cockSwing`); the rail and any
muzzle part ride on the barrel (`breakPivot`, as the Double-Barrel's), and the optic stays on the tube. A kid holding
one gets a thin barrel, a spring tube, a forend and a stock.
