## v1.432 — The Double-Barrel, a break-action shotgun (gun batch 7, D.85); 41 guns
Michael chose what the play lane builds next on 4 Nov: **A**, gun batch 7, to 46: six more, one a version, the kinds
the builder's pick from what the shop is thin on ("a second revolver, a break-action shotgun, a cheap clear bullpup, a
DMR" were the examples). Every shotgun in the shop was a pump or the Semi-Auto, so the first is the break-action.

**The Double-Barrel**, $90, under SHOTGUNS straight after the Spring Shotgun, the side-by-side with the walnut stock
out of somebody's grandpa's closet:
- **Two barrels, a pull each.** Each barrel throws three BBs (the SPAS-12's three) at 33 m/s. The first pull fires the
  right barrel and the gun stays cocked; the second, once the shot's quarter-second lockout is out, fires the left.
  Nothing to pump between them (`barrels: 2`, read in `onLmbDown`, `js/70-input.js`).
- **Then break it open.** After both, hold LMB to break it and drop two shells in: 1.5 s, the longest re-cock of any
  shotgun (the Thunder Pump 1.05, the SPAS-12 1.0). From the second shot to ready again is about 1.6 s. The cock bar
  reads BREAK OPEN · LOAD and the hint "Click & hold LMB to break it open and load two shells."
- **The cloud** sits between the SPAS-12's and the Spring Shotgun's (0.48; 0.42, 0.55), straight for 9 m. The walk is
  the Spring Shotgun's (1.0), at 6.5 lb (6). The shell pouch holds 36 (a bandolier holds 54 for $14; spare pouches 36
  for $10).

So it is the hardest-hitting pair of shots up close (six BBs in a quarter second), and then you stand there with it
open. The shop's line for it reads "Break-action, 2 barrels (a shot each, then break it open)".

The first-person model is new: a case-hardened grey action, two blued barrels side by side on a hinge at the action's
front with a rib between them, a brass bead on the rib at the spring shotgun's height (so you aim it as you aim the
SPAS-12), orange tips, a walnut forend under the barrels, a top lever, two triggers in the guard, and the spring
shotgun's wrist and stock in walnut. Breaking it tips the barrels down 30° on the hinge with the off hand on the forend,
and the brass of two shells shows at the breech. It takes a sight (on the action), two rails and the under-barrel slot
(on the barrels), and the muzzle and stock slots. Whatever is bolted on ahead of the hinge rides on the barrels and
tips with them (`breakPivot`, in `ensureFPGunAccessory`, `js/54-fp-gear.js`). A kid holding one has a blocky one of his
own (two barrels over a walnut forend, the stock), but no kid carries one. The game has 41 guns.
