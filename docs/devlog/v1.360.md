## v1.360 — A briefing says who your crew brought: "Today it's three a side"

Rewriting each briefing for every crew would be dozens of texts that change as the crew grows, so the written text stays and, when the sides as built differ from the match as written, one line goes after it: how many a side and who is different. "Today it's three a side: Eric is with you and Priya is with them." It names a kid added to your side ("is with you"), added to theirs ("is with them"), a written kid who moved sides ("is on your side today", "is on their side today") and a written kid left out ("isn't playing"). A briefing that already counts its sides (the v1.349 ones, and the Last Saturday's "Six of you against eight", which names its sides as built) gets no line, and nor does a match the crew doesn't change. The line is in `crewChangeLine`, called from `sideCountDesc` (`js/24-scenarios.js`, the maps lane's file: one function beside v1.349's, which the play lane wrote), so the map card's brief and the intro card say the same.
