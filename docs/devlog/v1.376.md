## v1.376 — The upstairs landing, with your crew on it
Backlog D.74 (Michael, 23 Oct, his brother's idea: the landing outside his room was their common room, with a black futon, a black coffee table, a TV on a raised platform in the corner and a two-spot desk). His C on 28 Oct ("Basically A, but I added ... photos"), then B: a local session wrote his photos and floor plan up as `docs/refs/landing.md`. This version is that guide's step 1, built from its measurements. The bedroom's furniture and colours are untouched, as he asked.

**Before:** the hall south of your room ended at a shut door that said "Go outside" and opened the map. A glass back door on the hall's west wall went to the backyard range. Michael says the real house had no door there.

**Now:** the hall's door stands open, swung back against the landing side, and you walk through onto the landing (`buildLanding`, `js/30-bedroom.js`). It is 4.8 by 4.7 m with a flat 2.7 m ceiling and a white drum light, tan walls, beige carpet and white baseboards.
- **North wall:** the bifold closet beside the opening.
- **East wall:** the pair of double-hung windows, with trees outside as through your bedroom window. The black futon sits under them, turned 28° from facing west toward the TV, with the black coffee table in front of it.
- **South wall:** a recess holding the white built-in desk: a drawer pedestal, a computer spot, a second pedestal, a second spot. At its west end is the corner unit cut at 45° with open shelves. Its top is the TV platform, 0.39 m above the desk, with a black CRT facing the room. Two black office chairs are pulled out at the spots. The desk tops are empty (Michael).
- **South-east corner:** the stairwell, with no door. Four steps go down along the east wall to a square platform, then the flight turns west and goes on down out of sight, with a black handrail. The top of the stairs is an invisible wall, and E there says **Go out back (the range)**. That is v1.257's back door moved here, still the `back_door` interactable. Coming home from the range puts you at the top of the stairs, facing into the landing.
- **West wall:** the opening to the hallway, 1.1 m wide and 4.5 m long. It has an attic hatch, a return-air grille and two recessed lights. At its end are two shut six-panel doors: Sean's in the north wall and the far-end door.

The back door, its glass and its casing are gone; the hall's west wall is plain. "Go outside" is gone too: matches start from the map table only (Michael, 28 Oct).

**The crew** (D.28's five, in the order they joined) are there whenever you are home. The first two sit on the futon, the next two at the desk chairs, and the fifth stands by the hallway, facing the room. Nobody carries a gun in the house. A new seated pose (`poseKidSeated`) puts a kid's hips on the seat, his legs angled down to the floor in front of him and his hands in his lap. Walk up to one and the prompt reads "Talk to Eric". Each E gives his next line, three lines a kid, in the prompt and in his voice (`CREW_HOME_LINES`). With nobody in your crew the landing is empty.

**Lighting.** The bedroom's sun comes in from the north and, with no shadows, through every wall. Every north-facing face on the landing was in full sun (the hallway's south wall and the desk front were blown out white). Those faces now take their colour at half, so they read like the rest of the landing. The bedroom's own lights are unchanged.
