## v1.374 — The room-tour note stays in the room
Found in play (critic, v1.372). The sticky note from v1.357, "A LOOK ROUND YOUR ROOM", stayed on screen once you left the bedroom. A new save starts at the front door, so a first-timer's first walk out carried the note over the map, the briefing, the tutorial (on top of its STEP 1 OF 5 panel) and the result card. It also still read 0 OF 4 though the door had been ticked. The cause: `updateInteractables` hides the note when the mode isn't the bedroom, but `stepGame` only calls `updateInteractables` in the bedroom, so the hide never ran. The note was left as it was drawn the last frame you stood in your room.

`stepGame` (`js/84-main.js`) now hides it on every step outside the bedroom. Back home, it is drawn again with the door ticked. Nothing else about the tour changed.
