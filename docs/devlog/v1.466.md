## v1.466 — The shop's Mods tab: a sort row
Michael chose **B** on "What the play lane builds next, after the sort row" (decisions, 9 Nov): the same sort row as the
Guns tab's (v1.464) on the Mods tab, by slot, price and owned first, now that the store-bought and homemade parts share
one column.

**What a player sees.** Under the Gun Mods header a line reads **Sort: SHOP ORDER · SLOT · PRICE · OWNED FIRST**, the
same underlined blue links as on the Guns tab, the chosen one bold black.
- **SHOP ORDER** (to start with): the order the tab has always had.
- **SLOT**: grouped from the top of the gun to the back, sight, rail, under the barrel, muzzle, stock, with a header over
  each slot (as each kind has one on the Guns tab), shop order inside it. The slot tag on each row was already there.
- **PRICE**: cheapest first ($5 to $55 today).
- **OWNED FIRST**: the parts you own a copy of lead, the rest in shop order behind them.

The sort holds through a buy (the tab redraws after each) and through the other tabs and back for the visit. Nothing
else on the rows changed: price, BUY / BUY ANOTHER, the copies you own.

Under it: `SHOP_MOD_SORTS`, `SHOP_MOD_SLOTS`, `SHOP_MOD_SLOT_NAMES`, `shopModSlot` (the slot tag, now in one place),
`shopSortedMods` and `shopState.modSort` (66-menus-shop). It reuses the Guns row's CSS, so index.html is unchanged.
