## v1.449 — The Long Revolver (D.87, gun batch 8's last gun); 50 guns
Michael chose **B** on "What the play lane builds next, after the third homemade wave" (control room, 5 Nov): gun batch
8, four more guns toward 50, the kinds left to the builder (backlog D.87). The question's last suggestion was a second
revolver kind, and this is it. It is the fiftieth gun.

The game had three revolvers: the Six-Shooter (a spring cap gun, single action), the Magnum (CO2, double action) and the
Snub-Nose (the Magnum cut short). The new one is the other end from the Snub-Nose: a long-barrelled single-action
target revolver.

**The Long Revolver**, $170, last under PISTOLS after the Snub-Nose: a stainless CO2 revolver with an eight-inch barrel
and a walnut grip.
- **Single action**: thumb the hammer back for every shot, as the Six-Shooter (hold LMB, 0.35 s, the Magnum's hammer
  time). So it fires slower than the Magnum, which needs no thumbing.
- **The straightest revolver**: a 48 m/s BB (the Magnum 44, the Six-Shooter 32), straight for 12 m (the Magnum 9), a
  0.22 cone (the Magnum 0.30), aimed 0.26. Of the pistols only the Bolt Pistol beats it (52 m/s, 16 m), and that works
  a 1 s bolt each shot.
- **3.5 lb**, the Magnum's weight, and a 1.04 walk (the Magnum 1.06).
- Six in the cylinder, spare shells $5 (0.4 lb, a speed loader). A sight and a rail, as the other revolvers.

**The model** (`buildFPLongRevolver`, `52-fp-guns`) is built on the Six-Shooter, so its hammer comes back and its
cylinder turns a chamber as you thumb it, and its hands and aimed pose are the revolvers'. The frame and strap are
stainless, the grip walnut with a brass CO2 cap under the butt; everything forward of the cylinder is replaced by a
20 cm barrel with a half underlug round the ejector rod, a flat rib and a front blade near the muzzle, its tip at the
Six-Shooter's height. A kid holding one gets a stainless revolver with a long barrel.
