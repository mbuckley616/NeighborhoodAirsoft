## v1.435 — The workbench's Stock label no longer sits on the Under label
`workbench-labels` had been red on main since v1.425 opened the STOCK slot: on five guns the new Stock label crossed
the Under label. The workbench shows a long gun nearly end-on, and the Stock label hangs under the butt (6 cm below the
stock's lowest point, at its rear), so on a gun whose butt sits low and whose handguard is deep it projected only 10-13
px under the Under label, and the labels are about 16 px tall. The Semi-Auto Shotgun, the Bucket Gun and the MP5 crossed
in the bench's opening view (turned 0.5), the AR and the Electric Rifle square on as well.

Moving the anchor didn't work: the Under anchor is measured from the under-barrel parts and the Stock anchor from the
stock's box, and the two don't scale alike on screen (pushing the Stock anchor 5 cm under the Under one put it 80-90 px
lower, off the gun on 22 guns). So the Stock label is now pushed clear on screen, each frame the labels are placed
(`projectWorkbenchLabels`, `js/54-fp-gear.js`): if its box crosses another label's, it moves to just below that one, 2 px
apart. On the guns where nothing crosses it stays where it was.
