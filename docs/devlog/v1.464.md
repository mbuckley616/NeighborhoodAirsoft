## v1.464 — The shop's Guns tab: a sort row
Michael chose **B** on "What the play lane builds next, after the balance pass" (control room, 9 Nov): a sort row over
each kind's list on the Guns tab, so the Automatics' 19 and the Rifles' 14 stay easy to compare at 50 guns, in the
early-2000s site style, with v1.240's kind chips kept.

**What a player sees.** Under the kind chips (ALL, PISTOLS, SHOTGUNS, RIFLES, AUTOMATICS) a line reads
**Sort: SHOP ORDER · PRICE · RATE · WEIGHT · OWNED FIRST**, as underlined blue links the way an old store page lists its
sort options; the chosen one is bold black. Each kind keeps its header and its own guns; the sort reorders the guns
inside it.
- **SHOP ORDER** (to start with): the order the shop has always had.
- **PRICE**: cheapest first. The price is already on the right of each row, so nothing is added under the name.
- **RATE**: the most BBs a second the trigger gives, fastest first, with the number in red at the start of the row's
  line ("18 a second", "1.3 a second"): an automatic's cycle, an electric or gas semi's fastest pull, a burst gun's
  bursts with the 0.25 s between them, and a racked or broken-open gun's shots over that 0.25 s and its cocking. So the
  MAC-10 leads the automatics at 18, the Burst Rifle the rifles at 7.2 (its 12 a second inside a burst would mislead),
  the Break-Barrel ends them at 0.6, and the Rattle Can and Tick-Tock end the automatics at 2.
- **WEIGHT**: lightest first, in pounds from the weight the loadout already counts ("8 lb"); the M60 is last at 17.
- **OWNED FIRST**: the guns you own lead their kind, the rest in shop order behind them.

The sort holds through a gun's DETAILS and back, and with any kind chip (RIFLES alone, by price). A pad reaches the links
like any other control on the page. The workbench's gun list is unchanged.

Under it: `SHOP_GUN_SORTS`, `shopSortedGuns`, `shopGunRate`, `shopGunWeight`, `shopGunSortValue` and `shopState.gunSort`
(66-menus-shop); the row's CSS in index.html.
