## v1.427 — Northcliff Trace at real scale: the slide along a car that is nearly square
Card `fable/d51-northcliff` (backlog D.51), the last red before the card is finished. After v1.421 put Northcliff's
street at real scale, `road-slide`'s check "walking square into a car's side, he slides under 0.3 m along it" failed at
0.51 m: the long car nearest the road start now sits 3° off the x axis (angle −3.09 rad).

**What was wrong.** `updatePlayer` (`js/72-player.js`) moves x and z apart. Pushing square into a side 3° off an axis,
the step's small x part is clear on its own, so the split let it through every frame; the v1.139 face slide only stepped
in when the split got under half the step, and it then pushed him 0.5 mm back out, which re-opened the gap for the next
frame's x part. The result was a 0.17 m/s creep along any near-axis car, on any map, while he walked straight into it.
Real scale only made it show, by turning a car that way next to the test's start.

**What changed.** When the move collides, the face's own projection (the step with the part that goes into what blocks
him taken out) is now the slide whenever that spot is clear, instead of only when the split got under half the step and
the projection went further. Square into a side the projection is zero, so he stays put; at an angle he slides at the
face's true rate. When the projected spot is blocked by something else (a second obstacle), the split's result stands,
as before.

**A test that pinned the old street.** `road-slide`'s "past the car" check was `x < 27`, the west end of the car on the
old 74 m street. The 17° walk now meets the car's east end head-on, rounds it at the face's true rate (a little slower
than the split's cut, which over-shot) and ends at x 27.00, then stops on the lamp post at x 25. The car's west end is
now x 27.28, so the check is "x under the nearest long car's west end", measured in the test, not a number.

### Verified
- `road-slide`: square into the car 0.00 m along it (was 0.51 m); every Northcliff road start at 0°, 3°, 9°, −9° and
  17° gets past the car (17°: x 27.00 against its west end at 27.28), and he is inside an obstacle on 0 of 3,600
  frames.
- Green after the change: cars, winnmark-cars, car-side-fire, northcliff, northcliff-real, fence-bound, go-cart,
  golf-cart, ladder, ladder-prompt, backdrop, country-club, hollow-fort, and the suites listed in the PR comment.
