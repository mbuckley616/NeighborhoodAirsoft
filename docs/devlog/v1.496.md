## v1.496 — White snow on Bunratty and Winnmark (it was pink)
The critic found it on 26 Nov (Found in play): in every winter match the snow on Bunratty was pink, and on Winnmark
partly pink, most of all past the houses toward the tree line. The two maps' big ground meshes colour each vertex as a
*multiplier* on the summer grass (Bunratty's leaf litter x1.05/0.67/0.83, the river's red clay x1.6/0.74/0.97; Winnmark's
litter, the creek's clay, the pond's sand and the course's stripes). The winter pass (`applySeasonLook`, 63-seasons)
turned the ground's material to snow, and left those vertex colours alone because they are not green, so snow times
clay came out pink.

**What changed** (63-seasons). A big ground mesh whose material the season recolours, and whose vertex colours are
multipliers on it, is now recoloured by what each vertex really shows: the summer material times the vertex. That
colour goes through the season's usual rule (`seasonColor`: an upward face of grass, litter or clay is snow in winter;
grass is straw in fall), and the new vertex is the result over the new material. So the snow is white everywhere it
lies, and what the season leaves alone keeps its own colour: a steep clay bank stays clay, and on a fall day the woods'
leaf litter and the river's clay stay brown instead of being tinted by the straw. Meshes with no such multipliers (the
other maps' grounds, every tree and hedge) go through the pass exactly as before.
