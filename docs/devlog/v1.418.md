## v1.418 — The Taped-On Toy Scope, the first part of the second homemade wave (D.83)
Michael chose what the play lane builds next on 2 Nov: **A, more homemade parts**, a second wave in D.14's tone: "taped-on
scopes, a soda-bottle silencer look, a bungee sling, a longer mag taped up, a sock-padded stock", each fitting the newer
guns too. This is the first: the taped-on scope.

The sight slot had the Red-Dot ($35) and the 4× Scope ($55), both store-bought. **The Taped-On Toy Scope**, $14 in the
Mods tab (SIGHT), is a dollar-store telescope duct-taped to the top of the gun:
- **It zooms, a little:** aimed through it the view is 41° (the irons 55°, the 4× Scope 24°), under half the scope's
  extra zoom (`adsZoomBonus` 0.35, the scope's 0.8).
- **Through a small lens:** the scope picture comes up as the 4× Scope's does and the gun model hides behind it, but the
  lens is two thirds the size (42 of the screen's short side, the scope's 62), ringed in the telescope's red plastic, a
  faint blue tint in it, and the cross is drawn on in marker: short, thick and a few degrees crooked, with no red dot.
- **Nothing from the hip:** the Red-Dot tightens hip shots 15 % and the scope 20 %; the toy none. Cheap is its point.

The model is three stepped plastic sections (red at the front with a black lip round the big lens, then yellow, then a
black eyepiece), lying on a folded cardboard shim on the top rail, held by two bands of grey duct tape round the tube
that run down the sides and under the rail. It fits the sight slot of every gun, all forty, and the aim line is set off
its own tube height as the other optics' are. The sight code now asks for "any sight" and "a sight you look through"
(`getMountedSight`, `gunScopeView`, `js/14-gear.js`) instead of naming the red-dot and the scope, so the next sight part
needs no new branches in the HUD.
