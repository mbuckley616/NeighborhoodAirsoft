## v1.387 — Kids under heavy fire camp, fall back or push
Backlog D.81 (Michael, control room, 28 Oct): "some NPCs should have a tendency to fall back if they are receiving a ton of fire from enemies ... or maybe they have a tendency to camp once they are fired upon and they're in cover", the three kinds of player he sees under fire (panickers, campers, pushers), and "A pistol does not offset your aggression / bravery the same way a high-powered AK or LMG with thousands of BBs ready to shoot would." His answer to the question (29 Oct): **A**, by each kid's temperament, from the aggression he already has.

**What was there.** Nothing measured how much fire was on a kid. A BB within 2.5 m stamped a near miss (at most one every 0.25 s) and the v1.67 reaction ducked him, or ran him to cover, the same whatever was coming at him: one pistol BB or a string from the M249 read alike.

**Now** (`js/80-ai.js`, beside the cover helpers; the count in `updateBBs`, `js/78-bb-flight.js`):
- **Fire pressure.** Every live BB that passes within 1.5 m of a kid it could hit adds the weight of the gun that fired it, once per BB: a pistol (Spring, Six-Shooter, Bolt, Electric) 1; a rifle, shotgun, sniper or SMG 2; a heavy automatic (the AK, the M249, the G36C, the AUG, the M4) 3. The sum fades by e every 1.5 s. Both sides count, your crew's fire on the other side's kids too. Past 8 he reacts: a steady 2 a second from a spring pistol never gets there (about 3); a semi rifle at 3 a second or an AK string does in about a second.
- **By his aggression** (the `aggression` every kid already has, 0.2-0.88; nobody's numbers change):
  - **under 0.4, he camps:** down in his cover (or the nearest cover within 8 m that isn't toward the fire), twice as long between peeks, no moving cover after a shot and no marching out while he can't see you; he still pops up and fires over the top.
  - **0.4-0.7, he falls back:** to the nearest cover 3-16 m off that stands at least 2 m farther from the fire, his stand on its far side, firing loose rounds back toward the fire as he goes (one every 0.45-0.95 s, chest high, 3 m wide). With no cover behind him he scrambles 5 m straight away. Still pinned there 2.5 s later under heavier fire (12 or more), he goes back again, three times at most. Once there he holds it like a camper.
  - **over 0.7, he pushes:** the near-miss duck no longer turns him, and he marches at his target to 0.6 of his usual push distance before he settles to fight.
  - Snipers and defenders camp whatever their number: they hold their spot by design.
- **Calm.** Three seconds without a BB near him and the episode is over; he is himself again. A tag, a walk home, a flag run or a squad order ends it too, and none starts on a VIP, a practice dummy or the cart course.
- **A fix on the way:** the v1.67 run for cover under fire stood the kid on the *shooter's* side of the cover he ran to (`nearestShieldingCover` and the reaction both took the threat as behind him, `e.pos - dir`, where `dir` points at the shooter). He now stands on its far side.

### Still open
- The numbers are a first guess for the feel: the 1.5 m radius, the limit of 8, the 1.5 s fade, the 3 s calm, the 2x peek delay. A playtest will say whether kids break too easily under your AK, or not enough under the M249.
- Your own pressure on a kid comes from where you stood when you fired; a kid falling back runs from that spot, not from you if you have moved since.
- Online, a friend's BBs count at the middle weight (2), whatever their gun: their gun isn't on the BB here, and that code is under the co-op FREEZE.
