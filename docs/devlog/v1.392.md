## v1.392 — Riverside Market at real scale: a camping kid stays put on the turned rows
v1.391 left `under-fire`'s camp case flaky on the new lot: a timid Marcus under an M249 camped in 2 rounds of 3, and
when he did he sometimes ended 4–5 m nearer you. Two causes, one in the test and two in the AI.

The test's: its stream of BBs starts 2.5 m from him toward you, 1.1 m off his side. On the turned rows his own start
leaves him at 4 s at a parked car's face in some rounds (x 28.9, the south face of the car at z −11.7) and in the aisle
in others, 5 m apart, and from the car's face the stream starts inside that car and never passes him: no fire, no
episode. Each round now puts him at one spot, the open aisle east of that car column, (32.5, −9), the cars 1.9 m to his
west, standing and with no cover picked, before the stream. That pins a lot coordinate, as the card allows; the case's
lines are unchanged.

The AI's (`80-ai`), both found by tracing his steps with the stream's start moved clear: camping is meant to be "down in
his cover, peeking less" (D.81), and two paths walked him out of it toward the fire anyway. (1) The near-miss reaction
still runs while he camps (it is skipped only for falling back and pushing), and the nearest car that shields him from
the north is the next one up the aisle: 4.8 m toward you. Camping, it now keeps the camp's own rule, no cover more than
1 m nearer the fire; a car that breaks it is passed over and he gets down where he is. (2) The v1.122 escape (three
trigger pulls held at a blocked line, and he must move) overrode the camp's "no repositioning" and, timid, took the
farthest cover from you in reach, which in an aisle beside a car column is still nearer you. It now waits while he
camps; the holds are kept, and he moves once the episode ends after 3 quiet seconds. Falling back and pushing are as
they were. On the old lot, rows across his way, neither path had a car up the line to take, which is why it showed
only now.

The full run found one more test pinning the old lot: `backdrop` still asked every map's backdrop for eight houses or
more, and the store's by night for four shops. v1.391 gave the lot and the store the real centre's bands, which by the
guide (item 7: "the real place has none near") have no houses within sight, and the store's shops now start 69 m past
the glass, beyond the night's 52 m fog. Their lines now ask for shops and trees (by night at the store, its own lot's
cars); every other map's are unchanged. And `market-real`, this card's own suite, checked the starts with
`collidesObstacles`, which reads the player's obstacles (the bedroom's, where the suite stands), not the lot's: v1.391's
new free-for-all start `ffa_5` fell on something in the bedroom's plan. It checks against the lot's now.
