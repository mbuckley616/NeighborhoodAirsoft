## v1.434 — Northcliff Trace at real scale: a freed base kid comes looking once his side is tagged
Card `fable/d51-northcliff` (backlog D.51), red-CI upkeep. PR #235's head 9d4f8b8 failed `northcliff` in CI (headless
1/2): "northcliff_night_4v4: every kid moves or fires". Christian, the cautious shotgun at the back of their base in the
bend, walked 2 m and fired nothing in 60 s, while his side lost 6 lives. It passed 2 of 3 runs here before the fix, so
it is intermittent, but a player could meet it: one of their side sitting out the whole match at the base.

**What was wrong.** v1.431 frees a held kid (`holdNear`) in one of two ways. A foe comes within 30 m of his spot or
fires at him; that frees him and nothing more. Or a kid of his own side is tagged; that frees him and also marks him
bored, the "come looking" state (v1.152). But the second check only looked at kids that were still holding. A probe
over 6 rounds showed Christian freed by your crew's leads at 10-14 s, before anyone on his side was tagged at 13-23 s.
After that the tagged-side release never reached him. Freed but not bored, a cautious shotgun with nobody in range
hides and peeks at the base all round.

**What changed (js/80-ai.js, the play lane's file, a one-line condition).** The tagged-side release now checks every
kid with a hold who is not yet bored (`e.holdNear && !e._bored`), not only kids still holding. When a kid of his side
is tagged he comes looking, however he was freed. Nothing changes for kids without `holdNear`. The only kids with a
hold are the night match's side (`enemyHoldNear`) and the twins in the Twins 3v3, and in both the fight has reached
them by then.
