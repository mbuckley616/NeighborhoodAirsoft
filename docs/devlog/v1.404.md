## v1.404 — The M14, a marksman rifle (gun batch 5, D.63); 34 guns
Michael chose what the play lane builds next on 31 Oct: **C**, per-kid excuses (v1.397), then gun batch 5 toward 40,
"say a second pump shotgun, a gas pistol, an SMG, a DMR". The SPAS-12 (v1.398), the Gas Pistol (v1.399) and the
Thompson (v1.403) came first; this is the DMR, the last gun he named.

RIFLES jumped from the rifles (the AR and its kin: 45 m/s, straight for 10 m) to the snipers (70 to 85 m/s, straight
for 22 to 30 m, a bolt or the SVD's two pulls a second, and the slowest walks). **The M14**, $200, between the Burst
Rifle and the Spring Sniper Rifle, is the marksman rifle between them:
- **A battery semi** like the SVD: no bolt, every pull fires, **three a second** at most (the Electric Rifle 6, the
  SVD 2); a new mag is racked with the charging handle (0.6 s).
- **A BB faster and straighter than any rifle short of a sniper:** 60 m/s (the AR 45, the AUG's 58 the closest; the
  SVD 70), straight for 18 m (the AR 10, the SVD 22), spread 0.18 (the AR 0.28, the SVD 0.15).
- **It carries like the AR:** the same walk (0.85; the SVD 0.8), 9.5 lb.
- **A 30-round box mag**, a 50 for $22, a spare 30 for $10.

The first-person model is the spring sniper's barrel, sights and stock line, as the SVD's is, so its hands and aimed
picture are the sniper's: the forend and stock in walnut, a steel receiver cover, a straight black box mag ahead of
the trigger guard, a three-slot flash hider behind the orange tip, and in place of the bolt the op-rod handle on the
right. It takes a sight, two rails and the under-barrel slot. A kid holding one has a blocky M14 of its own (a long
barrel over a walnut stock, a box mag, no scope), but no kid carries one yet. The game has 34 guns.
