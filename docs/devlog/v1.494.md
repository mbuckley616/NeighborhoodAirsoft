## v1.494 — Leaves the kids can hear (fall days)
Michael chose A on "What the play lane builds next, after the season fixes" (control room, 24 Nov; backlog D.97): the
critic's leaves the kids can hear. On a fall day the leaves are dry underfoot. Until now fall had one rule of its own,
the wind (v1.487), and v1.492 had to take "they'll hear you coming" off a card because the kids couldn't hear anything.

**What changed:**
- **Your steps crunch** (72-player, 26-audio). On a fall day every step you take walking or sprinting plays a dry leaf
  crunch on top of the usual scuff: a few short, bright crackles (`playLeafCrunch`). A crouched step makes no crunch, so
  the sound tells you when you can be heard.
- **A kid near you hears it** (63-seasons `seasonFootstep`/`seasonHeardBy`, 80-ai). A kid within 12 m of a loud step,
  with no line on you, knows where you stood when you took it, as if he had seen you there. He reacts the way he does
  to a glow-stick tracer (v1.468) or your prints in the snow (v1.487): covering fire at that spot, a peek. He forgets
  that spot after the usual 3.2 s unless another step reaches him. Only the player crunches: the kids and a friend
  online are not heard. Fall nights are unchanged (no wind, damp leaves), and so are winter and the summer.
- **The briefing says it.** A fall day's weather line now reads "🍂 FALL · WINDY: LONG SHOTS DRIFT · LEAVES: THEY
  HEAR YOU WALK". The map card's badge is still "🍂 WINDY" (it is in the maps lane's 97-map-screen). The season cards
  are unchanged.
