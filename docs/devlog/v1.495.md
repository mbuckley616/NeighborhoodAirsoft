## v1.495 — Seth's back on your side in the fall and winter Winnmark matches
The critic found this on 25 Nov (Found in play). Four season cards on Winnmark say Seth is on your side: First Saturday
Back, Cold Hands, Sweatshirt Weather and Snow Day. Sweatshirt Weather and Snow Day say Trey is too. After a finished
summer, Seth wasn't on the field at all, and in those two Trey played against you. The cause was the crew's side rule
(`crewSideRule`, 92-summer-crew). It lets Seth stand with you only on Winnmark, and it knew Winnmark only by its summer
group, `winnmark_court`. The season copies are `fall_winnmark` and `winter_winnmark`, so it swapped Seth for another
kid. Michael chose **A** (control room, 25 Nov): Seth's back with you in the fall and winter, as the cards say.

**What changed** (92-summer-crew):
- **The side rule leaves all three Winnmark groups alone** (`WINNMARK_SIDE_GROUPS`: the summer's, the fall's and the
  winter's), the way it already left the summer's alone. Seth stays where the match puts him. He is still your rival in
  the summer's Winnmark replays after Lights Out (`crewSethTurned` is unchanged and looks only at the summer group).
- **Trey keeps his place in the two cards that name him.** In the season Winnmark matches, the match's own allies keep
  their places beside your crew, the way Seth always has (`crewSides`, `keep`). Before, a crew of five took every place on
  your side, and the street then sent Trey to fill theirs. The match's sizes still come from the first-time sides, so
  Sweatshirt Weather with all five of your crew is now 8 v 8 (Seth, Trey, your five and you, against eight). It was 7 v 9.
- The matches where Seth is against you are unchanged: the Leaf-Pile Fort ("Seth, Trey and Devon want it") still has
  him on their side.
