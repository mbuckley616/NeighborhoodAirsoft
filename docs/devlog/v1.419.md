## v1.419 — The Bungee Sling (D.83, the second homemade wave)
The second part of the wave Michael chose on 2 Nov (**A, more homemade parts**: "taped-on scopes, a soda-bottle
silencer look, a bungee sling, a longer mag taped up, a sock-padded stock"), after the Taped-On Toy Scope (v1.418).

The Shoelace Sling (v1.169) rides the gun on your shoulder: a sprint lasts a quarter longer, but the strap tugs and
aiming in is slower. **The Bungee Sling**, $10 in the Mods tab (UNDER), is its opposite trade, with the same parts of
the game it touches:
- **It snaps the gun up:** aiming in a third quicker (`adsSpeedMult` 1.35; the shoelace sling's 0.7). Letting go is
  unchanged.
- **And bounces it as you walk:** the walking cone is 1.3 times as wide (`moveSpreadMult`; the PVC Foregrip's 0.65).
  Standing still, no change; no weight.

The model is a yellow-and-black striped cord in a shallow sag under the handguard, a steel hook at each end, on the
under-barrel slot of every long gun with one (24 of the 40; not the pistols, the clear automatics, the P90, the Vector,
the AUG, the Thompson or the Uzi, whose front grips are there).
### Verified
