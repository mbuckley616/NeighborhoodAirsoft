## v1.358 — Setting the stage: four story cards on a new save, then Eric's text

**The cards.** NEW GAME now opens on four cards, one notebook page at a time with the ripped top, the holes and the red margin, over a short music sting (a soft C-G-E rising, then D down to C, under the Music slider like the result stings):
1. *Friday, 30 May 2003.* The last bell rings. No school till September: thirteen weeks of summer, and nothing you have to do.
2. *Everybody has one.* Every kid on the street has an airsoft gun now. Spring pistols from the mall, a battery rifle if your parents caved. Eye pro on, and a tag is a tag.
3. *The streets.* Winnmark, Bunratty, the Hollow down by the river, the lot behind the store: every street has its kids, and they all want the summer.
4. *Down the road.* Seth has set up targets at the end of Winnmark, and he's putting a crew together. By August somebody runs this neighborhood.

(30 May 2003 was a Friday, two days before the calendar's Sunday 1 June.)
