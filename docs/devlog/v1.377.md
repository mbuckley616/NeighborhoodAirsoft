## v1.377 — A match left behind lets go of the graphics card
Found in play (the deep-work session, 28 Oct, filed by the producer). Every scenario entry kept the scene before it on the GPU: eight entries of the night Hollow took `renderer.info.memory.geometries` from 1,478 to 11,445, and the long `stuck-sweep-*` suites ran out of memory on it (stuck-sweep-7 crashed on main alone in a cloud container). A player who plays a long evening, going home and back out, was piling up the same. The cause: three.js frees a geometry, a material or a texture only when told to (`dispose()`), and nothing ever told it. A dropped scene was gone from the game but not from the renderer, which keeps its own list of what it has uploaded. The sun's shadow map was the same: one render target per map, never freed.

`retireScene(old, new)` (`js/60-scene-entry.js`) now runs where `Game.scene` moves on: in `enterBedroom` (the match you are leaving) and in `enterScenario` once the new map is built (the room, or the last match on a retry or a next). It gathers what the new scene and the camera hold (your gun and hands ride on the camera), and disposes everything else the old scene holds: geometries, materials, their textures and the lights' shadow maps. A piece two scenes share is kept, and a shared piece disposed and later drawn again is simply uploaded again, so nothing can go missing, only be reloaded. `enterScenario` also drops the built maps of other scenarios it was still holding in `Scenes.scenarios`.

Nothing looks or plays different.
