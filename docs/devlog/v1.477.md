## v1.477 — A pallet stack by your start in the south fort
The weekly full run on main went red on `fort-spawn`'s "no tag before 6 s" (Take the North Fort, tagged at 5.8 s at the
`team_b` start), and the play builder diagnosed it for the maps lane (backlog, Found in play, 15 Nov). It was no
regression: standing still at the start (0.5, 32.04), at the back of the south fort, you were tagged inside 12 s in about
1 round in 6, some before 6 s, nearly all by the east creek's defender (Tyler with an MP5, filling Mason's `a_creek` slot
now that Mason is in your crew) from (20.2, 3.2), ~35 m off. Re-sampled here, 4 of 20 rounds were tagged inside 18 s
(5.2, 8.4, 10.1 and 10.5 s), every one by Tyler, his BBs coming through the right-front window or the doorway: from the
creek the fort's front shows almost its whole floor, and the crew standing beside you draws his fire too.

**What changed.** In Take the North Fort (`hollow_attack_north_fort`, the day attack) a stack of pallets
1.9 m tall stands on the south fort's floor just north-east of your start (x 0.85-2.2, z 30.45-31.25). It is in every line
from the east creek to where you stand, out of the walk from your start to the doorway, and too tall to stand on. It
sits 0.75 m off your start, the clear-space margin `findClearSpawn` keeps (a first try at 0.35 m pushed your start out
behind the back wall). The scenarios get it through a new variant of `buildHollowScene`, `south_fort_attack`, with
`seedArg: 'default'`, so the woods and every other prop stay where they were; the other Hollow matches (Night Assault on the Fort
among them), the walled fort in Hold the South Fort and the Pickup Game are unchanged. The fix is the map's, as the diagnosis said: closing the
right-front window alone would not do, since one tag in four came through the doorway.
