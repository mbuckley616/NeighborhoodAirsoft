## v1.490 — The bedroom calendar past August (fall and winter, step 6)
The last step on the card for Michael's **B** ("Fall and winter campaigns"). Until now, after the Last Saturday the
bedroom calendar stayed on August with every day crossed out, though the fall and the winter were open.

**What changed** (63-seasons; two small edits in 92-summer-crew). Once the Last Saturday is won, the calendar on the
wall turns to the season you are in. The fall's page has a picture of orange trees over straw grass with leaves in the
air, under "FALL 2003". The winter's has snowy pines and snow, under "WINTER 2003-04". Clicking it opens that season's
months: September and October, or December, January and February. Each place has its own days in `SEASON_CAL`:

| Place | Days |
|---|---|
| Winnmark (fall) | 6–20 September |
| The Hollow | 21–30 September |
| The school | 1–18 October |
| Bunratty (fall) | 19–31 October |
| Bunratty (winter) | 6–19 December |
| Bellfield | 20–31 December |
| The market | 3–31 January |
| Winnmark (winter) | 1–20 February |

So Halloween Night falls on the 31st and Christmas week on Bellfield. Inside a place, the date moves with the share of
its matches you've won, as in the summer. Days gone are crossed out, today is ringed and the place's first day carries
its name in pencil. Once Snow Day is won, the view says "the year is won" on Friday 20 February. The weekdays are 2003's
and 2004's real ones.

The summer's calendar, `summerDayIndex`, the texts' dates and the rival's red days are unchanged. A save still in the
summer sees exactly what it saw before.
