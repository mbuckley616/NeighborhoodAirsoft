## v1.379 — The match HUD stops rebuilding itself every step
Found while chasing the play lane's last Found-in-play item, `vip-cover`'s flake (Owen and his neighbour standing inside each other in the full suite), which this run could not run three at a time: each copy grew Chromium to about 4.5 GB and two of three died with "Target crashed". The memory turned out to be the test harness's (below), but the hunt for it counted what one step of a match does to the page, and three HUD pieces were rebuilding their DOM on every `stepGame`, 60 times a second, whether anything had changed or not:
- the four loadout boxes at the bottom (`updateLoadoutHud`): cleared and rebuilt as four new divs with their spans, 240 elements a second;
- the roster of kids (`updateRosterHud`): its whole HTML rewritten every step;
- the eyewear overlay (`updateEyewearOverlay`): with no eyewear on, cleared (`innerHTML = ''`, the canvas filter reset) every step.

Now (`js/74-gun-hud.js`) the loadout boxes and the roster are built as text and written to the page only when the text differs from what is there, the hint under the boxes likewise, and the eyewear overlay clears once. Nothing looks different: the same boxes, rows and hint, updated the step their contents change.

### Verified
- A check script (thrown away), Protect Ryan, 7,200 steps in one page with you untouchable: the boxes' `appendChild` 28,800 → 0 after the first, the roster's rewrites 7,222 → 1,706-2,055 (it still changes whenever a distance does), the eyewear overlay's 7,200 → 0.
- The suites that read these boxes and rows, one at a time, all green: `gamepad` (2 of 2), `pad-orders`, `wallet`, `pool-noodle`, `pause-menu`, `pointer-lock`, `smoke` (three runs), `vip-cover` (Owen and Joe 0-2.3 s inside 0.35 m), `squad-hud`.
- `tests/smoke.test.mjs` (v1.377's GPU check): it counted the room's geometries before the room had placed the camera, and the count is of what the camera sees, so the first visit read 813 one run and 538 another and failed once here. It now takes a step in the room first: 538 both visits, three runs.

### The memory, for the record (tests only, not this version)
Headless Chromium sometimes grants the pointer lock BEGIN asks for, and while the page holds it a long `stepGame` loop inside one `page.evaluate` grows the browser by about 70 MB a second of wall time: a bare 20 s busy loop in a match grew it 1,407 MB with the lock held and 7 MB after `exitPointerLock()`; in the bedroom, on the title or on the result card (no lock) 10-80 MB. Players never see it (the game yields to the browser every frame). The cure is in `tests/lib/game.mjs` (`g.scenario` lets go of a granted lock quietly), which is a pipeline file, so it is on its own PR for Michael's Approve, not this one. Its first try paused the match in `pool-noodle` and `pad-orders` (the grant's own `pointerlockchange` was still queued, so letting go read as Escape); it now waits for the grant to land first, and both pass.
