## v1.467 — An ally who goes looking says so
v1.465 sends an ally of yours looking at 8 s when he has had nobody in his gun's reach, so a cautious kid like Owen at
The Portables no longer sits out the opening beside you. But he left without a word, and you only noticed your side had
moved off when you turned round. Michael chose **B** (decisions, "What the play lane builds next, after the Mods sort
row", 9 Nov): when an ally goes looking at 8 s he says so on the walkie ("Going up the left", "I'll find 'em"), a few
new lines per kid, in `docs/voice_script.md`.

**What changed.** A new line situation, `looking` (20-characters): two lines of each kid's own (`VOICE_LOOKING_OWN`, all
33 kids, in his voice: Owen's "Fine. I'll go to them.", Haden's "CONNOR! I'm going up!", Grant's "Huh. Leaving the cars.
Just this once.") and five for each aggression band (`VOICE_LINES_DEFAULT.*.looking`), drawn the way every other
situation is (his own 7 in 10). `updateKidBoredom` (78-bb-flight) queues the line when the early look moves a kid out of
`hiding`/`peeking`; a kid already on the move when 8 s comes says nothing, since he hasn't changed anything you'd notice.

**Walkie or voice.** The line goes through `tryNpcSpeak`, so it follows D.10 as Michael set it (2 Oct: "regular voice when
in earshot, but otherwise should come in as walkie talkie callouts"): an ally beside you, as Owen is at The Portables, is
heard where he stands; one out of earshot comes over the walkie with its click and static and shows on the squad box. No
direction words ("up the left"): the kid doesn't know which way he'll go when he says it, and a wrong side is worse than
none.

**Two at once.** Several allies can be sent on the same frame. A queued line used to get one try once the voice
cooldowns cleared, and the walkie's 4 s gap then dropped the second ally's line. `queueNpcLine` takes a `walkie` flag:
such a line also waits for the walkie when its speaker is out of earshot, up to 6 s, so both are heard, 4 s apart.

The script (`node scripts/voicescript.mjs`) now has the 81 new lines under `looking`, toned "telling his side he is
heading out to find them".
