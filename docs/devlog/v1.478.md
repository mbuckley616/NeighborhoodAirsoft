## v1.478 — Mitchell's look holds until his AK reaches
The critic (16 Nov, Found in play, on v1.477) stood through First Time in the Woods (`hollow_skirmish_3v3`) again after
v1.476: your crew's Mitchell (an AK) now took the early look at 7.8-7.9 s in 3 rounds of 3, but 0.12-0.15 s later he was
back in `hiding`, moved 0.0-0.4 m all minute and fired 262-292 BBs, most past his ~35 m carry, landing 3-6.

**What was wrong.** v1.476 bounded only the early look's trigger (`_inReachNow`) by what the AK carries. Once the look
sent him off (`_bored`, state `advancing`), three things still read the AK's table, near 50 m and far 100 m:
- `advancing` commits back to a fight on a line inside FAR, so with Seth's team 47 m off he stopped on his first 6 Hz check;
- `hiding`, for a kid on the march, counts a line inside the fire-hold (the AK's NEAR, 50 m) as a shot from here, so he stayed;
- `spawnEnemyBB` ends a kid's boredom on any shot with a line (v1.152), so his first string into the dip ended the look,
  and every later one restarted the 25-30 s clock, so the late look never came either.
Reproduced here before the fix: 0.4 m moved in 3 rounds of 4 (16 m in the fourth), 118-268 BBs, 59-214 of them at foes
more than 2 m past his carry.

**What changed.**
- `updateEnemies` (80-ai): `reachFar` (v1.476's carry bound, about 34.7 m for the AK; every other gun its FAR) is now
  worked out beside the engage table. A bored AK kid (going looking: the early look or the late clock) with his target
  past it has no shot from where he is (`hiding` marches him on) and doesn't commit back from `advancing` until he is
  inside it. He still fires on the move as before (the AK's "fire while you close").
- `spawnEnemyBB` (76-bbs): for the AK, only a shot at a foe inside that carry (`_inReachNow`) ends his look or restarts
  his idle clock; a shot past it is not fighting.
- Only the AK, only a bored kid: an AK kid who is not looking (an attacker, a skirmisher who never got bored, the other
  side's kids in their own peek cycle) fires and commits from the same ranges as before, so first-shot distances on the
  maps are unchanged. The other side's last kid going looking is held to the same rule as your crew.
