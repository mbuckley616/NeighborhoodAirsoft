## v1.431 — Northcliff Trace at real scale: the night match meets at the corner, not down the straight
Card `fable/d51-northcliff` (backlog D.51). After v1.429 was merged with main, `northcliff-real` sometimes saw the night
match's first shot at 41-57 m, past the guns' 10-40 m. In 10 probe rounds it happened 4 times, at 55-58 m.

**What was wrong.** Every time, the long shot came from your crew's Joe (AK, which engages out to 100 m). He was still
on the straight at x 12-20, firing west at Haden, who had come down the west leg to the foot of the bend at (-37, -19).
Haden fills Mason's side from the crews (D.57), so v1.429's `holdNear` on the twins' entries never reached him: the
night match's side had no hold. A trace showed two ways out of the base:
- he wedged 3 m short of his spot in the huddle, and the deploy watchdog (v1.23a) sent him `advancing` after 1 s;
- once that was stopped, your crew's lead kids came within 40 m of the base by about 8 s, and Haden came down the leg
  to meet them while Joe was still 50-60 m back.

**What changed.**
- **`enemyHoldNear` on a scenario (js/64-scenario-flow.js, core).** Every kid of their side gets that hold, crew fills
  included, unless his own entry sets one. Only the night match uses it: `enemyHoldNear: 30` (js/24-scenarios.js).
  At 40 m the leads still drew Haden down the leg while Joe trailed (50-60 m shots in 4 of 10). At 25 and 30 m every
  first shot came at 17-29 m. 30 keeps them a little more lively.
- **A held kid is also freed once a kid of his own side is tagged, and then he comes looking (js/80-ai.js).** The
  first CI full run (b38bbe9) failed `northcliff`'s "every kid moves or fires". Christian, a cautious shotgun at the
  back of their base, held all 120 s of a round while three of his side were tagged. A freed but cautious kid still
  camps, so a release of this kind also marks him bored. That is the game's existing "come looking" state (v1.152).
- **A holding kid who wedges on his deploy settles where he is (js/80-ai.js, the play lane's file, a 4-line edit).**
  He no longer pushes on. This also applies to the twins in the Twins 3v3, the only other kids with a hold.
