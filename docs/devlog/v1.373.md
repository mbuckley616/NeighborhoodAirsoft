## v1.373 — The M4, the all-round automatic rifle (gun batch 3)
Backlog D.63 (Michael, 22 Oct: "I think my ideal would be 30-50 ... And generally just a few more Automatic rifles"; his A on 23 Oct, batches of four to six). Batch 3 is his "few more Automatic rifles", one a version: the G36C came in v1.371, the AUG in v1.372, and this is the third.

The **M4** is a battery carbine, $275, under AUTOMATICS between the G36C ($260) and the AUG ($290). Each of the other two rifles trades something away; the M4 is the all-rounder. It has the AK's BB (55 m/s, straight for 20 m) and fires faster than any of the rifles, 12 BBs a second (the AK 10, the G36C 11.7, the AUG 10.8). You walk with it at 0.92, quicker than with any other rifle (the G36C 0.9, the AUG 0.88, the AK 0.85), and at 6.5 lb it is the lightest of them. What it gives up is aim. It is tighter than the AK aimed (0.32 of the hip spread, the AK 0.35), but wider than the G36C (0.30) and the AUG (0.28). Its hip spread is the AK's. The mag is a curved black 300, with a 450 high-cap for $40 and a $20 spare. It takes a sight, two rail parts and an under-barrel part.

In first person (`buildFPM4`, `js/52-fp-guns.js`) it is a black flat-top: a top rail runs the length of the gun, carrying a flip-up rear aperture and the front post on its tall base. Below it are a railed handguard with a rail down each side, a short barrel with its orange tip, the curved mag raked forward in its well, the pistol grip and guard, the buffer tube and the sliding stock. The charging handle is at the back of the upper. Your right hand is on the grip and your left under the handguard. Aimed, the front post's tip sits on the reticle (`js/74-gun-hud.js`), and an optic sits on the rail between the irons. The kids' copy (`buildEnemyGunMesh`, `js/50-kids.js`) is drawn in their blocky style, though no kid carries one yet. It also has its own reticle, a shop picture, a Loadout-kid model and a spare mag in the loadout list.
