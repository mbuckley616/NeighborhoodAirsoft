## v1.448 — The DMR (D.87, gun batch 8's third gun); 49 guns
Michael chose **B** on "What the play lane builds next, after the third homemade wave" (control room, 5 Nov): gun batch
8, four more guns toward 50, the kinds left to the builder (backlog D.87). The question's third suggestion was a battery
DMR, and this is it.

The game already has a battery marksman rifle, the M14 (v1.404), in walnut, so the DMR is built to sit beside it rather
than on top of it: the modern kind, on the AR's frame, that reaches a little further and fires a little quicker, and
pays for it in a small mag and a higher price.

**The DMR**, $240, under RIFLES after the M14: a tan battery marksman rifle on the Spring Assault Rifle's frame, with a
long barrel in a slim float tube and a short 20-round mag.
- **4 a second** at most, a pull a shot (the M14 3, the SVD 2, the Electric Rifle 6).
- **A fast, straight BB**: 63 m/s (the M14 60, the SVD 70), straight for 20 m (the M14 18, the SVD 22), a tight cone
  (0.17; the M14 0.18, the AR 0.28), aimed 0.25.
- **Short mags**: 20 in its mag (the M14 30), a long mag of 40 for $20, spares at $10 (0.6 lb each).
- **Heavy**: 9.5 lb and a 0.85 walk, as the M14.

So against the M14 it is the better shooter by a little on every count but the mag, and it costs $40 more. Against the
SVD it fires twice as quick but its BB is slower and curls sooner.

**The model** (`buildFPDMR`, `52-fp-guns`) is built on the AR (`buildFPAR`), so its hands, its two rails, its
under-barrel slot, its aimed pose, its reticle family and its kid's gun mesh are the AR's, and every workbench part
already fitted to the AR fits it. The lower, grip and stock are tan; a slim tan float tube with four vent slots runs
round the barrel ahead of the handguard; the barrel goes 8 cm further out to the orange tip, so a muzzle part sits
further forward (the muzzle slot measures the gun's front); the mag is black and 0.7 as long, its top still in the
magwell; and a small battery box rides on the buffer tube's right.
