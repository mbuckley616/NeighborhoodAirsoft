## v1.469 — The Tennis-Ball Grip
The second of Michael's **A** on "What the play lane builds next, after the ally callouts" (decisions, 9 Nov): the
tennis-ball grip on the UNDER slot, steadier on the move, a little heavier. v1.468 was the first, the glow-stick tracer.

**What a player gets.** The **Tennis-Ball Grip**, $8, 0.3 lb, in the shop's Mods tab under UNDER: an old tennis ball
from the garage, slit along its seam and pushed onto a fold of duct tape under the handguard, a band of tape either side.
It goes on any long gun with an under-barrel slot, so it is a trade against the PVC foregrip, the bipod, the slings, the
taped mags and the taped flashlight.
- **Walking**, your shots stay about a quarter tighter (`moveSpreadMult` 0.75; the foregrip's is 0.65, at 0.8 lb).
- **Holding an automatic's trigger**, the aim climbs 0.85 as far (`climbMult`, as the sock-padded stock and the shoelace
  sling do): something to squeeze. The foregrip does nothing for the climb, so each has its reason.
- Standing still nothing changes.
- **In first person** your off hand closes on the ball, as it does on the foregrip and the taped mags (`updateFpHands`,
  52-fp-guns); the ball hangs 6 mm under the handguard at the rail's middle, where that hand goes.

Under it: `ATTACHMENTS.tennis_grip` (14-gear, last in the list), its model in `buildUnderParts` (54-fp-gear). Its effects
go through `underPartMult`, which `fireBB` and the climb already read, so nothing else changed.

  without; on the workbench it takes the AK's under slot in place of the foregrip and goes on no muzzle or pistol. On the
  AK in Bunratty, 300 seeded hip shots: walking cone 1.126° bare, 0.846° with the ball (0.75), 0.735° with the foregrip;
  standing 0.135° all three; the climb scaled 0.85 with the ball, 1 with the foregrip, and a 1.5 s held burst climbing
  0.019-0.020 rad with the ball against 0.022-0.024 bare over five runs (the climb's kick is not on the seeded random, so
  the test compares it loosely); 0.3 lb in the loadout weight. Green 2 runs of 2 in its final form.
- `tests/fp-hands.test.mjs`, extended: on the MP5 with the ball the off hand is on it (gap under 3 mm), the handguard
  hand hidden; the other hand checks unchanged. Also green: `homemade-parts`, `shop-gun-kinds`, `smoke`.
