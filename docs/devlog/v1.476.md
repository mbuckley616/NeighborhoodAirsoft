## v1.476 — Mitchell's AK knows how far it carries
The critic (13 Nov, Found in play; same on v1.472) stood through First Time in the Woods (`hollow_skirmish_3v3`): your
crew's Mitchell (an AK, team `player`) sat in `hiding` at about (2.5, 30.2), moved 0.0 m all minute, and fired 252-293
BBs at Seth's team 36-65 m off, landing 3-5. Every short BB came down 34-35 m out in the creek dip, with a clear line,
so it was the drop — and he never pushed in to where he could hit.

**What was wrong.** The AK is the game's "aggressive ranged" gun, and its engage table (80-ai) sets its FAR to 100 m — the
range past which a kid won't fire — against a BB that reaches only about 35 m from a kid's 1.05 m muzzle (a level shot
under the flight's gravity and hop-up, no curve). An ally who has had nobody in sight for his first 8 s goes looking
(v1.465, the early look, Michael B); the test for "nobody in sight" is `_inReachNow`, a line to a foe inside his gun's
FAR. With the AK's FAR at 100, Mitchell read the other side 42-65 m off as "in reach", so he never counted himself as
having nobody to shoot, never took the early look, and held his spot emptying mags into the dip.

**What changed.**
- `kidGunReach(weapon)` computes what a gun carries from a kid's muzzle: a level shot integrated under the same gravity
  and hop-up lift the BBs fly with (78-bb-flight), to where it meets the ground. It is memoised per weapon (AK ≈ 35 m).
- For the AK, `_inReachNow` is now bounded by that carry, not by its 100 m FAR: a foe past ~35 m is seen but read as out
  of reach. So when Seth's team is 42-65 m off, Mitchell finds nobody there he can hit and takes the early look at 8 s,
  leaving his spot to close the distance — exactly the behaviour Michael approved for Owen's pistol at The Portables.
- Only the AK is changed. Every other gun keeps its tuned FAR, which sits near its carry and is a deliberate engagement
  range (the pistol peppers to 30 m though its BBs land at ~19, and Owen's early look reads that FAR unchanged). The AK
  was the one gun whose FAR was set as an aspiration far past any kid-muzzle carry.

### Verified
- `tests/idle-ally.test.mjs`, one new case (two checks). First Time in the Woods, one foe pinned 42 m out with a clear
  line (beyond the AK's 34.7 m carry), the rest sent far off, the player untaggable: Mitchell sees the foe (`_hasLOSNow`)
  but reads him out of reach (`_inReachNow` false), takes the early look at 7.8 s and hands off to advancing. On v1.475's
  code `_inReachNow` was true, so the look never fired and he sat. The checks read the look firing and the handoff, not
  how far he travels — his advance crosses the creek dip, whose ground slows it by an amount that is not deterministic
  (0-24 m over 20 s in sampling), so a distance bound would be flaky (CI caught it: 0.4 m one run, 12.8 m another).
- The suite's existing checks unchanged and green: Owen on his pistol still takes the early look at 7.8-7.9 s at The
  Portables (pistol FAR untouched); the 25-30 s idle clock, the walkie callouts, snipers and VIPs hold.
- `last-kid`, `crew-balance`, `ai-clock`, `smoke` green. The change feeds only the ally early look (`_inReachNow` has one
  reader); enemy engagement, suppression, the flank bounds and first-shot distances on the maps read ENGAGE_FAR, untouched.

### Still open
- The same FAR-past-carry gap is in the other guns set well above their carry — the AR (60 vs ~28), the UMP and MP5 (50
  vs ~31), the sniper (200 vs ~47) — but there it bites less: the sniper is left out of the looking system by design (it
  holds), and the SMG/AR gaps are smaller. Whether their firing ceilings should also come down to what the BBs reach is a
  tuning call for a playtest, not built here.
- A cautious AK ally who did see a reachable foe in his first 8 s still spends that window's fire before the 25-30 s idle
  clock can send him on; only a real playtest can say whether the push now reads right in a live match.
