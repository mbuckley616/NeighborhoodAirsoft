## v1.414 — The Uzi, a burst SMG (gun batch 6, D.63); 39 guns
Michael chose what the play lane builds next on 1 Nov: **A**, gun batch 6, to 40: six more in the neighborhood-kid
tone, one a version, "say a revolver, a bullpup, a lever-action, a light machine gun, a burst SMG, a pump sniper".
The Magnum, the KSG, the Lever Gun and the M60 (v1.410-v1.413) came first; this is the burst SMG.

The only burst gun was the Burst Rifle (an electric M16, three BBs 1/12 s apart). The boxy little SMG with its mag up
the grip was in every action movie and every toy aisle, so **the Uzi**, $180, under AUTOMATICS straight after the
MAC-10 (before the AK-47), is an electric one with the switch set to burst:
- **Three BBs a pull, almost at once:** 1/20 s apart (the Burst Rifle's 1/12), then the next pull. Pulled as fast as a
  finger goes it throws about 7.5 BBs a second, so the MAC-10 (18) and the MP5 (13) out-hose it; what it has is the
  burst landing together.
- **Tighter than the hosers:** 0.30 from the hip (the MP5 0.34, the MAC-10 0.5), 0.38 of it aimed; a BB between
  theirs (44 m/s, the MAC-10 38, the MP5 50), straight for 12 m (7, 14).
- **Quick:** a 1.1 walk (the MP5 1.05, the MAC-10 1.15), 4 lb (3, 5). Sixty in the stick mag, a long mag 120 for $16,
  spares 60 for $9; run dry, a new mag is racked in (0.55 s).
- Its BBs press a kid as an SMG's (2), not a pistol's: it is "Medium", not light.

The first-person model is the MAC-10's box, grip and mag, top knob, trigger and sights (so its hands and aimed picture
are the MAC-10's): the box runs 6 cm further forward with a ribbed top cover kept under the sight line, a plastic
handguard under the front, the barrel out through a big barrel nut to the orange tip, a grip safety on the grip's
back, and the MAC-10's wire stock swapped for the Uzi's metal stock folded along the right side. It takes a sight and
one rail, as the MAC-10 does. A kid holding one has a blocky Uzi of its own (the long box, the barrel nut, the mag up
the grip, the folded stock), but no kid carries one. The game has 39 guns.
