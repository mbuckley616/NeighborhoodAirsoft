## v1.416 — The Pump Sniper, a spring sniper racked by a pump (gun batch 6, D.63); 40 guns
Michael chose what the play lane builds next on 1 Nov: **A**, gun batch 6, to 40: six more in the neighborhood-kid
tone, one a version, "say a revolver, a bullpup, a lever-action, a light machine gun, a burst SMG, a pump sniper".
The Magnum, the KSG, the Lever Gun, the M60 and the Uzi (v1.410-v1.414) came first; this is the pump sniper, the
batch's last, and the shop's fortieth gun.

The shop's snipers were the Spring Sniper Rifle and the L96 (bolts) and the SVD (battery). The cheap long gun in the
sporting-goods aisle, the one with a pump under the barrel where the bolt would be, is **the Pump Sniper**, $110,
under RIFLES straight after the Spring Sniper Rifle (before the L96):
- **A quicker next shot:** rack the pump for every shot, 0.75 s (the sniper's bolt 1.1, the L96's 1.3, the KSG's pump
  0.7). From one shot to the next ready is about 1.1 s; a bolt's is about 1.4.
- **For a little less reach:** a 68 m/s BB straight for 20 m (the sniper 75 and 25, the L96 85 and 30, the Lever Gun 55
  and 14), a touch wider (0.14 from the hip, the sniper 0.12), a quarter of it aimed as the sniper's.
- **Lighter:** 8 lb and a 0.8 walk (the sniper 10 lb and 0.75), "Heavy, slower" on its page where the bolt guns are
  "Very heavy". Twenty in the mag, a long mag 30 for $16, spares 20 for $8.

The first-person model is the Spring Sniper Rifle's stock, barrel and sights (so its aimed picture is the sniper's):
the bolt and the folded bipod come off, the stock is black plastic, the forend is cut back to 12 cm, a tube runs under
the barrel to a band at the muzzle end, a short box mag sits under the receiver, and a ribbed olive pump rides on the
tube where the support hand sits. Racking it slides the pump back 4.8 cm with the hand on it (the shotgun's way: the
pump is the cocking part), stopping 2.5 mm short of the forend; the cock bar says PUMP FORESTOCK. It takes a sight, two
rails and the under-barrel slot, as the sniper does, the rails and the slot on the short forend behind the pump (a
first try left the rails where the sniper's long forend had been, 5.5 mm off anything; `gun-parts` caught it). A kid holding one has a blocky one of its own (the long barrel, the
green pump, the black stock, the mag), but no kid carries one. The game has 40 guns, and batch 6 is done.
