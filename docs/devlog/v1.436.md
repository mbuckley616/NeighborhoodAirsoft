## v1.436 — The ammo panel says HOLD LMB TO RACK after a spring gun's shot
The critic (4 Nov) found the ammo panel's status line reading READY · CLICK TO FIRE all through the rack or break-open
on every spring gun (the Spring Pistol, the Spring Shotgun, the Double-Barrel and the Derringer measured), while the cock
bar above it said RACK or BREAK OPEN · LOAD, so HOLD LMB TO RACK never showed. A shot runs `doFire`, which draws the
panel, and only then does `onLmbDown` (`js/70-input.js`) un-cock the gun; nothing drew the panel again until the rack
finished. Now `onLmbDown` draws it once more after a shot has decided whether the gun stays cocked: an automatic or an
electric semi still reads READY, a double-barrel with a barrel left still reads READY, and a spring gun reads HOLD LMB
TO RACK until it is racked.
### Verified
- `gun-derringer` gains a check: over four pairs of shots, READY after the first barrel, HOLD LMB TO RACK after the
  second and a third of a second into loading. Without the fix it fails (READY after both barrels and while loading).
- `gun-pistol`, `gun-shotgun`, `gun-double`, `gun-electric`, `gun-uzi`, `gun-ar` and `smoke` green.
