## v1.399 — The Gas Pistol, a blowback pistol whose gas cools (gun batch 5, D.63); 32 guns
Michael chose what the play lane builds next on 31 Oct: **C**, per-kid excuses (v1.397), then gun batch 5 toward 40,
"say a second pump shotgun, a gas pistol, an SMG, a DMR". The SPAS-12 was the first (v1.398); this is the second.

The pistols were the Spring Pistol (rack the slide every shot), the Six-Shooter, the Bolt Pistol and the Electric
Pistol (a BB a pull, 5 a second). **The Gas Pistol**, $130, last under PISTOLS, shoots like the Electric Pistol, a BB
every pull with nothing to rack, and gas is what sets it apart:
- **The fastest, tightest BB of the pistols:** 40 m/s (the Electric Pistol 34), straight for 6 m (4), aimed spread
  0.32 (0.4).
- **The gas cools as the mag empties:** a full mag's first BB leaves at full speed, the last at 80% of it (`gasFade`
  0.2, read in `fireBB` from how much of the mag is gone). A fresh mag is full speed again, so it pays to change mags
  before the last few.
- **What it costs:** a smaller mag (15, the Electric Pistol 18; a 25-round long mag for $16, a spare for $9), a
  slower cycle (4.5 a second, 5), and a steel slide and gas mag are heavier (3 lb, 2.5), so a slightly slower walk
  (1.1, 1.12).

The first-person model is the spring pistol's frame, slide, sights and grip with a bare steel slide on a black frame,
wooden panels with screws on both sides of the grip, a hammer behind the slide and the gas mag's brass fill valve under
the grip. Every shot the gas throws the slide back 5 cm and it is home again in about 0.12 s (`userData.blowback`:
`updateHeldMesh` moves the cocking parts with the shot's kick). The shop's line for it reads "Gas blowback (a BB a
pull, up to 4.5/s)". A kid holding one holds it as a pistol, but no kid carries one. It takes a sight and one rail, as
the pistols do. The game has 32 guns.
