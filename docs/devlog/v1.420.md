## v1.420 — A MUZZLE slot on every long gun, and the Soda-Bottle Silencer (D.83)
Michael answered where the rest of the second homemade wave goes on 2 Nov: **A, two new slots on the long guns, MUZZLE
and STOCK**. The Cardboard Barrel moves to MUZZLE beside the soda-bottle silencer; the sock-padded stock goes in STOCK;
the taped-up longer mag goes under the barrel; old saves keep the cardboard on the gun it was on. This version is the
muzzle half: the slot, the cardboard's move, and the silencer.

**The MUZZLE slot.** Every long gun has one: the 24 with an under-barrel slot, and the P90, the Vector, the AUG, the
Thompson and the Tick-Tock, whose own front grips fill their under-barrel spot but whose muzzles are free (29 guns). The
pistols, the MAC-10, the Uzi and the one-rail clear automatics have none. At the workbench it is a **Muzzle** row under
the Under-barrel row, with its label floating under the handguard; the gun list reads "1 sight · 2 rails · under ·
muzzle"; the shop's Accessory Slots line counts it; the Mods tab tags its parts MUZZLE. A long gun now carries a muzzle
part and an under part at once (a foregrip and a silencer, say), which it couldn't before. The slot code takes a list of
the homemade slots (`HOMEMADE_SLOTS`, `js/14-gear.js`) instead of naming the under-barrel slot, so STOCK, next, is a
capacity and its parts, and their numbers (`underPartMult`) multiply across all of them.

**The Cardboard Barrel** is the same part with the same numbers, now on MUZZLE. A save from before keeps it: loading
moves a cardboard barrel found on an under-barrel slot to that gun's muzzle (`ensureGunSlots`, run over every gun at
load).

**The Soda-Bottle Silencer**, $9: a green two-liter bottle with a red label band, its neck back over the muzzle under a
band of duct tape, a grey sock showing through the plastic, the red cap holed at the far end. Kids don't listen for your
shots yet, so what it does is for you:
- **Your shots are quieter:** your own shot plays at 0.4 of its loudness (`shotVolumeMult`; the kids' shots unchanged).
- **The BBs leave a touch slower:** 6 % (`velocityMult` 0.94; the cardboard's 0.85), so a little more drop past 20 m.
- Nothing on the spread, no weight.
