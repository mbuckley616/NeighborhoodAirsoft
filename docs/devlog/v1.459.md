## v1.459 — The title: Campaign, Online and Settings
Card `fable/d2-room-list`, its first step: backlog D.2 step 3 (Michael, 28 Sep: "Startup offers Campaign (the current game) and Online Multiplayer, and maybe a third for Options/Settings"; his answer of 28 Oct, B: the title and a room list, after co-op). Until now the title page had CONTINUE, NEW GAME, SETTINGS and STORY, and online play could only be found by walking to the Pickup Game board in your room and pressing PLAY ONLINE.

**What a player sees.** The title's notebook page now has three buttons: **CAMPAIGN**, **ONLINE** and **SETTINGS**. CAMPAIGN turns to the page the title used to be (CONTINUE when there is a save, NEW GAME or ENTER MIKE'S ROOM, STORY) with a **◀ BACK** under it; nothing on that page has changed, and NEW GAME over a save still asks first. SETTINGS opens the settings over the title as before.

**ONLINE** goes straight to the online screen, titled "Online", over your room. If you have a saved summer it loads first, exactly as CONTINUE loads it, because online play borrows your look, your gun and the maps you have opened; the screen's way back reads **◀ TO YOUR ROOM**. With no save you go online as a new kid and nothing is saved: the way back (and CLOSE) reads **◀ BACK TO THE TITLE** and takes you there, and BACK TO YOUR ROOM after an online match does the same. The screen is the Pickup Game's online panel (your name, HOST A ROOM, JOIN by code) with a new **Co-op** row: **HAVE A FRIEND OVER ▶** opens the phone's co-op screen (have a friend over, or go over to theirs). Co-op needs a summer that saves (the money your friend's match pays goes into it), so with no save the row says "Having a friend over needs a summer of your own: start one from CAMPAIGN on the title." The same row is on the panel when it is opened from the board.

**Why this shape.** The online screen already lived on the board and needs a scene to come back to between matches (a match ends in your room, and a rematch reopens the board's panel there), so the title enters your room and opens it rather than drawing a second online screen on the title. Starting a summer from ONLINE without asking would skip the mirror, the story cards and the room tour, so a new kid online saves nothing instead, and CAMPAIGN is still how a summer begins.

Under it: `showTitlePage` and `showTitleScreen` (84-main) switch the title's two pages and bring the title back from the room; `openOnlineFromTitle` and `Net.fromTitle` (94-pickup-online) carry where the screen came from, and `closeModal` (66-menus-shop) and the result card's BACK TO YOUR ROOM (98-boot) read it. The buttons keep their old ids, so `#startBtn` and `#continueBtn` still start and load a game.
