## v1.430 — Crew jerseys in place of the bandanas (D.84)
Michael asked on 2 Nov whether the crews should wear soccer or football jerseys with a number and the kid's name on the
back, since a bandana can read as gang-adjacent, most of all in public places like the parking lot, and a jersey makes
it feel like a game and is easier to tell apart. He chose **A** on 3 Nov: jerseys for every crew, the bandanas go.

**The jersey** (`buildKidJersey`, `js/50-kids.js`). A kid in a crew wears its jersey over whatever he has on. It is
loose and untucked over the shirt and belt. The sleeves are in the crew colour with a band at each cuff, and there is a
V at the neck and a band at the hem. On the back is the kid's name over a big number, and the small number is on the
left chest. The print is white, or navy on a pale jersey (Mitchell's off-white, the yellow crew). A tee or the hoodie
stays under it. A hat still goes on over it, and that includes the VIP's cap: a VIP now wears his crew's jersey under
his cap. Before, he lost the bandana to the cap.
- **Who wears what is unchanged from the bandana (v1.200, v1.401).** Your crew wears your colour (you and every ally
  in a match), each captain's crew its captain's, and Seth's crew his red. Kids in no crew wear their own clothes as
  before: the street's own kid in a 1v1, the Pickup Game's kids, the tutorial's. Seth never wears yours. No crew colour
  at the mirror means no jerseys on your side, as no colour meant no bandanas.
- **Numbers.** Each kid keeps one number, 1-99, all summer (`kidJerseyNumber`): it comes from his id, and no two kids
  share one. Eric is 33, Seth 50, Trey 10. You pick yours at the mirror under YOUR CREW in a new row, "Your jersey":
  your number (0-99, 10 to start) and the name on your back (letters, capitals, 10 at most; left blank, your back shows
  only the number). An ally whose number is yours wears the next one. Both are saved in your look, so a friend online
  or in co-op sees your jersey as you set it.
- **The briefing's lineup** shows both sides' crews in their jerseys (`drawBriefingLineup`, `js/24-scenarios.js`).
- **The captains' trophies.** Beating a captain's finale used to pin his crew's bandana over the Pickup board. Now it
  pins a small jersey with his number (`js/30-bedroom.js`, a small edit in the maps lane's file), Tyler's still with its
  stripe. The result card's line reads "JERSEY Marcus's crew's jersey goes on your wall." The Red and Blue Bandanas
  sold in the Clothes tab are a hat you choose to buy, not the crew's mark, so they stay.
