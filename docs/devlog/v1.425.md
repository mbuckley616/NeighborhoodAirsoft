## v1.425 — A STOCK slot on every long gun, and the Sock-Padded Stock (D.83)
The stock half of what Michael answered on 2 Nov for the rest of the second homemade wave (**A, two new slots on the
long guns, MUZZLE and STOCK**; the muzzle half was v1.420). The sock-padded stock's effect was its own question, since no
gun climbs today and "kicks less" had nothing to act on. Michael chose **A, steadier when aimed**, and added "maybe A as
well as faster movement while aiming down sight?". It does both.

**The STOCK slot.** The same 29 long guns as the muzzle slot (the 24 with an under-barrel slot, and the P90, the Vector,
the AUG, the Thompson and the Tick-Tock). The pistols, the MAC-10, the Uzi and the one-rail clear automatics have none.
v1.420 had already laid the slot's plumbing (`HOMEMADE_SLOTS`, the workbench row, the gun list's "· stock", the shop's
slot line, the Mods tab's STOCK tag), so opening it only needed its capacity set (`getGunAccessoryCap`, `js/14-gear.js`).
At the workbench the **Stock** row sits under the Muzzle row, and its label floats under the butt.

**The Sock-Padded Stock**, $8: a white tube sock with a red and a blue stripe, stuffed and pulled over the butt, its toe
bulging off the back and a band of duct tape at the cuff. It is a homemade part in D.14's tone, so it is a trade:
- **Aimed, the gun sits steadier:** with the gun all the way up, the whole cone is a fifth tighter (`adsSteadyMult` 0.8,
  `fireBB`, `js/76-bbs.js`). That covers the gun's own spread and the walking cone, eased in with the aim. From the hip,
  no change.
- **Aimed, you walk quicker:** with the gun up you normally move at 0.70 of your speed; with the sock, 0.84
  (`adsWalkMult` 1.2 on `ADS_MOVE_MULT`, capped at full speed, `js/72-player.js`).
- **It's 0.4 lb more to carry**, in the loadout weight like the taped mag and the foregrip. Michael's answer named no
  cost. The weight is the builder's, kept small so the part is still a trade. Say if it should go.

The model is fitted to each gun: `measureGunForStock` (`js/54-fp-gear.js`) reads the bare gun's rearmost point on the
centre line and the box round everything in the last 5 cm before it, and the sock is built round that box. So it fits
the AR's thin buttstock, the FAMAS's tall bullpup butt and the Thompson's wooden one alike.
### Verified
- `homemade-parts` gains the stock:
  - The sock is in the Mods tab as STOCK with its picture, bought for $8.
  - At the workbench, a Stock row on the AR and the P90 and none on the pistol or the Uzi. It offers only the sock, two
    clicks mount it, it shows on the gun, and its label reads "Stock: Sock-Padded Stock". It can't go on a muzzle or a
    pistol.
  - On the 29 long guns the sock wraps the butt 5-7 mm proud on every side, with its toe 1.9 cm past the end, 7.6 cm
    long, 4.4-6.0 cm wide and 6-17 cm tall (the FAMAS is the tallest). The other 11 guns have no slot.
  - In a match on the AR, the sock taken off and put back on the same seeded shots:
    - On the gun in hand, 0.4 lb in the weight.
    - Aimed, standing, the median cone is 0.040° (0.050° bare, ×0.80). Aimed at a walking sway it is 0.357° (0.447°,
      ×0.80). From the hip, 0.144° both ways.
    - Walking 2 s with the gun up covers 5.12 m (4.26 m bare, ×1.20).
  - Two passes green.
  - The suite's three passes over all 40 guns in one page ran it out of memory twice. The sock is measured on the
    muzzle check's gun builds instead of on a third pass of its own.
- `under-parts`, `shop-pics`, `shop-tabs` and `smoke` pass. parsecheck: all 38 files and their concatenation OK.
