## v1.441 — The Taped-On Flashlight (D.86, the third homemade wave's first part)
Michael chose **A** on "What the play lane builds next, after gun batch 7" (control room, 4 Nov): a third wave of homemade
parts, starting with a taped-on flashlight for the night matches. It is filed as backlog D.86, and this is its first part.

**The Taped-On Flashlight**, $7, 0.5 lb, in the UNDER slot of every long gun that has one (pistols, the P90, the Vector,
the AUG and the other guns with no under-barrel slot can't take it). It is a yellow plastic torch from the kitchen drawer
with a black head, duct-taped under the barrel. It is a trade against the Tactical Flashlight ($18, a side rail):
- It leaves both rails free for a laser and a light, and costs under half as much.
- It is warmer and dimmer, and it doesn't reach as far: a spotlight of 2.2 out to 9 m, against the Tactical's 3.4 out to
  14 m, with a fainter beam.
- The batteries rattle. Each shot has a one-in-four chance (`blinkChance`) of knocking the light out for 0.2 s, beam and
  all, after which it comes back. On an automatic the light stutters while you hold the trigger.

**Where it sits.** Under the handguard it would sit in the off hand on almost every gun (a first measure found the hand
inside it on 24 of 26). So the light hangs just ahead of the off hand, or on a pump gun ahead of the pump the hand works.
That point is measured once for each kind of gun, with the hands on (`fpOffHandFrontZ`). The light's top is set just
under the lowest point of the gun along its length, and each of the two tape bands reaches up to whatever is above it
(`gunUndersideOver`). On a gun with a tube under the barrel, like the Thunder Pump, the tape runs up past the tube to
the barrel. On the short SMGs (the MP5 and the UMP) the head sticks 5-7 cm past the muzzle, as a real one would. In the
shop picture the beam is hidden and the frame is fitted to the torch.

The blink happens in `doFire` (`js/74-gun-hud.js`), which draws from `Math.random`, not the seeded game random, so
nothing seeded moves. `updateGun` runs the timer and turns the light and its beam off and on.
### Verified
- `homemade-light`, a new suite:
  - In the Mods tab as UNDER with its picture, bought for $7.
  - On the AR, the UMP, the Thunder Pump and the M60, with the hands on: no overlap with the off hand, 6 mm ahead of it,
    centred, 11.8 cm long. The tape reaches the gun on all four and the torch cuts into none of them. The Uzi has no slot.
  - In a night match (After Dark, Winnmark) on the AR: it shows on the gun in hand, in the scene, lit at 2.2 out to 9 m,
    with no Tactical Flashlight. With the Tactical on a rail instead: 3.4 out to 14 m and no taped light. 0.5 lb in the
    loadout weight.
  - A shot with the dice at 0.9 leaves it lit for all 20 frames after. One at 0.1 puts it out for 12 frames (0.2 s), then
    back at 2.2. Over 400 seeded shots it blinked 100 times. With the Tactical, 0 blinks.
- `under-parts`, `workbench-labels`, `shop-pics`, `shop-tabs`, `sell-back`, `fp-hands`, `fp-hands-late`, `homemade-parts` and `smoke` are green.
