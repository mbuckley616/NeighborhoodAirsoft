## v1.487 — Wind, deep snow and footprints (fall and winter, step 3)
Step 3 of Michael's **B** ("A, and the weather changes play"): the weather's rules, in the fall and winter matches only.
The summer plays exactly as before. Snow forts that can be knocked down are their own next version.

**Fall days are gusty** (63-seasons; one line in `updateBBs`, 78-bb-flight). The wind pushes every BB in the air one
way, both sides' alike. It starts where the BB's own curve starts (its gun's `curveOnsetDistance`, 4 m for most guns),
so a shot inside about 10 m goes where it was aimed and a long one drifts. That is the wobble the game already has,
pushed one way, which keeps the brief's "reliable inside ~10 m". The day's wind comes from the match id, so its
direction and strength (2.5 to 4.5 m/s², plus gusts of up to 35% over the round) are the same each time you play that
match. The falling leaves blow the same way, which is how you read it. Fall nights (Halloween Night, Under the Stadium
Lights) are calm. I first tried 3.5 to 6 m/s², which pushed a spring-pistol shot 1.8 m off at 30 m; that read as too
much, so I lowered it.

**Deep snow** (72-player, 80-ai). In winter, you and every kid move at 85%: walking, sprinting, deploying, retreating
and the zombie runs alike. That's one `Season.moveMult`, which is 1 outside winter.

**Footprints** (63-seasons, 80-ai). In winter, you and every kid leave a print every 0.8 m, left and right of your
line. They're drawn as one instanced mesh of small dark ovals, up to 600, that fill back in to snow over 30 s. They're
never solid. A kid without a line to his target checks twice a second whether he is within 4 m of that target's trail
(prints younger than 30 s). If he is, he knows where its newest print is, as if he had seen the target there then. He
plays it exactly as he plays a glow-stick tracer (v1.468): covering fire at that spot while it's fresh, then his
last-known spot. So in the snow you can be tracked, and a kid can be too.

**What the player is told.** The map cards carry 🍂 WINDY on a fall day, ❄ DEEP SNOW in winter and 🎃 HALLOWEEN on
Halloween Night. The briefing's place line adds "🍂 FALL · WINDY: LONG SHOTS DRIFT" or "❄ WINTER · DEEP SNOW: EVERYONE
SLOWER, AND YOU LEAVE TRACKS".

### Verified
- New suite `tests/seasons-weather.test.mjs` (8 checks, green twice). It is split from `tests/seasons.test.mjs`, which
  grew past the runner's 10-minute limit with these checks in it. It enters three matches: Short a Player, First
  Saturday Back and Cold Hands. It checks the night calm and the repeatable wind through `seasonWeather` alone, without
  entering a match. `seasons` is green beside it.
- Leaving a winter match for the bedroom now resets the weather too (`enterBedroom` calls `seasonWeather(null)`), so no
  later match can start at 85% or in the wind.
- Wind: a 30 m/s BB fired across the wind high over the map. In the summer it goes straight (0 m). On First Saturday
  Back it is 6.8 cm off at 10 m and 1.32 m at 30 m (3.64 m/s²). The same match gets the same wind each time, and
  another match gets another. A fall night, the summer and winter have none.
- Deep snow: one second of walking forward from the start covers 4.14 m on Short a Player and 3.52 m on Cold Hands,
  85.0%.
- Prints: after 5 s of Cold Hands there were 268 to 294 prints from 15 walkers (4 of them yours), all drawn, none solid. Each
  foe kid then got a fresh trail laid from his feet to his own target. `seasonTrailFor` gives the newest print (where the
  target stands) and nothing to a kid 400 m away. After half a second, 1 to 3 of the 8 foes were following their trail. Each had
  his last-known spot where the trail ended, or where his target had walked on to since. The rest had a line and didn't
  need it.
- The cards and the briefing show the tags above, and a summer card shows none of them.
- Green: `smoke`, `bb-sweep`, `gait`, `deploy-progress` and `retreat-progress`. In the summer the new multiplier is 1
  and there's no wind. `last-kid`: see the PR comment. Parsecheck is clean.
