## v1.485 — The seasons' look (fall and winter, step 1)
Michael answered **B** on "Fall and winter campaigns: what shape" (control room, 21 Nov): two new chapters after the
summer, Fall 2003 and Winter 2003-04, on the summer's maps, and the weather changes play. This first step is the look
the two chapters will be played in. No match uses it yet, so nothing a player sees changes in this version. The chapters,
their matches and the map screen's season tab come next, and then the weather's rules.

**How a season is laid on a map** (new file `js/63-seasons.js`, maps lane). Every map is built from its own colours,
about 750 of them across the map files, so a season can't be a palette swap. A match now names its season
(`sc.season`: 'fall' or 'winter'; no season means the summer). Once the map is built, `applySeasonLook` passes over it
and changes only the summer's greens. It also changes bare dirt on the ground in winter. Paint, brick, asphalt,
cardboard and cars keep their colours.
- **Fall**: lawns go to straw with a little green left. Broadleaf trees go orange, red-orange, gold or brown, one colour
  per tree, and about one tree in seven stays green. Pines keep their needles. The sky goes hazy, the sun warm and a
  touch dimmer, and the fog comes 12% closer. About 260 leaves drift and swirl down round you.
- **Winter**: the ground is snow. Trees are frosted pale or a dark evergreen, about half each, and pines are dark or
  frosted. The sky is overcast white, the light cool and the fog 28% closer. Night is a lighter navy, with snow throwing
  the light back. About 900 snowflakes fall round you.
- What counts as ground: a mesh that is low and flat, a plane, or the map's big ground. The big vertex-coloured meshes
  (the ground and the scenery past the edge) are judged face by face, so an upward face is ground. A pine's crown is
  marked by `_buildPineDetail`, and cones count as pines.

The pass recolours copies of the materials and vertex colours it changes, never the shared ones the house and tree kits
cache, so the next summer match is exactly as before. Cars and bins are marked `noSeason` (32-street-kit) and keep their
paint. The pass draws no game random numbers, so a map's cover is laid out the same in every season, and it adds nothing
solid. The leaves and snow are one Points mesh (one draw call). `seasonStep`, called from `stepGame` in a match, moves
them and wraps them round the camera as you walk, so there are always some in view. `Season.force` sets a season for the
tests.

### Verified
- New suite `tests/seasons.test.mjs` on Bunratty (Sean's 1v1), 9 checks green. In the summer there is no pass and no
  particles. Entering winter and then the summer again gives the same materials colour for colour (12,102), with the same
  sky and fog. In fall, green materials drop from 216 to 32, there are 260 leaves and the sky changes. In winter, 99% of
  the ground's upward faces are light (59.6% in summer, counting pavements and roofs), there are 900 flakes and the fog's
  far plane goes 400 → 288. All three seasons have the same 329 obstacles at the same places, and cars keep their paint.
  The snow falls about 1 m a second, and after the camera moves 40 m all 900 flakes are still within 16 m of it. No page
  errors.
- Screenshots in all three seasons of Bunratty, Winnmark (day and night), the Hollow and the market lot: the season
  switched each time, with no page errors. The first fall picture turned a green bin lid orange and a green car tan, and
  the Hollow's pines orange; all three are fixed (marked cars and bins, marked pine crowns).
- `smoke` green; parsecheck clean, now 40 files (63-seasons added to `CUTS` and the tags).
