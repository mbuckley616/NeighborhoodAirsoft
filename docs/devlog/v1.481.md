## v1.481 — Nick's patience runs out
Michael answered **B** on "Nick Won't Budge: a player who sits" (control room, 19 Nov). That was the question v1.480 left.
In Nick Won't Budge (`bunratty_nick`) Nick holds his backyard behind house 2 as a defender. The `lane_west` start is
43 m away and out of his sight, and his AR carries about 28 m from a kid's muzzle. A player who sat at the start drew
nothing (0 BBs in 600 s, the critic) and the match never ended. Michael chose: after about 40 s with no shot either
way, Nick walks out to the first cover from which his rifle reaches you and holds there, and the card goes back to
"you can't just sit".

**What changed.**
- `patience` on an enemySetup entry (copied in `startScenario`, 64-scenario-flow). Only Nick has it: `patience: 40`.
- `updateKidPatience` (80-ai) runs once a frame beside the boredom clock. It counts seconds while the kid has no line
  to a foe and no BB has passed near him in the last second. A line or a near miss resets it. Tagged or walking home,
  it resets and drops any walk. It does not run during the opening hold or while he is deploying.
- When the clock passes his patience, `kidPatienceSpot` finds his walk-out spot. It fills the kids' route grid
  (`kidFieldTo`) from where he stands. For each piece of the match's cover, it takes the stand-behind spot facing his
  nearest foe (`coverStandPos`). It keeps the ones 6 m or more from the foe and inside the gun's carry less 4 m (about
  24 m for the AR, so his BBs land instead of dropping at the edge). Of those with a line to the foe, it picks the one
  nearest by the walk. He taunts and sets off in `deploying`. On this walk he steps by the route grid (round the houses,
  not straight at the spot). He doesn't stop when he first gets a line, as a deploying kid does: he goes on to the cover.
  If you shoot at him on the way, he takes the fight where he stands.
- On arrival (or if he wedges on the way) that spot becomes his anchor. From there he plays the defender as before:
  peeks and fires, moves at most 5 m from his spot, and never chases you through the houses. If you move out of his
  reach and sit again, the clock runs again.
- The card: "He won't chase you through the houses, but he won't wait forever either: sit tight and he walks out to
  where that rifle reaches you, so you can't just sit. Get round the houses and flank him."
- 80-ai is the play lane's file. The edit is a small, separate function plus three gated lines in `deploying`. A kid
  without `patience` deploys exactly as before.
