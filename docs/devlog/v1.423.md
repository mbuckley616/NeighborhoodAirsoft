## v1.423 — Real shopping carts at Riverside Market (B.21)
Michael, on the control room (2 Nov): Riverside Market's shopping carts look wonky, "no wheels, and there isn't that
metal 'mesh' that covers the framing of the cart." He was right on both counts: a cart was a grey box drawn in
wireframe (its triangle edges, not a mesh), a flat plate under it and a red bar, standing on nothing. The store had its
own copy of the same box with four thin discs for wheels.

Both now use one cart, `makeShoppingCart` in the street kit (`js/32-street-kit.js`), built as the real ones are:
- **A wire-mesh basket**, 56 cm across at the handle and 48 at the nose, its floor sloping up toward the front, with a
  swing gate at the back. The mesh is a 4.5 cm wire grid on every panel (a small texture cut out by its alpha), so it
  reads as wire close up and as a grey sheen far off.
- **A tube frame**: the rim, the corner posts, the floor's edge, the handle's arms and the red plastic grip at 1.02 m.
- **The chassis**: two side rails on four casters (an 11 cm wheel in a fork at each corner), the back legs and front
  legs up to the basket, and the wire bottom rack between the rails.
- Three meshes a cart (wire, steel, rubber and plastic), so the lot's 15 and the store's 10 cost what the old ones did.

The lot's corrals (four carts each), the walk's corral and the three strays use it, and the store's row of eight by the
doors nests them 30 cm apart with only the last one's gate down. Collision and cover are unchanged: every cart, corral
and the store's row keeps the box it had.
