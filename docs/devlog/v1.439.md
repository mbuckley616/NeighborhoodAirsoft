## v1.439 — The Drum Shotgun, a battery shotgun on full auto (gun batch 7, D.85); 45 guns
The fifth gun of batch 7 (Michael's **A** of 4 Nov: six more to 46, the kinds the builder's pick from what the shop is
thin on). With the Auto Pistol in, shotguns were the thinnest kind (six), and all six fired a cloud a pull. This one
holds the trigger down.

**The Drum Shotgun**, $210, last under SHOTGUNS: a black battery shotgun fed from a fat drum under it.
- **Five clouds a second, held.** Full auto at 300 a minute, each shot the spring shotgun's cloud of two or three BBs,
  so the 100-BB drum is gone in about eight seconds; a charging handle on each new drum.
- **The trade.** A slower BB than any shotgun but the Thunder Pump (28 m/s; the Spring Shotgun 30) in the shotgun's
  cone, and the heaviest shotgun (9 lb; the Semi-Auto Shotgun 7) with the slowest walk of them (0.9).
- **Drums.** A big drum of 160 ($22) and a spare 100 ($15), both on its page. It takes a sight, two rails, a muzzle and
  a stock part; the drum fills the under-barrel, so no under slot.

The first-person model is the Semi-Auto Shotgun's receiver, sights, grip and charging handle in black, its side saddle
taken off, and a drum under the receiver ahead of the grip: a wind-up key on its left face and a clear window on the
right with BBs behind it. A kid holding one (a loan to your crew) has the shotgun's block with the drum under it.
