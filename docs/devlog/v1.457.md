## v1.457 — The balance pass, step 5: the L96's bolt is quicker (D.88)
v1.456's long duel found the L96 ($190), the dearest bolt sniper, losing to the cheaper ones: 7 of 20 against Trey where
the Pump Sniper ($110) won 16. Its rounds read as a first BB that misses, then the bolt while Trey's next shot comes.
Its Still open said to tune it next, one change, 20 rounds before and after in all three matches.

**The change:** the L96's bolt takes 0.9 s, was 1.3 s (`GUN_SPECS.l96.cockTime`, 14-gear). That is quicker than the
Spring Sniper Rifle's 1.1 s and still slower than the Pump Sniper's 0.75 s rack. Its BB is unchanged (85 m/s, straight
for 30 m, the tightest of the rifles), so the dear sniper now buys the best shot and a fair follow-up, and still holds
ten. The shop's line said the bolt was "stiffer to work"; it now says it works smoother.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.3 s bolt (before) | 1/20 | 12/20 | 4/20 | 17/60 | 6.27 | 3 |

| 0.9 s, 40 more duel rounds | - | - | 25/40 | - | 2.30 | 2 |

- **The duel, the fight it is bought for: 4 of 20 → 35 of 60** (10 and 25 of 40), about level with v1.456's Spring
  Sniper (10 of 20) and under the Pump Sniper, SVD and DMR (14-16 of 20). It no longer sits at the bottom of its class.
- **The close fights hardly moved**, as they should not for a bolt sniper: Sean round his corner 1 → 0 of 20, Marcus at
  the lot 12 → 14 of 20. The quicker bolt does not make it a corner gun.
- The "before" duel (4 of 20) came in under v1.456's 7 of 20 for the same gun: twenty rounds still swing by about three
  either way, which is why the after was run to 60.
