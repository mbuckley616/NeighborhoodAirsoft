## v1.488 — Snow forts that BBs knock down (fall and winter, step 4)
The last of the weather's rules in Michael's **B**: "snow forts can be knocked down".

**What changed** (63-seasons, one line each in 32-street-kit and 78-bb-flight). In a winter match, every cardboard box
on the map is a block of packed snow instead, where it stood and as big. These are the `addHomeDepotBox` boxes the
summer's matches are built round, now marked `cardboardBox`. A BB that hits a snow block is swallowed with a puff of
snow, and every hit counts, from either side. At 15 hits the block slumps to 55% of its height. You can shoot over it
then, and a kid stops picking it as cover to stand behind (cover under 0.5 m doesn't count). At 30 hits it falls in: it
is gone from the map's obstacles and its cover list, with a last puff. Nothing else on the map breaks, and in the
summer the boxes are cardboard as before.

Also in this version: `seasonBriefTag` had been declared twice in 63-seasons in v1.487 (legal, as the later one wins,
but against the split's rule), and now it is declared once.
