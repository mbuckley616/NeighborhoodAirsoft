## v1.454 — The balance pass, step 2: the bot racks its gun, and the lot measured again (D.88)
v1.452's table had every gun you rack, cock or break open losing all five rounds to Marcus at the Riverside Market lot,
and its Still open asked whether that was the guns or the bot before any of them was tuned. It was the bot.

**The bug.** The pull that fires a single-action or racked gun leaves the trigger held and spent (`lmbConsumed`), and
the hammer, pump, bolt or barrel only comes back on a fresh pull. The bot held on: with the trigger down it never
pressed again, so every one of those 19 guns fired once a round and then stood there. Against Sean it hardly showed,
since he comes into sight at 13 m and the first shot usually tagged him. Marcus is first seen 42 m off, too far for
that first shot, and he walked up to the bot and tagged it with its gun still uncocked (the Long Revolver: one shot at
0.27 s, then nothing for 8 s while Marcus closed from 20 m to 7). The bot now lets go of a spent trigger first, then
pulls to cock as a player does.

**Two more fixes to the bot**, from the same Still open:
- **Hold-over.** A BB falls under gravity less the hop-up's fading lift (`updateBBs`), so a chest aimed at 30 m is a
  BB 0.8 m low at 48 m/s and 2.3 m low at 32 m/s. The bot now pitches up for the drop at the gun's own speed (a gas
  gun's as its mag empties), solving the same flight `updateBBs` steps. The BB's sideways curve is left to chance, as
  for a player.
- **The outcome.** A round that ended with no tag the bot's per-frame check saw now counts by `endScenario`'s own
  outcome, not as a loss.

What v1.452 called a car roof in the line was neither: the Spring Sniper's BB that stopped 18 m out was curving off
from a 42 m shot. No game code changed in this version.
