## v1.393 — The crew-first ladder, step 1: five match types, and the solo matches your crew now plays
Card `fable/d57-crew-ladder` (backlog D.57), step 1 of four. Michael asked (local session, 22 Oct) why your crew plays
some matches and not others, with nothing on screen to say why, and answered the plan in `docs/crew_ladder.md` with "All
of it" (decisions, "The crew-first ladder and Crew Captains"). Its rule: your crew plays every match unless the match
says why not. On main, 32 of the 78 ladder matches took the crew. Just after Eric, Sean and Nick join, the Hollow's four
fort matches were you alone against five or six, with Sean among them.

**The types.** Every ladder match but the two tutorials now has one. It shows as a chip on its map card, after the
matchup, and as one line at the top of both briefings, the map's and the loading screen's (`matchType`, `matchTypeLine`
and the `MATCH_TYPES` table, in `92-summer-crew`):

Three matches have a line of their own:
- King of the Course: "The twins' rule: one at a time on their course. Your crew watches from the fence." It is the
  plan's "one climber at a time", which no longer fits since the treehouse became the twins' course in v1.375.
- Downhill Derby: "The whole street shoots at the cart. Crews don't count on the hill."
- Cart Path Only: "Eric's driving. You shoot."

Both rides came after the plan was written, so their types are my reading. So is SNUCK OUT. The plan names five types,
but its Bunratty table gives the night lane "snuck out alone" as the reason, and none of the five fits a match you play
alone because you snuck out.

With nobody in your crew yet, a CREW line names who is with you ("No crew yet: Nick plays with you today."). A callout
says "Just you and them."

**The solo matches your crew now plays (12).** These had no reason to be solo:
- Bunratty: Storm the Court, Hold the Cul-de-Sac, The Pincer.
- The Hollow: Storm the North Fort, Hold the South Fort, Night Assault on the Fort.
- Hold the Doors, Hold the Bunker, Hold the Bulb Fort, Hold the Creek Fort, Hold the Portables and Hold the Gazebo.

Each has `crewPosts`: anchors on your side, picked from those nearest your start, that your crew holds in turn. A
solo match had no ally place for `crewSides` to copy, so without posts the scene would have dropped the kids.
`crewTeamMatch` now counts these matches. Your crew starts in the fan round you, as in any team match, and walks to
those posts.

Their sides: on main a team match's other side grows by as many kids as your first-time crew adds to yours. That
would have put eight kids in the Hollow's forts and ten at the creek. Instead, a match turned crew keeps its own kids,
and the street makes their side at least as many as yours, the first time through. So the Hollow's forts are 4 v 5 and
4 v 6, Bunratty's three are 2 v 3, and Hold the Bulb Fort is 5 v 5. A crew kid the match had on the other side is
swapped for a street kid, as in every team match. That takes Sean out of the Hollow forts' attackers.

**CLUTCH.** The plan replaces "alone against three for no reason" with your crew already tagged out. In the two CLUTCH
matches your crew is fielded at the match's sideline posts (`crewPosts` with `clutchOut`) and is out from the first
frame. Each kid stands with his hand up at his post, already "walked off" (v1.264), so every BB passes him. He counts
for nobody. Their side keeps the match's own number, and nobody from the street fills a place.

What the screens show for a CLUTCH match:
- The map card counts only you (1V3).
- The briefing's roster says "already out" under each crew kid and has no Bench buttons.
- "Today it's one of you against six" counts only you.
- The crew-change line doesn't call the out kids "not playing".

**A hold's extra attackers attack.** When the street adds kids to a hold's side, they took the place of an attacker but
lost its role. In Hold the Portables they waited "deploying" at their huddle the whole 120 s (`high-school`). In a
match with `crewPosts` they now keep the attacker role, and come again when tagged as the match's own kids do.

**Words.** Storm the North Fort's briefing said "You're coming up from the south end alone"; "alone" is gone. Several
other briefings still name the old sides. Hold the Cul-de-Sac and The Pincer still name Sean on the other side, and
v1.360's "Today it's…" line after them says who is really there. Rewriting every briefing for its new sides is step 4,
as the plan's Owed list says.

**Not in this step:**
- The side-consistency rule (step 2). Mitchell and Mason still stand with Seth in the Hollow, and a street kid filling a
  place on your side can be one of Seth's (Devon in a fort match when you have only Eric).
- The captains (step 3).
- The order, the renames and the side matches (step 4).
