## v1.461 — Recorded voices, step 1: every line has an id, and the script
Card `fable/d9-recorded-voices`, backlog D.9. Michael asked on 2 Oct whether VoiceStudio (an app that runs AI voice
models on his own computer) could give the kids better voices than the browser's; he chose more lines first (v1.150,
v1.256, v1.397), and on 21 Oct (**A**) "now: a card writes every kid's script and the clip player; you and a local
session record them". A cloud session can't run VoiceStudio, so this card makes everything ready for that evening: the
script, the player and the packing script. This version is the first part: what every line is called, and the script.

**Every line the game can speak has an id**, worked out from where it sits in its table, so nothing said changes:
`(speaker, situation, index from 1)`. A kid's own line is `seth.taunt.3`, his own excuse `seth.excuse.2`, a crew kid's
line in your room `eric.home.1`; a line any kid of a kind may say is named for its table: `band.high_agg.taunt.3` (the
pushy kids' defaults), `excuse.far.2`, `teammate.4`, `infection.1`, `noodle.5`, `squad.push.2` (a walkie answer to an
order). 953 lines in all: 752 are one kid's own across the 33 kids, 201 are shared. Four places in the game speak
(`tryNpcSpeak` for every situation, `kidSayLine` for the noodle and the crew in your room, `squadWalkieSay` for an
order's answer, and the walkie inside `tryNpcSpeak`); each now knows the id of the line it says, and `VOICE.log`
carries it.

**A clip is one kid reading one line**, so its name is the line's id under the kid who says it (`voiceClipKey`): his own
line is already his (`seth.taunt.3`), a shared line gets his id in front (`seth.teammate.4`), and a line that says a
friend's name (`They got {name}!`) adds the name (`@sean`), which is many clips for one line and is left to the browser
voice. An index moves if someone puts a line in above it later, so a clip will carry its line's words and play only
while they match (step 2): a stale clip falls back to the browser voice, never says the wrong line.

**`docs/voice_script.md`**, the script Michael feeds VoiceStudio: how a clip is named, then each kid with a one-line
voice brief (his age as the game has it, how he talks, his height and build, how pushy his lines are), the shared
tables he draws from, and his own lines with their ids and the tone each situation is read in; then the shared lines,
table by table, with who may say them. It is **generated** by `node scripts/voicescript.mjs` from the game's own tables
(`voiceLineCatalog`, read in headless Chromium), so it can't drift: change a line in `js/`, run the script. The voice
briefs live in the script (`VOICE_BRIEFS`), from the comments over each kid's lines and his CHARACTERS entry.

Recording order the script recommends: each kid's own lines first. A kid draws his own line 7 times in 10 (an excuse 1
in 2), so his own lines are most of what you hear from him; 752 clips cover them, and the 201 shared lines would be
up to 33 readings each.

Under it: `voiceLineOwnId`, `voiceLineSharedId`, `voiceLineIdFor`, `voiceClipKey`, `VOICE_SITUATION_TONE` and
`voiceLineCatalog` in 20-characters. Small edits outside the play lane's files: `CREW_HOME_FALLBACK` (the crew's
"Hey.") named in 30-bedroom (maps) so the script has it, and the noodle and the squad's answer pass their ids (14-gear,
76-bbs, play). No random draw moved: the noodle and the answer still draw once, as before.
