## v1.452 — The balance pass, step 1: a 1v1 for every gun (D.88)
Michael chose **B** on "What the play lane builds next, after gun batch 8" (control room, 6 Nov): a balance pass over the
50 guns, a headless 1v1 round for every gun against the same kid, a table of wins, time to first tag and shots per tag,
then the outliers tuned one at a time, each a version. This version is the measure and the first table. No game code
changed.

**`scripts/balance.mjs`** plays the rounds. A bot stands at the player's start with the gun aimed in. It turns to the
chest of the nearest kid it can see within 70 m and first pulls the trigger a quarter second after it sees him. It holds
the trigger on an automatic and racks or breaks the gun open when it must. It clears a jam with R, and when the gun runs
dry it puts in a fresh mag in 0.6 s plus 0.4 s times the gun's reload time. A round ends at the first tag either way, and
it is a win if the bot tagged first. The renderer is off, so a round takes a few seconds. Four browsers at once ran the
500 rounds below in about 20 minutes. `node scripts/balance.mjs --guns dmr,m14 --rounds 20` re-measures a few guns before
and after a tuning. It is not a suite: it measures, it doesn't pass or fail.

The two 1v1s are the critic's: Sean with his AK-47 on Bunratty (he comes into sight at about 13 m, at 3 s or 11 s) and
Marcus with his pistol at the Riverside Market lot (18-27 m, in sight early). Each gun has its own shop default: the
base mag, no parts. Five rounds a gun in each:

"First sight to tag" and "Shots to tag" are medians over the wins. Read them with the bot in mind: it aims perfectly, so
spread and the BB's flight decide whether a shot lands, and its first shot comes 0.25 s after first sight.

**What the table says:**
- **Twelve guns won all ten**: the DMR, the SVD, the MP5, the UMP, the P90, the Vector, the AK-47, the G36C, the M4, the
  RPK, the M249 and the M60. They are the dear end of the shop, as their prices say they should be.
- **The bottom**: the Derringer 1 of 10, the Spring Pistol and the Spring Shotgun 2 of 10. They are the cheap end, so
  that is mostly as meant. Against Sean's AK the Derringer won 1 of 5 where the Spring Pistol won 2 and the Six-Shooter 3.
- **The Rattle and the Sprayer lost all five to Sean** (both 4 of 5 at the lot), the only automatics to lose every Sean
  round. They are the two cheapest automatics, but the Tick-Tock and the Bucket at the same end won 3 and 4 against him.
  They are the first outliers to look at.
- **At the lot, every gun you rack, cock or break open between shots lost all five**: 19 guns, from the Spring Pistol to
  the L96. Every gun that fires again without that (electric, gas, CO2 double-action, automatic) won 2-5. Against Sean
  the same racked guns mostly won with their first shot. One BB, then a rack, loses to a pistol at 20 m only if the first
  BB misses, and it missed every time. It may be the bot, not the guns: it checks its line from the eye, and the BB
  leaves the muzzle lower, so at the lot a car's roof may stop the shot. To find out before any of those guns is tuned.

The first measure had a bot bug worth writing down for the next one: it pulled the trigger before it turned to its target
on the frame it first saw him, so every gun's first shot went where it had been looking. Every gun you rack lost all ten
rounds. Fixed before the table above.
### Verified
- `scripts/balance.mjs` ran 500 rounds (50 guns, 2 matches, 5 each); none reached its 150 s limit, the longest took 34 s.
  7 of the 500 ended with the match over but no tag the bot saw (the Rattle, the Ghost, the Electric Pistol and the Auto
  Pistol against Sean, the Spring Shotgun, the Pump Sniper and the L96 at the lot). The table counts them as losses. Each
  ended on a whole second, between two of the script's 60-frame chunks, so the game decided something the bot's
  per-frame check missed.
- A check of the fixed bot (3 rounds each against Sean): Long Revolver, Spring Shotgun and M14 3 of 3, Spring Assault
  Rifle 2 of 3, each winning with its first shot about 0.5 s after first sight.
- `node scripts/parsecheck.mjs` green (no `js/` file changed); `smoke` green.
