## v1.484 — Nick comes back after 15 s
Michael answered **B** on "Nick Won't Budge: when he takes cover after his walk" (control room, 20 Nov). That was the
question v1.482 left. In Nick Won't Budge (`bunratty_nick`), after 40 s with no line and no BB near him, Nick walks out
to where his AR reaches you (v1.481, v1.482). If you shoot at him there, he runs to the nearest cover that shields him,
as any kid under fire in the open does. On Bunratty that cover is back in his yard, out of sight. His 40 s clock then
started again, so a player who stayed put waited about 40 s more for him (the critic saw 92 s).

**What changed** (80-ai). Once Nick has walked out the first time, his clock is 15 s instead of his `patience` (40).
The rule for the clock is the same: it counts only while he has no line to you and no BB has passed near him in the
last second. So he comes back about 15 s after the shooting stops. The first walk-out is unchanged at 40 s. The walk-out
spot is still picked by v1.482's rule. Only kids with `patience` have this clock, and only Nick has it.
