## v1.473 — Kids can't see through hills
The critic (12 Nov, Found in play) stood at the start of Two in the Yards with Jamie playing along: Devon's sniper fired
all match, 146 BBs in 8 one-minute rounds, and every one stopped in the lawn's crest about 35 m out, short of the road.
From his eye the crest hides you entirely.

**What was wrong.** A kid's sight line (`hasLineOfSight`, 80-ai) tested the map's obstacles (houses, fences, cars, trees)
and never the ground. Over a crest he "saw" you, took his shot, and the BB's flight, which does stop at the ground, ended
in the grass. The v1.93/v1.470 clear-line cast before a shot was the same: obstacles only.

**What changed.**
- Every sight line now tests the ground too: if the terrain rises above the straight line from his eye to you anywhere
  between the first and last metre, he has no line. This is the one sight test the kids use (picking a target, peeking,
  shooting, squad orders, capture the flag, seeing your glow-stick tracer), so it holds on every sloped map: Winnmark,
  Bunratty, the Hollow and the rest. Flat maps answer at once, as before.
- The clear-line cast with an eye line (v1.470, out to 3 m short of you) also tests the ground, 15 cm under the line for
  the BB's sag: a crest his eye just clears still takes a sagging BB, so he lifts his muzzle up to the same 0.7 m, or
  holds and moves on after three held pulls, as he does at a car.
- The heights come from a 1 m grid of the map's own ground, filled as lines need it and kept for the round: Winnmark's
  ground costs about 1.5 µs a sample and the kids test their lines every frame.

At the start of Two in the Yards Devon now holds instead of shooting the lawn; he peeks from the back yards and takes you
when you come over the crest or down the side yards, as the match's blurb says ("he'll let Jamie flush you out").
