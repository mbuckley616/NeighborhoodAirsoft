## v1.398 — The SPAS-12, a second pump shotgun (gun batch 5, D.63); 31 guns
Michael chose what the play lane builds next on 31 Oct: **C**, per-kid excuses first (v1.397), then gun batch 5, four
to six more guns toward 40, "say a second pump shotgun, a gas pistol, an SMG, a DMR". This is the first of them.

The shop had one pump shotgun that sells, the Spring Shotgun ($60, 2 or 3 BBs a pump), plus the Thunder Pump's wide
air cloud and the Semi-Auto Shotgun with no pump. **The SPAS-12**, $140, sits under SHOTGUNS between the Thunder Pump
and the Semi-Auto Shotgun. It is a better pump for a price in time and weight, not a straight upgrade:
- **Three BBs every pump**, never two.
- **A faster BB** (34 m/s; the Spring Shotgun's 30), in a **tighter cloud** (spread 0.42, 0.55) that flies straight
  for 10 m (8).
- **What it costs:** a slower pump (1.0 s, 0.85), steel and heavier (8.5 lb, 6), so a slower walk (0.92, 1.0).
- **Shells:** 45 BBs, fifteen pumps, with a 72-BB shell holder ($22) and spare shells (45, $14).

The first-person model is the Spring Shotgun's receiver, barrel, tube, ribbed pump and bead, all in black, with a
vented shield along the top and sides of the barrel (its top under the bead's line), a pistol grip in place of the
wrist (the Semi-Auto Shotgun's grip, so the right hand holds it the same way), and a folding metal stock opened out
behind: two rods to a butt plate with its hook under. The left hand stays on the pump, which racks every shot. Aimed,
it looks down the bead (its own aimed pose; the Spring Shotgun's sits the bead lower). A kid holding one would show
the shotgun's blocky gun, but no kid carries one. It takes a sight, two rails and the under-barrel slot, as the
shotgun does. The game has 31 guns.
