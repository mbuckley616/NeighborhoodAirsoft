## v1.364 — Online teams: a room of up to eight, kids in the empty slots, and a rematch
Card `fable/d2-online-teams`, its first step: backlog D.2 step 2 (Michael: A on "go on to teams", 21 Oct), with D.67 (the host picks the map and the time of day as on the Pickup Game board; a rematch after a game) and D.68 ("multiple people ... or an NPC Draft to fill empty slots"). v1.230's online play was one friend in a 1v1 on Winnmark with the host's day or night, lives and clock; Michael and his brother asked for more of the board and more people.

**The room.** HOST A ROOM still gives a four-letter code, and now the room holds up to eight. The host's panel is the board itself: Where (any map he has opened), How many (1v1 up to the map's biggest team size), When, Lives and Clock, teams only for now. Each friend lands on Red, or on whichever side has fewer people, and can MOVE HERE to the other while there is room. Every slot no person takes is a kid from that street, and each side's people pick their own side's kids from their own board, a row of the street's kids as on the board's "With you" (D.68's draft); a side's picks start from the board's defaults, and START waits ("PICK THE KIDS") while a side is short. Guests see the host's settings as a line of words and the sides as the host has them, updated as anything changes. The board can't be made smaller than the most people on a side, and a map too small for them is greyed out.

**The match.** The host's game is still the referee, and now plays every kid too. START sends each guest the roster (who is who, on which side, at which of the map's spots: Blue's are the map's start and its allies' anchors, Red's its other side's; kids take the first spots of their side, people the last), and every game builds the same match from its own side, so a Red friend's own team is Red. The host's game sends where everyone is about 20 times a second (`w`: each person and kid, his facing, crouch, whether he is walking home or out, and his lives); each guest sends only where he is. In a guest's game every other kid and person is moved from the host's word and posed as before (gun held, crouched, gun up when tagged or out). Each shot is sent once and flies in every game, the kids' too; only the host's copy tags, and a tag on a friend is told to him, as is his own tag on anyone (his hit marker and his tag count). The star stays a star: guests talk only to the host, who passes on what the others need.

A tagged player still walks home (v1.332). One with no lives left no longer ends the match for his side: he stays on the field gun up, "YOU'RE OUT · YOUR SIDE PLAYS ON", nothing tags him and he can't fire, and the match ends when a side has nobody left in it, people and kids, or on the clock, as a pickup team game does. The host's result is Blue's, and each guest's game reads it for his own side.

**A friend who drops** (his browser closes, or he leaves with F): his kid stays where he is with his look, his gun and his lives left, and the host's game plays him from then on as one of its own kids (D.2 step 3 of the card); the others see "BLUEY LEFT · A KID PLAYS THE SPOT". If the host leaves, the match is over for everyone, as before ("The host left").

**REMATCH** (D.67): on the host's result card, the old PLAY AGAIN button reads REMATCH and takes everyone still in the room back to it on the board, the code and the settings as they were, for the host to start again or change; a guest's reads "REMATCH: THE HOST'S CALL" until then. BACK TO YOUR ROOM leaves the room, as before.

Under it: the PeerJS transport keeps a link per friend (`send(m, to)`, `closed(pid)`), stays on the matchmaker so friends can still join between matches, and turns away a ninth person or anyone after START ("That room's match has started"). The kid AI loop hands a remote kid to `netDriveRemote` before its out-kid branch, so an out friend is posed by his own game's word. A friend's kid left behind gets `netBot` and no voice lines (he wears Seth's lines under another name otherwise). Everything is behind `Net.active`: the single-player game is as it was, and nothing loads at startup.
