## v1.364 — Cart Path Only: a golf-cart ride-along at the club
Backlog D.72. Michael's note (23 Oct) was "We could maybe incorporate vehicles like golf carts as well", and after the go-cart race shipped (v1.354) the question went to him in decisions: a cart to drive, a ride-along, carts as cover, or not now. He answered **B** on the control room this afternoon: "a ride-along match, Eric drives a golf cart round a set loop of the course, you ride on the back and shoot, kids in the trees and bunkers, three lives as the race."

**Cart Path Only** is a new match at Willow Bend, after The Eighteenth and before Hold the Gazebo on the club's ladder. (I first put it before Night Swim, but Seth's text the night before Night Swim (v1.363) has to follow the match just before it, and that text and its test are under the open Seth FREEZE, so the ride sits one match earlier.) Eric drives a white golf cart, with him at the wheel, and you sit on the back seat. The loop starts by the halfway hut. It goes up the cart path, east along the head of the fairway (between the green and the fairway bunker), down by the trees at the east fence, and back along the south lawn. That is 104 m with the corners rounded (5 m arcs), and he drives it twice. He slows to 3 m/s for each corner and opens up to 5.6 m/s on the straights, so the ride takes about 58 s. You only aim: W, A, S and D do nothing. Your view turns with the cart, so you keep looking where you were relative to the seat. The two carts that stood parked on the cart path are left off in this match, since Eric's cart uses the path.

Five kids hold spots round the course: Marcus (UMP) in the greenside bunker, Jamie (shotgun) on the green's far side, Trey (MP5) by the willow past the pond inside the loop, Brooke (UMP) behind the lawn tree west of the path, and Devon (AR) among the carts by the barn. Each is 10-16 m off the loop. Lives work as in the race (Game.scenario.race is set, so the same code runs): three, a tag costs one and gives 1.5 s of grace, the cart drives on, and finishing the second lap with a life left wins ($120; a loss pays $28).

The kids needed one rule of their own. On their spots, with nobody shooting back, they tagged a rider three times in the first 7-13 s in 4 of 4 rounds, mostly Marcus from 23 m. He was ahead of the cart as it drove up the path toward him, so his aim at where you were needed no lead. Widening their aim with the cart's speed made it worse: they never lead, so a passing cart is missed by the lag, and the extra spread put more BBs where you had moved to. Instead, on the course a kid holds fire until the cart is within 15 m (`RIDE.range`, a check in `spawnEnemyBB`). He lets you come to him and shoots as you pass. You can shoot him from as far as you can see him.

The code: `rideSetup`, `updateRide` and `buildGolfCartMesh` in `72-player` beside the go-cart (`updateCart` hands a ride to `updateRide`); `startScenario` sets it up for a scenario with `ride: true` on a map that gives a `rideLoop` (`64-scenario-flow`). The club's scene gives the loop, Eric's start and the five spots, and drops the path's two parked carts in its `ride` variant (`48-other-maps`, the maps lane's file: one condition and two blocks of data). The scenario is in `24-scenarios`, and its ladder line is in `90-progression`.
