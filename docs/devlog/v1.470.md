## v1.470 — Trey stops shooting the parked car
The critic (10 Nov, Found in play) stood on the street 45 m due east of Trey's step in Trey on Overwatch: Trey had an eye
line to you and fired 14 BBs in 40 s, and every one stopped in the sedan parked about 15 m in front of him. v1.456 had
seen the same while building the sniper duel and moved the duel's spot instead.

**What was wrong.** Before a kid fires, the clear-line check (v1.93, `spawnEnemyBB` in 76-bbs) casts the shot's first 3 m
against every obstacle, lifts the muzzle up to 0.7 m to clear it, or holds fire. Nothing looked past 3 m. Trey's eye line
cleared the car's cabin (it sits on the street 1.15 m up, the cabin 0.55 m over that); his muzzle, lower and a quarter
metre to the side, cleared its top by 2 cm on a straight line, and the BB's flight sags about 10 cm under the straight
line by then, so it struck the cabin and bounced back.

**What changed.** When the kid has an eye line on his target, the muzzle's line is now cast out to 3 m short of the
target as well, and 15 cm under it for the sag. The same lift applies (he raises the gun up to 0.7 m, as when leaning over
a hood), and if nothing clears it he holds, which after three held pulls moves him on as before (v1.122). Two things stay
as they were, on purpose:
- The last 3 m before the target are left out, so a kid still shoots at the wall or car you are crouched behind.
- With no eye line (covering fire at the spot he last saw you) nothing changes: that fire is meant to land on your cover.
Picket fences (`bbPass`) are left out of the far cast, as they are out of the eye line, since a BB goes through them.

At the critic's spot Trey now holds three pulls, walks up to the sedan and shoots over its hood from there.
