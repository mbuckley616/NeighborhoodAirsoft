## v1.475 — Kids read a friend's streak
Michael chose **A** on "What the play lane builds next, after tracers online" (decisions, 12 Nov): on the host, a friend's
glow-stick streak by night tells the kids where that friend fired from, as yours has since v1.468.

**What was wrong.** v1.474 sent the streak over the link, so the host's game drew a friend's streak, but only to see:
the sighting (a kid within 35 m of the BB who sees it go by at night, with a clear line from his eyes, knows where it
came from) read the shots of the player at the keyboard alone. Online, the friend with the Glow-Stick Tracer paid
nothing for it in the host's game, where the kids are run.

**What changed.**
- `netSpawnShot` (94-pickup-online): on the host, a friend's shot that arrives with the streak now carries where he stood
  when it left (his kid's place in the host's game) and who he is (`_tracerBy`), as your own shot carries yours.
- `tracerSeen` (78-bb-flight) notes whose streak the kid saw; `threatScore` and `updateEnemies` (80-ai) read that: the
  pull of the sighting (3 s) goes to that friend, and for 0.3 s the kid takes it as having seen him there (covering fire
  at that spot, a quick peek), exactly as he does with yours. A sighting of your streak is unchanged.
- Only on the host and only for a friend's shot (pickup game online or co-op); a guest's game runs no kids. By day
  nothing, as before. Mason's streak stays only to see.
