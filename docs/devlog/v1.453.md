## v1.453 — Hollins Ridge High at real scale: the campus, its three areas and the four matches
Card `fable/d51-hollins-ridge` (backlog D.51, the sixth and last map), steps 1 to 4 in one version, as Willow Bend's
were: a new place at real scale cannot carry the old anchors, so the ground, the buildings and the re-anchored matches
only make a playable game together. Michael answered step 0's three questions on 6 Nov: the matches play in three areas
(A: the front, the west lot, the stadium), four portables are kept as a made-up annex at the lot's north end (A, so The
Portables and Hold the Portables keep their names and ids), and the mural's made-up mascot is a ram (A). His note named
the real school's mascot; the card keeps no real mascot in the repo, so the ram it is.

**The place** (`buildHighSchoolScene`, `js/48-other-maps.js`, all of it new; `docs/refs/high-school.md`; -z north, the
origin in the west lot; step 0's plan, `v1.451.md`, has the measured sizes). A level plateau, falling 5 m at the west
and south through a grass bank to Scott Rd (`schoolGroundY`). The stadium in the west: the red eight-lane track round a
blue apron, the turf with its yard lines, black end zones, a ring of our own at midfield and yellow goalposts; the home
grandstand on the east side, twenty concrete rows from a 1.2 m front wall with the dark blue fascia up to 8 m, three
sections with blue rails, a block of blue seats high in the middle and the two-level glass press box on top; behind it
the concourse with the concession window (Michael's A: the old concession stand became this window), the restroom doors,
trash cans, picnic tables, the ticket booth and an equipment trailer; the low aluminium visitor stands backing onto the
road, the scoreboard's shape behind the north goalpost (no panels), eight 28 m light poles with lamp banks, the
chain-link with its blue windscreen round the track, open at both ends of the home stands. The west lot: four double
rows of stalls running north-south, cars nose to nose, curbed grass islands with a small tree at the row ends, 10 m lot
lights, the fire lane behind the stands; at its north end the four portables in a row on a gravel pad (P1-P4, their
doors and landings facing the lot) beside the blue-roofed building (17 x 29 m), the service road past them. The school:
brick wings with a tan band on the taller ones, flat grey roofs with rooftop units, narrow windows lit at night, the
taller white block, and the white gym dome (42 m across, 13 m high, grey seams) at the north-west corner facing the lot.
The front entrance on the south-east face: the glass hall (14 x 9.5 m) under its curved standing-seam roof between two tan
stone piers, the name over the doors, the circle drive (31 m) round its grass island with the red fire-lane kerb, the
12 m flagpole and red maples, the drive out to the road, guest parking to the west, the east lot's rows to the east with
the yellow buses along its far side. The back drive climbs 5 m from Scott Rd to the lot between young oaks and pines,
with its faculty-and-buses sign. The retaining wall along the road below the classroom wings carries the mural: our own
chrome block letters spelling RIDGE on a white and pale-blue splash, HOLLINS RIDGE in black capitals above, a ram's head
with curled horns beside them, no signature. North of it all the practice field, the baseball field and the four tennis
courts; two-storey houses and woods round every side. `EDGE_BACKDROPS` has no school entry any more (as Winnmark and
Willow Bend: the map builds its own surroundings).

**The three areas** (the variant, `SCHOOL_AREAS`): `front`, x 165..245, z 68..124 (80 x 56 m: the walk, the circle, the
guest parking's east end, the east lot's first rows); `lot`, x -52..32, z -78..-16 (84 x 62 m: the lot's north half,
the portables and the blue-roofed building); `stadium`, x -126..-50, z -40..40 (76 x 80 m: the turf's east half, the
track, the home stands and the concourse). Only what reaches into the area is solid or cover; a low post line marks the
open sides. Every mesh but the signs is baked into one mesh per look (`bakeMeshesByLook`); the sun's shadow box sits
over the area; the sun is 1.2, as the other rebuilt maps. Kids walk round by the field (`fieldAdvance`).
