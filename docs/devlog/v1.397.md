never with you. Rebecca is never against you. A captain and his crew never play for another captain.

Your crew and Rebecca are never against you. Nobody of Seth's or Marcus's crew stands with you, and nobody against his
own captain. When you have no crew, the street makes up your numbers, and every kid in the game is in some crew. So a
kid from another street's crew may fill a place on your side: Ryan, Owen and Joe at the school or Bellfield. A kid of
the zone's own crew who was filling your side goes over to his captain's side instead, in place of a kid there from
outside it. That keeps the school's kids playing for the school. A kid on the wrong side is
swapped at his spot for one who may play there, the zone's own crew first. He keeps the spot's gun, role and start, so
every count and every match's shape stays the same. A kid of Seth's crew comes in this way only once you know he runs
with Seth: Evan after the lot, Andrew after the twins' treehouse, Garrett and Colin after Bellfield's flags. Seth
himself is never added this way: he plays only where a match puts him.
