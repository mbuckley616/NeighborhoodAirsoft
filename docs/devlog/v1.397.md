## v1.397 — Every kid has his own excuses (D.32's next step)
Michael chose what the play lane builds next on 31 Oct: **C**, per-kid excuses first, then gun batch 5. Since v1.256
a kid you tag sometimes makes an excuse instead of owning it (60% of the pushy kids, 40% of the middle, 15% of the

But every kid drew from the same 22 lines, so Owen and Sean complained in the same words.

Marcus doesn't know these yards ("Y'all have home field, that's why!"), the twins blame each other ("CONNOR! You left
me!"), Joe blames your cheap gun, Devon the wind on his shot, Nathan says "Statistically, that shouldn't have hit,"
Malik's shells stuck, Owen's leg fell asleep, and Jamie, who doesn't talk trash, "is still learning these yards."
When a kid makes an excuse it is one of his own one time in two (`EXCUSE_OWN_SHARE`) and otherwise the shared line
for how he was tagged, so the situations still show. His own lines are ones that fit any tag. How often a kid makes
an excuse at all is unchanged, and the picker still never repeats his last three lines or anyone's last two.
