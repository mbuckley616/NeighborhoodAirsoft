## v1.402 — The crew-first ladder, step 4a: side matches and the new order
Card `fable/d57-crew-ladder` (backlog D.57), the first part of step 4. The plan (`docs/crew_ladder.md`, Michael's "All
of it", 22 Oct) found the zones 16, 15, 12, 6, 11, 4, 4 and 5 matches long, with every match gating the next. Its fix is
side matches: a zone's extra matches still pay and count toward the captains' and the rival's scores, but no longer
gate anything. It also moves two matches, so each zone's order reads as a run against one crew that ends in its
captain's finale.

**Side matches** (`SIDE_MATCHES`, `isSideMatch` in `90-progression`), the nine the plan names:
- Winnmark: Every Kid for Themselves, Two in the Yards, The Whole Block, Last Stand at the Fort;
- Bunratty: Lights Out on the Lane;
- the Hollow: Night Assault on the Fort, Full-Auto Mayhem (Night), Night Game in the Woods;
- Northcliff: Lamplight on Bellfield.

A side match opens with the main match before it. The main match after it needs only that main match
(`prevMainMatchId`). A zone's capstone, the match that opens the next zone, is its last main match (`zoneCapstoneId`). The
main lines are now 13 at Winnmark, 15 at Bunratty, 9 in the Hollow and 12 at Northcliff. The map card of a side match
says SIDE at the start of its place line ("SIDE · Winnmark Court · ♥ 1 life"). As a badge beside the type chip it
wrapped the Winnmark cards' top rows, and `map-screen` counted 8 cards in view at 1280×720, not the 10 it wants. A locked card names the main match that opens it ("Win #6 to unlock" for the
cul-de-sac, not #5, the side free-for-all). NEXT on the map and the bed's next match pick the main line first.
