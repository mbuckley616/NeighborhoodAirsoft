## v1.442 — The Rubber-Band Bipod (D.86, the third homemade wave's second part); guns built for pictures no longer stay on the camera
Michael chose **A** on "What the play lane builds next, after gun batch 7" (control room, 4 Nov): a third wave of homemade
parts, filed as backlog D.86. The bipod is its second part. Its effect while crouched was left to the builder; this is
what last run's attempt chose, kept as it was.

**The Rubber-Band Bipod**, $9, 0.4 lb, in the UNDER slot of every long gun that has one. It is two paint stirrers cut to
12 cm, hinged on a wooden clothespin with a red rubber band and taped under the gun just ahead of your off hand, with a
band of rubber round each foot.
- **Standing** the legs lie folded forward along the underside, and the gun shoots as it does bare.
- **Crouched** (not in a slide) they swing down and splay out in 0.25 s. While they are down the whole cone, aimed or from
  the hip, is 0.6 as wide (`crouchSpreadMult`), scaled by how far down they are. Stand up and they fold back in 0.25 s.
- **The cost:** the sticks catch as you bring the gun up, so aiming in is 0.85 as quick (`adsSpeedMult`), standing or
  crouched. With 0.4 lb on top, it is a part for the player who settles behind cover and shoots from there, which is what
  the LMGs and snipers are for.

The legs are posed by `setBipodLegs` (`54-fp-gear`), which `updateGun` calls only when they move. The clothespin sits 3 cm
ahead of the off hand, measured on each kind of gun with the hands on, as the flashlight's is. The hinge sits 1.6 cm under
the lowest point of the gun along the folded legs, so they clear any tube or pump in front.

**Why last run's attempt was reverted, and the fix.** With the bipod in, `under-parts` closed its browser at its second match.
v1.441 has the same fault, which only showed once a part made the suite slower: here it runs past the 10-minute cap on
v1.441 too. Every first-person gun builder hangs its gun on `Game.camera`, and `buildBareGunMesh` (the shop's pictures, the
workbench, v1.441's measure of where the off hand sits) left it there, visible. `under-parts` builds each long gun with
every part showing. After that, 71 guns hung on the camera, and their 25 lit Taped-On Flashlights were spotlights in the
match scene. Every material then compiled for 25 more lights, and the second match took 90 s to draw its first frame
(17 s on v1.440).

In play, the measure's spare gun (one per kind, built the first time a long gun's parts are built with no hands on, for
example in the shop's Mods pictures) stayed on the camera at its origin. `buildBareGunMesh` now takes the gun off whatever
it was hung on. Every caller either puts it somewhere else (the workbench's turntable, a picture's holder) or only measures
it. The underside measure also skips see-through beams. On the Thunder Pump it had read the bottom of a laser's beam.
