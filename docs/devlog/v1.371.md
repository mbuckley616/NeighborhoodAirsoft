## v1.371 — The G36C, the first automatic rifle of gun batch 3
Backlog D.63 (Michael, 22 Oct: "I think my ideal would be 30-50 ... And generally just a few more Automatic rifles"; his A on 23 Oct, batches of four to six). Batches 1 and 2 brought the game to 25 guns, with the only full-auto rifles the AK-47 and the M249. Batch 3 is his "few more Automatic rifles", one a version; this is the first.

The **G36C** is a battery rifle, $260, under AUTOMATICS between the AK-47 ($240) and the M249 ($320). It is the AK's gun made shorter and lighter. It fires 11.7 BBs a second (the AK 10) and is tighter aimed (0.30 of the hip spread, the AK 0.35), and you walk with it at 0.9 (the AK 0.85). Its short barrel gives a slightly slower BB (52 m/s, the AK 55) that flies straight for 18 m (20). Its hip spread is the AK's. The mag is a smoky see-through 300 (the AK's drum holds 350), with a 470 high-cap for $40 and a $20 spare. It weighs 7 lb (the AK 8). It takes a sight, two rail parts and an under-barrel part, as the AK does.

In first person (`buildFPG36`, `js/52-fp-guns.js`) it has a dark grey receiver with a long slotted top rail carrying flip-up irons, a vented handguard, and an orange tip on a short barrel. The clear mag rakes forward with BBs showing inside it, and the skeleton stock is folded out. Your right hand is on the grip and your left under the handguard. Aimed, the front post's tip sits on the reticle (`js/74-gun-hud.js`), and an optic sits on the rail between the irons. The kids' copy (`buildEnemyGunMesh`, `js/50-kids.js`) is drawn in their blocky style, though no kid carries one yet. It also has its own reticle, a shop picture, a Loadout-kid model and a spare mag in the loadout list.
