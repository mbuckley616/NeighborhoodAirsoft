## v1.426 — The Taped-Up Long Mag (D.83)
The last part of the second homemade wave. Michael's answer on the slots (2 Nov, **A**) put "the taped-up mag under the
barrel (more BBs, heavier, slower reload)", and that is what it does.

**The Taped-Up Long Mag**, $18, on the under-barrel slot of the 24 long guns that have one (so, like the Taped Twin Mag,
the PVC foregrip and the slings, one of them at a time). Two mags duct-taped end to end and fed up through a hole cut in
the first one's floor:
- **Half as many BBs again in the gun's mag.** At BEGIN the gun loads 1.5 times its mag from the bag, rounded: the AR's
  25 becomes 38, its 40-round upgrade 60, the AK's 350 becomes 525 (`getMatchMagSize`, `js/14-gear.js`, used by
  `startScenario`, the briefing's "YOUR GUN LOADS", the loadout list and the gun slot's "Mag:" line). Spare mags in your
  slots are ordinary mags and still load their own size.
- **Slower to reload, both ways there are.** Swapping in a spare mag takes 0.6 s (0.4 bare), and a speed loader feeds
  27 BBs a second (40 bare): `getReloadMult` 1.5, in `onLmbDown` (`js/70-input.js`) and the feed in `updateGun`
  (`js/74-gun-hud.js`).
- **1 lb more to carry**, in the loadout weight like the twin mag (1.2) and the foregrip (0.8).

The model hangs under the handguard where the twin mag does: a black mag body over a grey one, raked back a little,
15.7 cm long, a wide band of duct tape over the join, a floor plate on the bottom and two bands round the handguard. On
the guns whose off hand moves to an under-barrel part (the MP5's kind), it takes the off hand as the twin mag does.
