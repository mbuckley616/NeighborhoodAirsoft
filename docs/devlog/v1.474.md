## v1.474 — Tracers show online
Michael chose **A** on "What the play lane builds next, after the kids' homemade parts" (decisions, 12 Nov): tracers
online. The Glow-Stick Tracer's green streak (yours since v1.468, Mason's since v1.472) was drawn only in the game that
fired the shot. Online, every shot crosses the link once (`netShot`, 94-pickup-online: where from, which way, its curve)
and flies in the other games, but the message carried nothing about the part, so your friend saw a plain BB.

**What changed.**
- A shot with the streak now carries one more field, `tr: 1`, in its `f` message: your own shot from `fireBB`, and on the
  host a kid's shot from `spawnEnemyBB` (Mason with his glow sticks). The host relays a guest's shot to the other guests
  as it was, field included.
- `netSpawnShot`, which flies someone else's shot here, puts the streak on it when the field is there, the same green
  streak, bright at night and faint by day by this game's own time of day.
- In the other game the streak is only to see, as Mason's is: no kid there takes it as a sighting of the shooter (the
  v1.468 sighting reads the shots of the player at the keyboard). A shot without the part sends no field and draws none,
  so older builds and plain shots are unchanged.

Co-op (95-coop) goes through the same two functions, so a friend in your room sees your streak too.
