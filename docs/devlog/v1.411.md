## v1.411 — The KSG, a bullpup pump shotgun (gun batch 6, D.63); 36 guns
Michael chose what the play lane builds next on 1 Nov: **A**, gun batch 6, to 40: six more in the neighborhood-kid
tone, one a version, "say a revolver, a bullpup, a lever-action, a light machine gun, a burst SMG, a pump sniper".
The Magnum (v1.410) was the revolver; this is the bullpup.

The game already had three bullpups, the FAMAS (a spring rifle), the P90 (an SMG) and the AUG (a battery rifle), and
SHOTGUNS had the fewest guns of the four kinds (4; AUTOMATICS 17), so the bullpup is a shotgun: **the KSG**, $150,
under SHOTGUNS between the SPAS-12 and the Semi-Auto Shotgun, the stubby black video-game pump every kid wants:
- **A spring pump like the Spring Shotgun:** 2 or 3 BBs every pump at 30 m/s.
- **Two tubes:** 60 shells, the most of any pump (the Spring Shotgun 40, the SPAS-12 45, the Thunder Pump 30); long
  tubes for 90, $24; spare shells 60 for $15.
- **Short:** the quickest pump of the shotguns (0.7 s; the Spring Shotgun 0.85, the SPAS-12 1.0, the Thunder Pump
  1.05) and the quickest walk of them (1.04; the others 0.92 to 1.0), 7 lb (the Spring Shotgun 6, the SPAS-12 8.5).
- **The short barrel's price:** a wider cloud (0.62; the Spring Shotgun 0.55, the SPAS-12 0.42), straight for 7 m (8).

The first-person model is the spring shotgun's receiver, barrel, pump and bead, all in black: the one tube under the
barrel becomes two side by side and the pump's clamp wraps both; the wrist and stock come off for a pistol grip behind
the trigger guard and a long boxy body from the receiver back to a steel butt pad (16 cm behind the grip), with the
two loading ports on its belly and a flat rail on top kept under the bead's line. The pump racks as the shotgun's does,
and the left hand rides it. Aimed, you look down the bead as on the SPAS-12; the reticle is the shotgun's ring a
little wider. It takes a sight, two rails and the under-barrel slot. A kid holding one has a blocky KSG of its own
(the barrel over the tubes, the pump, the long body back to the butt), but no kid carries one. The game has 36 guns.
