## v1.446 — The Gas Carbine (D.87, gun batch 8's first gun); 47 guns
Michael chose **B** on "What the play lane builds next, after the third homemade wave" (control room, 5 Nov): gun batch
8, four more guns toward 50, the kinds left to the builder. It is filed as backlog D.87. The question named a
pistol-calibre carbine first, and this is it.

**The Gas Carbine**, $175, under RIFLES after the Electric Rifle. It is the Gas Pistol's green-gas blowback in a short
olive carbine with a stock: a black gas mag with a brass fill valve on its base, a bare steel shroud round the barrel,
and a brass gas plug on the receiver. Every pull fires, and the gas throws the charging handle back.
- **5 a second** at most (the Gas Pistol 4.5, the Electric Rifle 6), a pull a shot.
- **A fast BB**: 50 m/s (the AR 45, the Gas Pistol 40, the M14 60), straight for 13 m (the AR 10, the M14 18), a slightly
  tighter cone (0.24; the AR 0.28), aimed 0.3.
- **The gas cools** as the mag empties, as on the Gas Pistol, but less: the last BB leaves at 85% of the first's speed
  (the pistol's at 80%).
- **6 lb**, between the Gas Pistol (3) and the AR (8), so a quicker walk than the rifles (0.95; the AR 0.85).
- 30 in its mag, a long mag of 45 for $18, spares at $10 (0.9 lb each).

Where it sits against the rest: it out-ranges the Electric Rifle and walks faster, but fires slower, and its BBs lose
speed through the mag. The M14 reaches further but is heavier and fires 3 a second.

It is built on the spring rifle's frame (`buildFPGasCarbine` on `buildFPAR`), so its hands, its rails, its under-barrel
slot, its aimed pose and its kid's gun mesh are the AR's, and every workbench part already fitted to the AR fits it.
The blowback is the Gas Pistol's `blowback` flag, which moves the AR's charging handle with each shot's kick.
