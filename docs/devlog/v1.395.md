## v1.395 — The SVD, a battery sniper (gun batch 4, D.63)
Michael wants 30 to 50 guns in the end and chose to get there in batches (D.63, answer A, 23 Oct). His list named
"1 or 2 other snipers"; the L96 (v1.336) was one, and the game had 28 guns. This is the other.

**The SVD**, $230, last under RIFLES after the L96. It is the Spring Sniper Rifle on a battery, so it plays differently
from both bolt guns rather than being a better or worse one:
- **No bolt.** Every pull fires one BB, two a second at most, and a new mag is racked with the charging handle (0.7 s),
  as on the other electric semi-autos.
- **What it costs for that:** a slower BB (70 m/s, the Spring Sniper's 75 and the L96's 85), wider (0.15, the
  Spring Sniper's 0.12; aimed 0.28 of it, 0.25), and straight for 22 m before it wobbles (25 m).
- **Lighter** (9 lb, the bolt guns 10 and 11), so you walk a little quicker with it (0.8, 0.75).
- **Mags:** 20, with a 30-round mag ($24) and a spare 20 ($10).

The first-person model is the spring sniper's barrel, sights and stock line (so its hands and aimed picture are the
sniper's), with the forend and stock in red-brown wood, a black receiver cover, a curved black mag under the receiver,
a slotted flash hider behind the orange tip, and no bolt: a charging handle on the right. Kids holding one would show
the sniper's blocky gun, but no kid carries one. It takes a sight, two rails and the under-barrel slot, as the sniper
does. The game has 29 guns.
