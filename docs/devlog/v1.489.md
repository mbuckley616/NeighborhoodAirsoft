## v1.489 — Coats, roof snow and Christmas lights (fall and winter, step 5)
Step 5 of Michael's **B** on "Fall and winter campaigns" (21 Nov): the season on the kids and the houses. Steps 1 to 4
turned the ground, the trees and the sky and added the weather's rules, but every kid still played the winter in a
t-shirt and shorts, the roofs stayed bare under falling snow, and the Christmas-week matches looked like any other week.

**The kids dress for the season** (`seasonDressKid`, 63-seasons; one line in `makeEnemyFromCharacter`, 50-kids). Every
kid in a fall or winter match, both sides, wears long sleeves to the wrist and long trousers: shorts are put away and
the bare leg takes his trousers' colour. He has a hood down his back too, except under a Halloween cape. In winter he
also wears a knit beanie with a pom-pom, a scarf with one end hanging down his front, and gloves. All three are one
colour of eight, picked by his name, so the same kid wears the same ones every match. A kid with a hat of his own, or a
costume's hat, keeps it. The sleeves and the hood are his shirt's colour, so a crew jersey's sleeves stay the crew's and
the sides read as before. A VIP's cap goes on in place of his beanie (`addVipCap`). It is all looks: the beanie lowers
with his head on a crouch, the rest rides the torso and arms, and no hitbox changes. `enterScenario` now sets
`Season.now` before the map is built (64-scenario-flow), so the kids a scene builder makes itself (Seth on Winnmark)
dress too, and a match entered straight after a winter one can't dress its kids for the wrong season.

**Snow on the roofs** (`seasonIsRoof`, 63-seasons). In winter the season pass now finds roofs on every map, whichever
builder made them: a mesh whose lowest point is over 2.2 m, at least 4 m² across, not green, with at least 45% of its
vertices on sloped or flat faces (either way up, as the house kit's roofs are double-sided and can be wound facing
down), or a wide, thin box (a flat roof). Its colour becomes snow, a little greyer than the ground's. It is a
recoloured copy, like everything else the pass changes. The first try counted only faces wound upward and missed
Winnmark's hip roofs; a version with no size limit would have whitened lamp heads.

**Christmas lights** (`addChristmasLights`, 63-seasons). Christmas Break on Bunratty and the three Bellfield matches in
Christmas week now carry `holiday: 'christmas'` (the map card shows 🎄 CHRISTMAS). They string coloured bulbs along
every house's front eaves, dipping between hooks every 1.5 m, and hang a wreath with a red bow on the door. They use the
spots Halloween uses (`holidayFront`, a door marker's web corners). The bulbs glow at night and are paler by day. It is
two merged meshes for the whole map and nothing is solid. Halloween's decorations and costumes ask only for
'halloween', so they stay away, and Halloween stays parked everywhere else.
