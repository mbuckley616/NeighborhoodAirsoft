## v1.486 — Fall 2003 and Winter 2003-04 on the ladder (fall and winter, step 2)
Step 2 of Michael's **B** on "Fall and winter campaigns" (21 Nov): the two chapters, their matches, and a way to reach
them on the map. Winning the Last Saturday now opens the fall, and winning the fall's last match opens the winter.

**The chapters** (`SEASON_CHAPTERS`, 63-seasons). Each chapter is four places with three matches each, so 24 matches in
all. They follow the year:
- **Fall 2003**: Winnmark Court in September (First Saturday Back, The Leaf-Pile Fort, Sweatshirt Weather), the Hollow
  in late September (Leaves Underfoot, The North Fort in October, The Fall Big Game), Hollins Ridge High in October
  (After the Last Bell, Homecoming Week, Under the Stadium Lights), and Bunratty Court at the end of October (Raking
  Season, Hold the Court Before Dark, and **Halloween Night** to end the fall).
- **Winter 2003-04**: Bunratty Court in December (First Snow, Storm the Snowy Court, Christmas Break), Bellfield in
  Christmas week (Bellfield in the Snow, Hold the Snow Fort, Lamplight in the Snow), the market lot in January (Cart
  Corral on Ice, Snowed In at the Doors, After Close in January), and Winnmark Court in February (Cold Hands, The
  Treehouse in the Snow, and **Snow Day** to end the winter).

Each match is one of the summer's plain matchups replayed later in the year. It copies that match's kids, rules and
pay, and it is built under that match's seed, so the cover is laid out the same as in the summer. It gets a new name, a
new story and its season. None of the bases has code of its own keyed to its id. Your crew comes along through the
summer's crew rules, so most matches are crew against crew, as the summer's late matches are. The stories name who
leads each side and give no head-counts. Within a place, each match opens the next. Pay is the summer match's own: I
changed no prices.

**Halloween Night** is the fall's finale, on Bunratty at night. It brings the parked Halloween look (B.18) back for
itself: lanterns and webs on the houses and the kids in costume. `Holiday.chapter` holds it for that match only, and
the bedroom clears it. Halloween stays parked everywhere else.

**The ladder** (90-progression). `ZONE_LADDER` is still only the summer's eight places, so the calendar, the crew's
story and the summer's tests read it as before. The whole ladder is `ladderZones()`, which is the summer's places and
then `SEASON_LADDER`. `zoneLadderIndex`, `isZoneUnlocked` and the lock reason all read it, so a locked fall place says
which match opens it. The bed's "Next" and the map's opening place follow the whole ladder into the fall. The summer's
score against Seth's crew stays the summer's (`isRivalMatch` leaves out season matches). That score's line says "this
summer", and a fall rivalry would be a design question for Michael.

**The map** (97-map-screen, index.html). Once the Last Saturday is won, tabs over the map read Summer and Fall, and
Winter joins them once the fall is won. A season's tab shows that season's places: a pin with a place that season is in
colour (grey while locked), and the other pins fade. A faded pin says "Nothing on here in Fall 2003". The place's
heading says when it is, for example "Fall 2003 · September". A new game sees no tabs.
