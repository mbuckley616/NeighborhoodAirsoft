The critic found it on 27 Nov (Found in play): two winter cards on Bellfield named kids who weren't in the match.

Lamplight in the Snow (`winter_bf_lamplight`) said "Haden, Connor and Evan have your back". The season matches are copies
of the summer's Bellfield Bulb and Lamplight on Bellfield, and since D.57 (v1.408) your crew takes the old allies' places
in those, so Andrew and Evan never play. The summer cards were reworded then to "your crew"; the season copies (D.96,
v1.486) were written later from the old allies. This is not the Seth question of v1.495: Seth's Winnmark has its own
rule, and the crew filling the allies' places is Michael's D.57 answer, so the cards follow the field, not the other way.

  has lights up."

against you on Bellfield, while the summer's has the twins against you and none of Seth's crew. Whether that is a fault or
the season's "everybody's out" is a question for whoever takes it.
