## v1.403 — The Thompson, a drum-fed SMG (gun batch 5, D.63); 33 guns
Michael chose what the play lane builds next on 31 Oct: **C**, per-kid excuses (v1.397), then gun batch 5 toward 40,
"say a second pump shotgun, a gas pistol, an SMG, a DMR". The SPAS-12 (v1.398) and the Gas Pistol (v1.399) came first;
this is the SMG.

The SMGs were the MP5, the UMP, the P90, the Vector and the MAC-10: light, quick on your feet, and the faster ones
burn a mag in seconds. **The Thompson**, $240, after the Vector under AUTOMATICS, is the other way round, the old
gangster-movie gun in blued steel and walnut:
- **The fastest, straightest BB of the SMGs:** 54 m/s (the MP5 50), straight for 18 m (14), a tighter cloud (0.30,
  0.34; aimed 0.36 of it, 0.4).
- **The slowest to fire:** 560 a minute, 9.3 a second (the UMP 600, the MP5 800, the Vector 1000).
- **Heavy:** 10 lb (the Vector 5.5), so a slower walk (0.96; the other SMGs 1.05 to 1.15).
- **A round drum:** 100 BBs, a 200 drum for $30, a spare 100 drum for $16.

The first-person model: a square blued receiver with the cocking knob on top, a finned barrel with its compensator
and orange tip, the round drum under the receiver with a winding hub on each face, a wooden pistol grip, a wooden
front grip under the barrel (the left hand holds it, so the under-barrel slot is taken, as on the Vector and the AUG),
and a wooden stock. Aimed, you look over the rear notch at a blade on the barrel. It takes a sight and one rail. A kid
holding one has a blocky Thompson of its own (receiver, barrel, drum, wooden grips and stock), but no kid carries one
yet. The game has 33 guns.
