## v1.413 — The M60, a belt-fed machine gun (gun batch 6, D.63); 38 guns
Michael chose what the play lane builds next on 1 Nov: **A**, gun batch 6, to 40: six more in the neighborhood-kid
tone, one a version, "say a revolver, a bullpup, a lever-action, a light machine gun, a burst SMG, a pump sniper".
The Magnum (v1.410), the KSG (v1.411) and the Lever Gun (v1.412) came first; this is the light machine gun.

The shop had two already, the M249 (a battery box-fed LMG, 12.5 BBs a second) and the RPK (the AK with a drum). The
one every kid knew from the old war movies on cable is the big belt-fed one with the carry handle, so **the M60**,
$340, last under AUTOMATICS after the M249, is the heavy end of the shop:
- **A slower beat for a better BB:** 10 BBs a second (the M249 12.5, the RPK 9.2), each at 58 m/s and straight for
  22 m (the M249 52 and 18).
- **Wide from the hip, tight aimed:** 0.42 from the hip (the M249 0.40), 0.30 of it aimed (0.35): stop and aim it.
- **What it costs:** the heaviest gun in the shop (17 lb; the M249 15) and the slowest walk of any (0.72; the M249
  and the sniper 0.75), a can of 500 (the M249's box 800), a big can for 1,000 at $45, spare cans 500 for $25, and a
  new can takes a long rack (1.1 s).
- Its BBs press a kid like any heavy automatic's (weight 3, v1.387's fire pressure).

The first-person model is the M249's receiver, feed cover, grip and sights (so its hands and aimed picture are the
M249's) in a greyer parkerized steel: the barrel runs on 12 cm past the M249's muzzle to a slotted flash hider and
the orange tip, a carry handle lies folded on the barrel's left, the box is a green ammo can with a latch and a longer
belt of brass up into the feed, and a deeper toe under the stock. A first try made the stock longer too, and aimed it
filled the bottom of the screen; the stock is the M249's length now and the aimed view matches the M249's. It takes a
sight, two rails and the under-barrel slot. A kid holding one has a blocky M60 of its own (the long barrel, the carry
handle, the green can, the long stock), but no kid carries one. The game has 38 guns.
