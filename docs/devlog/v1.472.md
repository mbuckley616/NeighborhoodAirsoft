## v1.472 — Kids carry homemade parts
Michael chose **A** on "What the play lane builds next, after the late-look callouts" (decisions, 11 Nov): a few kids'
guns show a homemade part and get its effect, so the parts you buy are also what you meet.

**Who carries what.** Four kids, each with one part on his own gun (`KID_GUN_PARTS`, 14-gear):
- **Seth: the Taped Twin Mag.** He flips the pair instead of reloading: his pause between trigger pulls is 0.85 as long.
- **Devon: the Sock-Padded Stock.** Steady on his shoulder: every shot's spread 0.8, and an automatic's string wanders
  0.7 as far (the stock's climb).
- **Sean: the Tennis-Ball Grip.** His shots on the move stay a quarter tighter (0.75 while he walks), a string wanders
  0.85 as far; standing, nothing changes.
- **Mason: the Glow-Stick Tracer.** His BBs leave the green streak, bright at night, barely there by day: you see where he
  is shooting from. It is only to see: no kid takes his streak as a sighting of you (the v1.468 rule reads your BBs only).

A part goes on only where yours would: a gun with that slot. Seth on a pistol (14 of his 29 matches) or anyone on a
MAC-10 carries nothing; Seth's UMP, AR, AK and shotgun, Devon's sniper and MP5, Sean's AK and Mason's MP5, AK and sniper
do. A crew kid on a gun you lent him (v1.337) has no part; when your BBs run out and he goes back to his own gun, his part
goes back on with it.

**What you see.** Each part in the kids' blocky style, placed from the gun's own pieces (`buildKidGunPart`, 50-kids): a
second dark mag beside the first with two bands of duct tape (on a gun with no hanging mag, a shotgun, under the
receiver ahead of the grip); a white sock with a red cuff over the butt of the stock; the yellow-green ball under the
front of the body, taped either side; two green glow sticks along the muzzle with a band of tape.

Under it: `fitKidGunPart` (50-kids) puts the part on as the match builds its kids (64-scenario-flow) and again in
`crewKidOwnGun` (92-summer-crew); `kidPartMult` (14-gear) is read by `spawnEnemyBB`'s spread (76-bbs), the burst's
wander and the pause after a pull (80-ai); `addTracerStreak` takes a kid's shot without the sighting.
