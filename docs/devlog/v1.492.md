## v1.492 — Three season cards say what the weather really does
The critic (24 Nov, Found in play) found three fall and winter match cards promising things the game doesn't have.
Storm the Snowy Court told you to "stay off the ice on the driveways", but there is no ice. The Treehouse in the Snow
said "its ladder is icy", but the ladder is the same as in summer. Leaves Underfoot said "every step crunches. They'll
hear you coming", but the kids don't hear the player, and footsteps sound the same in every season. The backlog item
gave two ways to fix this: change the lines to match what v1.487 built, or build ice and hearing. Building them is the
critic's proposal (proposals.md, 24 Nov), and that is Michael's call. So this version changes the lines.

**What changed** (`SEASON_CHAPTERS`, 63-seasons; card text only, play unchanged):
- **Leaves Underfoot** (a fall day at the Hollow, so the wind is on): "The woods have gone orange and the wind's up:
  watch which way the leaves blow, because a long shot drifts that way too." The leaves really do blow the wind's way
  (v1.487), so this tells you how to read it.
- **Storm the Snowy Court**: "The snow's deep, so it's a slow push up the court." Everyone moves at 85% in winter.
- **The Treehouse in the Snow**: "Hold it till the timer's done, and mind your tracks: they'll follow your prints in the
  snow." A kid who comes within 4 m of your trail follows it.

Cold Hands' "Gloves make the trigger hard to find" is left alone. The critic read it as flavour, and the kids do wear
gloves in winter (v1.489). If Michael promotes the proposal, the old lines can come back with what it builds.
