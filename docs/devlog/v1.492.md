## v1.492 — No more hitches on the school field at night (the route field fills once a cell)
The critic found it on 24 Nov (Found in play): Hollins Ridge at night (`school_night_4v4`, and Under the Stadium Lights
on the same field) hitched. Its worst steps took 200-520 ms where other maps take 4-8 ms. Almost all of that time was
`kidFieldTo` (80-ai), the distance field a kid walks to find his way round something solid. Since v1.429, on a map that
asks for it (`fieldAdvance`), every advancing kid checks his straight line once a second, and when it is blocked he
builds that field. The field was filled from a first-in-first-out queue that pushed a cell again every time its
distance got shorter. On the school field's open ground that came to about 104-120 pushes a free cell, 100-160 ms a
call, once a second a kid.

**What changed** (80-ai). One fill, `kidFieldFill`, now serves `kidFieldTo` and the ladder's field (`ladderField`). It
takes the nearest cell first off a binary heap (Dijkstra) and settles each cell once. The rules are the old ones:
eight neighbours, a diagonal costs 1.414, no cutting a corner past a blocked cell. So the distances are the same, cell
for cell. Nothing about where a kid walks changes, only how long it takes to work out. The heap's two arrays are kept
between calls and grow when needed.

  (5 goals), and under 40 ms a call (5.8 here). Green, with its three pocket escapes as before.
- `smoke`, `ladder` (2 suites), `high-school`, `ctf`, `ctf-hold` and `squad-orders` green. Parsecheck is clean.
