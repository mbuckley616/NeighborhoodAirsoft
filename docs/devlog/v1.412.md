## v1.412 — The Lever Gun, a spring cowboy carbine (gun batch 6, D.63); 37 guns
Michael chose what the play lane builds next on 1 Nov: **A**, gun batch 6, to 40: six more in the neighborhood-kid
tone, one a version, "say a revolver, a bullpup, a lever-action, a light machine gun, a burst SMG, a pump sniper".
The Magnum (v1.410) and the KSG (v1.411) came first; this is the lever-action.

The spring long guns each made you do one thing between shots: the AR and the FAMAS pull a charging handle (0.55 s),
the snipers cycle a bolt (1.1 s and more). **The Lever Gun**, $120, under RIFLES straight after the FAMAS, is the
cowboy carbine from the old westerns on TV, in walnut with a shiny brass receiver, and a new way to cock a gun:
- **Work the lever every shot:** 0.5 s, the quickest of the spring long guns (the AR 0.55, the bolts 1.1 to 1.3).
  The cock bar reads WORK LEVER and the hint "Click & hold LMB to work the lever"; the shop line says
  "Lever-action (work the lever)".
- **Between the AR and the sniper:** a 55 m/s BB (the AR 45, the sniper 75), straight for 14 m (10, 25), tighter than
  the AR (0.22, 0.28; the sniper 0.12).
- **Light for a rifle:** 7 lb (the AR 8), a quicker walk (0.9; the AR 0.85, the sniper 0.75).
- **What it costs:** twelve in the tube under the barrel (the AR 25), a long tube for sixteen at $14, spare BBs 12
  for $6.

The first-person model is the spring sniper's stock, barrel and sights, as the M14's is, so its hands and aimed picture
are the sniper's: the stock and forend in walnut, the receiver in brass, the folded bipod off, a blued tube under the
barrel past the forend held by two barrel bands, a hammer at the receiver's back, and in place of the bolt the lever: a
bar under the trigger and a loop behind it for the fingers, hinged at the guard's front. Working it swings the lever
down on its hinge, 50° at the full pull, and it snaps home as the spring catches (`cockSwing`, a new line in
`updateHeldMesh` beside the six-shooter's turning cylinder). It takes a sight, two rails and the under-barrel slot. A
kid holding one has a blocky Lever Gun of its own (a barrel over its tube, the brass receiver, walnut, the lever
loop), but no kid carries one. The game has 37 guns.
