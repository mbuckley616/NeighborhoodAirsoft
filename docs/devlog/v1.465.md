## v1.465 — An ally with nobody to shoot at goes looking at 8 s
The critic (9 Nov, Found in play): in The Portables your crew's Owen starts 1.2 m from you and hides and peeks there for
20-33 s while the rest of your side pushes up the lot; in two of three rounds you were out before he moved. It was D.19
working as chosen (C, 9 Oct): your allies start beside you, a cautious kid (Owen's aggression is 0.2) never marches with
nobody to fight, and an idle ally goes looking only after 25-30 s. Michael chose **B** (decisions, 9 Nov): in every team
match, an ally who has had nobody in sight for his first 8 s goes looking.

**What changed.** `updateKidBoredom` (78-bb-flight) takes one early look a round for an ally of yours: if by 8 s of the
round (`ALLY_LOOK_AFTER_S`) he has not once had a foe in sight, he is sent looking then, as a bored kid is (out of
`hiding`/`peeking` into `advancing`, marching until he next fires with a line). The 25-30 s clock stays for an ally who
goes quiet later in the round. Snipers, defenders, VIPs and taggers still hold, as before; a free-for-all has no allies.

**"In sight" means inside his gun's reach.** Owen does have a line to the other side from 3 s, but at 45-50 m with a pistol,
whose far range is 30 m: nobody he can shoot at, which is what the critic saw ("no target"). Counting that line would have
left him exactly where he was. So the early look is called off only by a foe in sight inside the kid's own far range
(`_inReachNow`, set beside v1.134's `_unseenTime` in `updateEnemies`, 80-ai). A kid with a foe in range never takes it.
