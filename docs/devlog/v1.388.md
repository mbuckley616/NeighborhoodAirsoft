## v1.388 — The bedroom's labels stop showing through walls
Found in play (critic, 29 Oct, on v1.387): the bedroom's floating labels showed through walls. `updateBedroomLabels` (`js/72-player.js`) faded a label by its distance from you only (full to 6 m, gone at 11 m), with no wall test, and since v1.376 there are two rooms. At a new save's spawn in the hall, MAP and LOADOUT floated on the hall's closet doors, in front of the things they name; from the top of the landing's stairs, WORKBENCH (and at some angles MIRROR) floated over the landing's wall, 7 m from things in the next room.

**Now** a ray from your eye to each label's anchor hides the label when a visible, opaque mesh stands on the line more than 0.5 m short of the anchor, so the thing a label names, right under it, never hides it, and glass (a transparent material under 0.6 opacity) doesn't either. The room's walls are one-sided planes, which a ray from the other room meets from behind and passes through, so the line is tested from both ends. Each label is tested once every 6 frames; a frame that tests all ten costs about 2.4 ms here, so about 0.4 ms a frame on average. A label you are standing at (the one the E prompt names) still always shows. Through an open door a label still shows when its anchor is in view: from the landing, MIRROR shows over the bathroom door down the hall when you look through the hall's doorway at it.
