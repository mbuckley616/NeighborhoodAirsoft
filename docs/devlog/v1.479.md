## v1.479 — The AR, UMP and MP5 allies close in like the AK
The critic (17 Nov, Found in play, on v1.478) found that the other long guns did what Mitchell's AK did before v1.476 and
v1.478. On Willow Bend's 18th (`club_eighteenth_3v3`) your crew's Owen, with an AR, fired 85-100 % of his shots past what
his BBs carry (about 28 m; median 43-51 m out), landed 0-1 and moved 0-23 m in a minute. At Bellfield (`bellfield_3v3`)
his UMP (carry about 30 m) fired 60-71 % past and landed 3-5.

**What was wrong.** The AK fix bounded three things by `kidGunReach` (what a gun carries from a kid's 1.05 m muzzle) instead
of the engage table's FAR, but only for `ak47`: the early look's "is anyone in reach" (`_inReachNow`), the bored kid's
fire-hold and commit while he is going looking (`reachFar`, `commitFar`), and the rule that a shot ends his look
(`spawnEnemyBB`). The AR's table says FAR 60 m and the UMP's and MP5's 50 m, about twice what their BBs carry. So Owen read
the other side 40-50 m off as in reach and never took the 8 s look. When he did go looking he stopped on his first sight
of them, and his first string into the grass ended the look.

**What changed.** `kidCarryBounded(weapon)` (80-ai) names the guns whose table reaches well past their carry: the AK, AR,
UMP and MP5. The three AK rules now read it, so those four guns follow them alike. A bored kid with one of them closes
to his carry before he settles in, and a shot past that carry doesn't end his look or restart his idle clock. He still
fires on the move as before. The pistol, shotgun and MAC-10 keep their FAR, which sits near their carry (Owen's early look
at The Portables was tuned on the pistol's). The sniper never goes looking. As with v1.478, a kid of either side who is
not going looking fires and commits from the same ranges as before, so first-shot distances on the maps are unchanged.
