## v1.438 — The Auto Pistol, a battery pistol set to AUTO (gun batch 7, D.85); 44 guns
The fourth gun of batch 7 (Michael's **A** of 4 Nov: six more to 46, the kinds the builder's pick from what the shop is
thin on). Pistols are still the thinnest kind (seven), and every one of them fired a BB a pull; the only machine pistol
was the MAC-10, filed under AUTOMATICS. So this is a sidearm that sprays.

**The Auto Pistol**, $95, under PISTOLS after the Electric Pistol: a black battery pistol with its switch set to AUTO.
- **Twelve a second, held.** Full auto at 720 a minute (the MAC-10 1100), so the 24-round flush mag is gone in two
  seconds; a slide racked on each new mag, as the Spring Pistol's.
- **The trade.** The pistol's soft BB (33 m/s; the MAC-10 38), the widest cone of the semi pistols but the Derringer's
  (0.48; the Spring Pistol 0.4), wandering past 5 m, and a pistol's weight (2.5 lb) and walk (1.13).
- **Mags.** A long stick mag of 50 ($12) and a spare 24 ($5), both on its page. It takes a sight and a rail, as every
  pistol; no under-barrel, muzzle or stock slot.

The first-person model is new, on the Spring Pistol's frame, slide and sights: the long black mag hanging 7 cm below
the grip with a steel baseplate, the selector on the left with an orange AUTO dot, and a slotted compensator past the
slide with the barrel and the orange tip through it. A kid holding one (a loan to your crew) has the pistol with the long
mag under its grip.
