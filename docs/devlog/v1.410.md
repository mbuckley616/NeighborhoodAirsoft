## v1.410 — The Magnum, a CO2 double-action revolver (gun batch 6, D.63); 35 guns
Michael chose what the play lane builds next on 1 Nov: **A**, gun batch 6, to 40: six more in the neighborhood-kid
tone, one a version, "say a revolver, a bullpup, a lever-action, a light machine gun, a burst SMG, a pump sniper".
This is the revolver.

The game already had one: the Six-Shooter, a $70 cap-gun single-action whose hammer you thumb back every shot. **The
Magnum**, $150, last under PISTOLS after the Gas Pistol, is the grown-up one, the heavy blued CO2 revolver an older
kid got for his birthday:
- **Double-action:** every pull turns the cylinder and fires, nothing to thumb back (`electric`: it stays cocked), but
  the pull is long, so **2.5 a second** at most, the slowest of the pull-a-shot pistols (the Gas Pistol 4.5, the
  Electric Pistol 5).
- **A faster, straighter BB than the other revolver:** 44 m/s (the Six-Shooter 32, the Gas Pistol 40), straight for
  9 m (5, 6), a tighter cloud (0.30, 0.36; aimed 0.3 of it). The Bolt Pistol's 52 m/s is still the pistols' best.
- **CO2 holds its push:** no fade, so the sixth shell flies as fast as the first (the Gas Pistol's last BB is at 80%).
- **What it costs:** six shells, always (the Six-Shooter's cylinder; spare shells 6 for $5), a slower walk (1.06, the
  slowest pistol; the Gas Pistol 1.1), and 3.5 lb, the heaviest pistol. Closing the cylinder on fresh shells is the
  hammer's 0.35 s.

The first-person model is the Six-Shooter's frame, cylinder, barrel, sights and bird's-head grip, in blued steel with a
darker cylinder and a black rubber grip, a full-length underlug under the barrel round the ejector rod, a vent rib on
top that the front blade stands on (its tip where the Six-Shooter's is, so the aimed picture is the same), and a brass
CO2 cap under the butt. Each shot's kick takes the hammer back and it falls home again; the cylinder stands one
chamber on for every shell fired from it (`doubleAction`, read in `updateHeldMesh`). The shop's line for it reads
"Double-action CO2 (a BB a pull, up to 2.5/s)". A kid holding one has a blocky blued revolver of its own, held as a
pistol, but no kid carries one. It takes a sight and one rail, as the Six-Shooter does. The game has 35 guns.
