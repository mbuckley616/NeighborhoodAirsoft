# Design brief

Michael's pillars and scope, copied from the top of `devlog.md` on 28 Sep 2026. Every proposal argues from these.

## Pillars
- **Suburban kid fantasy.** Players are kids in their neighborhood playing airsoft. Not military, not tactical.
  The bedroom hub framing reinforces this.
- **Unreliable BBs.** Long-range shots wobble from simulated hop-up spin. The core gimmick: reliable inside ~10 m,
  increasingly random beyond.
- **Spring-loaded mechanical feel.** Starter guns are pump/bolt action and need a manual cock each shot.
  Electric/auto/sniper come later.
- **Zone + scenario architecture.** Maps are reused across many scenarios (1v1, 2v1, modified cover, ...).
- **Bedroom hub.** Between scenarios only (not pause-accessible): save, gear, world map.

## Scope
- v1: the vertical slice, grown through v1.85 into bedroom → world map → Winnmark Court and Bunratty scenarios.
- v2+: more zones, scenarios, guns, gear; character customisation with a mirror moment.
- v3: PvP as a parallel system on shared map geometry, peer-hosted via WebRTC.

## Tone
Summer 2003. Kids, cul-de-sacs, cardboard cover, mailboxes, trash cans. Nothing grim.
